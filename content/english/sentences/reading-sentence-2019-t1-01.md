## 原句

> He quotes a giant of classical economics, Alfred Marshall, in describing this financial impatience as acting like “children who pick the plums out of their pudding to eat them at once” rather than putting them aside to be eaten last.

出处：2019年考研英语阅读 Part A，Text 1。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2019英一.pdf#page=2)。定位：所附试卷 PDF 第 2 页；本句原文已核对。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> He quotes a giant of classical economics, Alfred Marshall, / in describing this financial impatience as acting like / “children who pick the plums out of their pudding / to eat them at once” / rather than putting them aside to be eaten last.

“/”划分意群；方括号标出从句或嵌套成分。断句用于辨认结构，不表示每处都必须停顿。

### 主干读法

**主干读法**：He quotes Alfred Marshall in describing financial impatience as acting like children.

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法作用与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| He quotes a giant of classical economics, Alfred Marshall, | 主句主语 He 指 Haldane；quotes 为谓语，a giant 为宾语中心，of classical economics 修饰 giant；Alfred Marshall 为同位姓名。 |
| in describing this financial impatience as acting like | in describing 表引用发生于描述时；describe A as B 中 A 为 impatience，B 为 acting like… 动名词结构；this 指追求迅速回报的急躁。 |
| “children who pick the plums out of their pudding | 引号内 children 是 like 的宾语，who 定语从句修饰 children 并作主语；pick 为谓语，plums 为宾语，out of their pudding 表取出来源。 |
| to eat them at once” | 目的不定式修饰 pick；them 指 plums，at once 为时间状语。 |
| rather than putting them aside to be eaten last. | rather than 将立即吃与留待最后吃相对照；putting…aside 是动名词表达另一种做法，them 仍为 plums；to be eaten 为被动不定式，last 为顺序状语。 |

### 理解要点

先抓 describe impatience as acting like children；引语内 who 是说明孩子怎样做。them 始终指李子，不能指投资者；对比用于说明短期获利与延迟满足。

## 句子直译

他引用古典经济学巨擘阿尔弗雷德·马歇尔的话，把这种金融上的急躁描述为像孩子一样：把李子从布丁里挑出来立刻吃掉，而不是放到一旁留到最后再吃。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。建议先遮住参考答案完成断句与翻译。

### 迁移练习 1

> She describes the habit as acting like a reader who checks the ending first rather than following the story patiently.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：She / describes the habit as acting like a reader [who checks the ending first / rather than following the story patiently].

**训练句直译**：她把这种习惯描述为像一个先看结尾、而不耐心顺着故事读下去的读者。

**训练句拆解**：describe A as acting like B；who 限定 reader，rather than 对照两种阅读做法。

### 迁移练习 2

> The teacher quoted an old saying in describing students who use every hint at once rather than saving some to be used later.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The teacher / quoted an old saying / in describing students [who use every hint at once / rather than saving some to be used later].

**训练句直译**：老师在描述那些立即用掉每条提示、而不留一些以后再用的学生时，引用了一句旧谚语。

**训练句拆解**：in describing 表引用所处活动；who 修饰 students；to be used 被动不定式，逻辑受事 some hints。

### 考场价值

先抓 describe impatience as acting like children；引语内 who 是说明孩子怎样做。them 始终指李子，不能指投资者；对比用于说明短期获利与延迟满足。
