## 原句

> These fires were carefully planned and intentionally ignited during periods when weather kept the blazes easy to control, and helped eliminate some of the fuel that had built up around important park facilities.

出处：2026年考研英语阅读 Part A，Text 4，P5。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2026英一.pdf#page=5)。P 指文章自然段；PDF 第 5 页为本篇所在页。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> These fires / were carefully planned and intentionally ignited / during periods [when weather kept the blazes easy to control], / and helped eliminate some of the fuel / [that had built up around important park facilities].

`/` 表示意群边界，`//` 表示同层并列分句边界，方括号表示嵌入结构；不改变原文用词。

### 主干读法

**These fires were planned and ignited, and helped eliminate fuel.** 前半段被动，后半段 helped 为主动，两者共用 fires。

### 逐项分析

| 成分 | 结构与作用 |
| --- | --- |
| These fires | 主句主语，指前文 prescribed burns，即人为规划点燃的火。 |
| were carefully planned and intentionally ignited | 两个被动谓语共用 were；carefully 修饰 planned，intentionally 修饰 ignited，说明规划与点燃均人为进行。 |
| during periods when … | during 引时间范围，periods 为介词宾语；when 引导定语从句修饰 periods，在从句中作时间状语。 |
| weather kept the blazes easy to control | 关系从句主谓宾补；weather 主语，kept 谓语，blazes 宾语，easy 为宾补；to control 补足 easy，blazes 是 control 的逻辑宾语，主动形式表达被控制的关系。 |
| and helped eliminate … | 与前面的 were planned/ignited 并列的主动谓语，主语仍 fires；help 后不定式 eliminate 省 to。 |
| some of the fuel | eliminate 的宾语，some 限定只移除部分燃料；fuel 指林中的可燃物。 |
| that had built up | 限制性定语从句修饰 fuel，that 作主语；had built up 过去完成时，表示燃烧前已积聚，build up 在此为不及物用法。 |
| around important park facilities | 地点状语修饰 built up；important/park 修饰 facilities，指公园重要设施周边。 |

### 理解要点

不要把 helped 误解为沿用 were 的被动。“火被规划、被点燃”与“火帮助消除燃料”是被动和主动的转换。easy to control 中 control 虽主动，所控制的对象是 blazes。

## 句子直译

这些火经过精心规划，并在天气使火势易于控制的时期被有意点燃；它们还帮助清除了公园重要设施周围此前积聚的部分可燃物。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。

### 迁移练习 1

> The exercises were carefully designed and deliberately introduced during weeks when teachers kept the workload easy to manage, and helped remove some of the confusion that had developed earlier.

**任务**：划分意群，指出主干及嵌入成分的作用，再作直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The exercises / were carefully designed and deliberately introduced / during weeks [when teachers kept the workload easy to manage], / and helped remove some of the confusion / [that had developed earlier].

**训练句直译**：这些练习经过精心设计，并被有意安排在教师使工作量易于管理的几周内；它们帮助消除了此前形成的部分困惑。

**训练句拆解**：were designed/introduced 被动；helped 主动，主语 exercises；kept workload easy 宾补；confusion 是 had developed 主语。

### 迁移练习 2

> The roads were inspected and repaired during months when conditions made the work safe to perform, and reduced some of the delays that had built up over winter.

**任务**：划分意群，指出主干及嵌入成分的作用，再作直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The roads / were inspected and repaired / during months [when conditions made the work safe to perform], / and reduced some of the delays / [that had built up over winter].

**训练句直译**：这些道路在条件使施工安全的月份接受检查和维修，并减少了冬季积累的部分延误。

**训练句拆解**：roads 先承受 inspected/repaired，后为 reduced 主语；work 是 perform 逻辑宾语；that 指 delays，had built up 先于修复。

### 考场价值

不要把 helped 误解为沿用 were 的被动。“火被规划、被点燃”与“火帮助消除燃料”是被动和主动的转换。easy to control 中 control 虽主动，所控制的对象是 blazes。
