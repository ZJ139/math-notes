## 原句

> Of all the changes that have taken place in English-language newspapers during the past quarter-century, perhaps the most far-reaching has been the inexorable decline in the scope and seriousness of their arts coverage.

出处：2010年考研英语一阅读 Part A，Text 1。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2010英一.pdf#page=2)（试卷 PDF 第 2 页）。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

“/”划分意群；先辨认限定谓语，再把从句与修饰语接回所属成分。断句完整保留原句词序。

> Of all the changes / that have taken place in English-language newspapers / during the past quarter-century, / perhaps the most far-reaching / has been the inexorable decline / in the scope and seriousness of their arts coverage.

### 主干读法

Perhaps the most far-reaching change has been the decline. 前置 of 短语给比较范围，that 从句不属于主句主干。

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 作用及内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| Of all the changes | Of 短语限定比较范围“在所有变化中”；all 为前置限定语，the changes 是介词宾语，其后 that 从句修饰 changes。 |
| that have taken place in English-language newspapers | 限制性定语从句；that 指 changes 并作主语，have taken place 是现在完成时谓语，take place 无被动用法；in English-language newspapers 限定变化发生的领域。 |
| during the past quarter-century, | during 引出时间范围，修饰 have taken place；past 修饰 quarter-century，即过去四分之一个世纪。 |
| perhaps the most far-reaching | perhaps 修饰整个判断，保留“也许”；the most far-reaching 为主语，省略已知中心词 change，指影响最深远的一项，故下文用单数 has。 |
| has been the inexorable decline | has been 是现在完成时系动词；the inexorable decline 是表语，inexorable 修饰 decline，表示难以阻止的衰退。 |
| in the scope and seriousness of their arts coverage. | in 短语补充 decline 的方面；scope 与 seriousness 并列，都受 of their arts coverage 限定；their 指报纸，arts 修饰 coverage，表示艺术报道。 |

### 理解要点

不能把 all the changes 当作主句主语；真正主语是单数的 the most far-reaching（change）。decline 不仅指篇幅下降，还同时涉及报道的严肃性。

## 句子直译

在过去四分之一个世纪英语报纸发生的所有变化中，影响最深远的也许是其艺术报道在范围和严肃性上不可阻挡的衰退。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。先独立作答，再核对参考答案。


### 迁移练习 1

> Of all the reforms that have been introduced in the past decade, perhaps the most effective has been the change in teacher training.

请独立断句，指出与原句对应的结构，再直译。

**参考答案**

断句：Of all the reforms / that have been introduced / in the past decade, / perhaps the most effective / has been the change / in teacher training.

**训练句直译**：在过去十年推出的所有改革中，最有效的也许是教师培训方面的变化。

**训练句拆解**：of 限定范围；that 指 reforms，是被动从句主语；the most effective 省略 reform，决定 has 为单数。


### 迁移练习 2

> Of the problems that arose during the project, the most serious was the lack of reliable data.

请独立断句，指出与原句对应的结构，再直译。

**参考答案**

断句：Of the problems / that arose during the project, / the most serious / was the lack / of reliable data.

**训练句直译**：在项目期间出现的问题中，最严重的是缺少可靠数据。

**训练句拆解**：that 引导修饰 problems 的从句；主语 the most serious 省略 problem；of reliable data 补充 lack 的内容。

### 考场价值

不能把 all the changes 当作主句主语；真正主语是单数的 the most far-reaching（change）。decline 不仅指篇幅下降，还同时涉及报道的严肃性。
