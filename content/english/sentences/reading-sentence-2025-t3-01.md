## 原句

> Ever since taking on Netflix Inc. at its own game, old Hollywood has struggled to turn a profit in streaming, with the likes of Disney+, Peacock and Paramount+ losing billions of dollars each year, sparking concerns on Wall Street that the services will never be as profitable as cable once was.

出处：2025年考研英语阅读 Part A，Text 3，P1。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2025英一.pdf#page=4)。P 指文章自然段；PDF 第 4 页为本篇所在页。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> Ever since taking on Netflix Inc. at its own game, / old Hollywood has struggled / to turn a profit in streaming, / with the likes of Disney+, Peacock and Paramount+ losing billions of dollars each year, / sparking concerns on Wall Street / [that the services will never be as profitable as cable once was].

`/` 表示意群边界，`//` 表示同层并列分句边界，方括号表示嵌入结构；不改变原文用词。

### 主干读法

**Old Hollywood has struggled to turn a profit, sparking concerns that streaming will never be as profitable as cable.** with 部分具体交代平台亏损情况。

### 逐项分析

| 成分 | 结构与作用 |
| --- | --- |
| Ever since taking on Netflix Inc. at its own game | since 引时间起点，ever 加强“自从以来”；taking on 为动名词，逻辑施事 old Hollywood；Netflix 为宾语，at its own game 表在对方擅长的领域竞争，its 指 Netflix。 |
| old Hollywood has struggled | 主句主谓；old Hollywood 指传统影视业；has struggled 现在完成时，与 since 相呼应。 |
| to turn a profit in streaming | struggle to do 的不定式补足语；turn a profit 为盈利，in streaming 表业务领域。 |
| with the likes of Disney+, Peacock and Paramount+ | with 复合结构的名词部分；the likes of 意为“像……这样的一类”，列出平台实例。 |
| losing billions of dollars each year | with 结构的现在分词补足语，逻辑主语为上述平台；billions of dollars 为宾语，each year 为频率。 |
| sparking concerns on Wall Street | 现在分词结果状语，概括前面的持续亏损引发担忧；concerns 为宾语，on Wall Street 表担忧出现的金融界范围。 |
| that the services will never be as profitable … | that 引导 concerns 的同位语从句，说明担忧内容；services 指流媒体服务；will never be 为带完全否定的预测，非作者已证实事实。 |
| as profitable as cable once was | 同级比较；第二个 as 引比较从句，cable 指有线电视业务，once 表曾经；was 后省略 profitable。 |

### 理解要点

同位语从句里的 never 是金融界担忧的内容，不是无条件事实。with 交代伴随情形，sparking 再指出后果，不能把两处分词都当作独立谓语。

## 句子直译

自从在 Netflix 擅长的领域与其竞争以来，传统好莱坞一直难以在流媒体业务中盈利，Disney+、Peacock 和 Paramount+ 等平台每年亏损数十亿美元，这引发了华尔街的担忧：这些服务永远不会像有线电视过去那样赚钱。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。

### 迁移练习 1

> Ever since entering online retail, the company has struggled to earn a profit, with several branches losing money each month, raising doubts that the new business will ever be as successful as its stores once were.

**任务**：划分意群，指出主干及嵌入成分的作用，再作直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：Ever since entering online retail, / the company has struggled to earn a profit, / with several branches losing money each month, / raising doubts [that the new business will ever be as successful as its stores once were].

**训练句直译**：自从进入网络零售以来，公司一直难以盈利，几家分支每月亏损，这引发了疑虑：新业务是否有朝一日能像实体店过去那样成功。

**训练句拆解**：since 后 entering 逻辑主语 company；with branches losing 说明亏损；raising 为结果；that 解释 doubts，as successful as 比较省略 successful。

### 迁移练习 2

> Since launching the service, the charity has faced growing costs, with more families seeking help each week, causing concern that its funds may not last as long as expected.

**任务**：划分意群，指出主干及嵌入成分的作用，再作直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：Since launching the service, / the charity has faced growing costs, / with more families seeking help each week, / causing concern [that its funds may not last as long as expected].

**训练句直译**：自从推出这项服务以来，慈善机构面临不断上涨的成本，每周寻求帮助的家庭更多，这引发了资金可能无法维持到预期时间的担忧。

**训练句拆解**：launching 逻辑主语 charity；with families seeking 为复合结构；causing 引结果；that 同位解释 concern；as expected 是省略比较结构。
