## 原句

> Asked whether any particular papers had impelled the change, McNutt said: “The creation of the ‘statistics board’ was motivated by concerns broadly with the application of statistics and data analysis in scientific research and is part of Science’s overall drive to increase reproducibility in the research we publish.”

出处：2015年考研英语一阅读 Part A，Text 3，第 3 段。[查看试卷原文](/files/english/exams/2015英一.pdf#page=4)。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

“/”分隔意义组，“//”分隔同层并列分句；方括号标出嵌入结构，断句不改变词语归属。

> Asked [whether any particular papers had impelled the change], / McNutt said: / “The creation of the ‘statistics board’ / was motivated by concerns broadly with the application of statistics and data analysis in scientific research / and is part of Science’s overall drive / to increase reproducibility in the research [we publish].”

### 主干读法

Asked ..., McNutt said: “The creation was motivated by concerns and is part of a drive.” 引语中的两个谓语共享 creation。

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法身份与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| Asked whether ... | 过去分词状语，McNutt 是被问者，可理解为 When she was asked；不是 McNutt 主动询问。 |
| whether any particular papers had impelled the change | asked 的询问内容；whether 表是否，papers 主语，any particular 限定具体某些论文；had impelled 过去完成时，早于 said，change 宾语。 |
| McNutt said | 主句主语与过去时谓语；冒号后直接引语是发言内容。 |
| The creation of the ‘statistics board’ | 引语主语；creation 中心，of ... board 说明设立对象。 |
| was motivated by concerns broadly with the application ... | 引语第一谓语为被动；by concerns 表起因；broadly 表广义上，with the application 说明关切所涉对象。 |
| of statistics and data analysis in scientific research | of 后两项 statistics、data analysis 共同说明应用内容；in scientific research 限定应用领域。 |
| and is part of Science’s overall drive | 引语第二谓语用现在时 is；与 was motivated 共享主语，分别述过去动因和当前地位；part 表语，of ... drive 限定其所属。 |
| to increase reproducibility in the research we publish | 不定式修饰 drive 的目标；reproducibility 宾语，in the research 限定领域；we publish 是省略宾语关系词的定语从句，修饰 research，we 指期刊编辑方。 |

### 理解要点

主句 said 和引语内部谓语要分层。was motivated 与 is part 的时态不同有意义：设立的历史动因与它现在属于什么行动。

## 句子直译

当被问到是否有某些特定论文促成这项改变时，麦克纳特说：“设立‘统计委员会’的动因，是对统计学和数据分析在科学研究中应用的广泛关切；它也是《科学》为提高我们所发表研究的可重复性而开展的整体行动的一部分。”

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。请先独立断句，找出主干及指定结构，再做直译。

### 迁移练习 1

> Asked whether recent complaints had prompted the decision, the director said: “The new office was created by public demand and is part of our effort to improve the services we provide.”

任务：标出从句或非谓语边界，说明修饰关系，并直译全句。

**参考答案**

断句：Asked [whether recent complaints had prompted the decision], / the director said: / “The new office was created by public demand / and is part of our effort / to improve the services [we provide].”

**训练句直译**：被问到近期投诉是否促成该决定时，主任说：“新办公室是应公众需求设立的，也是我们改善所提供服务的努力的一部分。”

**训练句拆解**：Asked 被动，主任是被问者；had prompted 先于 said；引语内 was created 与 is part 并列。

### 迁移练习 2

> Asked how the error had been found, the researcher explained that repeated checks were necessary to protect the accuracy of the results they publish.

任务：标出从句或非谓语边界，说明修饰关系，并直译全句。

**参考答案**

断句：Asked [how the error had been found], / the researcher explained [that repeated checks were necessary / to protect the accuracy of the results [they publish]].

**训练句直译**：被问到错误是如何发现时，研究者解释说，需要反复检查，以保障他们发表结果的准确性。

**训练句拆解**：how 从句为 asked 的内容且过去完成时被动；that 从句作 explained 宾语；they publish 修饰 results。

### 考场价值

主句 said 和引语内部谓语要分层。was motivated 与 is part 的时态不同有意义：设立的历史动因与它现在属于什么行动。
