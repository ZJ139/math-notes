## 原句

> With no video production costs, illegal streaming sites have achieved profit margins approaching 90%, according to the Motion Picture Association (MPA), a trade group representing Hollywood studios that’s working to crack down on the thousands of illegal platforms that have cropped up in recent years.

出处：2025年考研英语阅读 Part A，Text 3，P2。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2025英一.pdf#page=4)。P 指文章自然段；PDF 第 4 页为本篇所在页。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> With no video production costs, / illegal streaming sites have achieved profit margins / [approaching 90%], / according to the Motion Picture Association (MPA), / a trade group [representing Hollywood studios] / [that’s working to crack down on the thousands of illegal platforms / [that have cropped up in recent years]].

`/` 表示意群边界，`//` 表示同层并列分句边界，方括号表示嵌入结构；不改变原文用词。

### 主干读法

**Illegal streaming sites have achieved profit margins approaching 90%.** 后半句交代数据来源，并介绍该组织正在打击非法平台。

### 逐项分析

| 成分 | 结构与作用 |
| --- | --- |
| With no video production costs | with 介词短语表示背景及原因；no 否定 production costs，video 限定制作对象。 |
| illegal streaming sites have achieved | 主句主语及现在完成时谓语；illegal、streaming 修饰 sites，说明非法流媒体网站。 |
| profit margins approaching 90% | 宾语中心 margins，profit 为名词定语；approaching 现在分词作后置定语，意为接近而非已经超过90%。 |
| according to the Motion Picture Association (MPA) | 信息来源状语；括号为机构缩写，数字属于该组织的报告。 |
| a trade group representing Hollywood studios | 机构同位语；representing 分词修饰 group，逻辑主语 group，studios 为宾语，Hollywood 限定 studios。 |
| that’s working to crack down on … | that 指单数 group/MPA，不能指复数 studios；is working 表当前正在推进，to crack down 为目标性不定式。 |
| the thousands of illegal platforms | on 的宾语，thousands of 表数千；the 特指所针对的平台。 |
| that have cropped up in recent years | 关系从句修饰 platforms；that 作主语，have cropped up 为现在完成时，in recent years 是时间范围。 |

### 理解要点

用单复数判断第一个 that 的先行词：that’s working 对应 group，而非近处的 studios。第二个 that have 对应 platforms。

## 句子直译

据美国电影协会所说，由于无需承担视频制作成本，非法流媒体网站已取得接近百分之九十的利润率；该协会是代表好莱坞制片公司的行业组织，正在努力打击近年来涌现的数千个非法平台。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。

### 迁移练习 1

> With no rental costs, the service has achieved savings approaching half its budget, according to the foundation, an organization supporting schools that is working to expand the projects that have succeeded locally.

**任务**：划分意群，指出主干及嵌入成分的作用，再作直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：With no rental costs, / the service has achieved savings [approaching half its budget], / according to the foundation, / an organization [supporting schools] / [that is working to expand the projects [that have succeeded locally]].

**训练句直译**：据基金会介绍，由于没有租金成本，这项服务实现的节省已接近其预算的一半；该基金会是一个支持学校的组织，正努力推广那些已在当地成功的项目。

**训练句拆解**：approaching 修饰 savings；organization 同位解释 foundation；that is 对应 organization，that have 对应 projects。

### 迁移练习 2

> With fewer administrative duties, the team has gained extra time, according to the director, a researcher studying language who hopes to extend the methods that were tested last year.

**任务**：划分意群，指出主干及嵌入成分的作用，再作直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：With fewer administrative duties, / the team has gained extra time, / according to the director, / a researcher [studying language] / [who hopes to extend the methods [that were tested last year]].

**训练句直译**：据主任介绍，由于行政事务减少，团队获得了额外时间；主任是一位研究语言的学者，希望推广去年测试过的方法。

**训练句拆解**：With 短语说明背景；researcher 是 director 同位语；studying 分词定语；who 指人，that 指 methods，were tested 为被动。
