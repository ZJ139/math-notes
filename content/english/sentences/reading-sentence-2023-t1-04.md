## 原句

> Branch points out that, even if a growing number of official guidelines and textbooks reflect scientific consensus on climate change, unofficial educational materials that convey more slanted perspectives are being distributed to teachers.

出处：2023年考研英语阅读 Part A，Text 1。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2023英一.pdf#page=2)。定位：所附试卷 PDF 第 2 页；本句原文已核对。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> Branch points out / that, even if a growing number of official guidelines and textbooks reflect scientific consensus on climate change, / unofficial educational materials / that convey more slanted perspectives / are being distributed to teachers.

“/”划分意群；方括号标出从句或嵌套成分。断句用于辨认结构，不表示每处都必须停顿。

### 主干读法

**主干读法**：Branch points out that unofficial educational materials are being distributed to teachers.

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法作用与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| Branch points out | 主句主语 Branch，谓语 points out 表指出；其后的 that 引出宾语内容。 |
| that, even if a growing number of official guidelines and textbooks reflect scientific consensus on climate change, | that 内先放 even if 让步条件从句；a growing number of guidelines and textbooks 为主语，official 修饰指南，reflect 为谓语，scientific consensus 为宾语，on climate change 限定共识主题。 |
| unofficial educational materials | 宾语从句核心主语 unofficial educational materials，unofficial 与前文 official 对照。 |
| that convey more slanted perspectives | 定语从句修饰 materials；that 作主语，convey 为谓语，more slanted perspectives 为宾语，more 表更为偏颇。 |
| are being distributed to teachers. | 宾语从句主谓语 are being distributed，为现在进行被动；to teachers 为接收对象。 |

### 理解要点

even if 段落是让步，不是主结论；作者提醒正式材料改善，并不排除非正式偏向材料传播。being 表正在发生的被动，不是仅有未来可能。

## 句子直译

布兰奇指出，即使越来越多的官方指导文件和教材反映了气候变化方面的科学共识，传达更为偏颇观点的非官方教学材料仍正在分发给教师。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。建议先遮住参考答案完成断句与翻译。

### 迁移练习 1

> The review notes that even if official reports become clearer, informal messages that distort the findings are still being shared.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The review / notes [that even if official reports become clearer, / informal messages [that distort the findings] are still being shared].

**训练句直译**：评述指出，即使官方报告变得更清楚，歪曲研究发现的非正式信息仍正在传播。

**训练句拆解**：that 宾语从句中 even if 为让步；messages 为核心主语，are being shared 为进行被动。

### 迁移练习 2

> Teachers warn that although textbooks explain the evidence, videos that promote unsupported claims continue to reach students.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：Teachers / warn [that although textbooks explain the evidence, / videos [that promote unsupported claims] continue to reach students].

**训练句直译**：教师提醒说，虽然教材解释了证据，宣传无依据主张的视频仍不断接触学生。

**训练句拆解**：although 让步；that promote 修饰 videos，videos continue 为宾语从句主干。
