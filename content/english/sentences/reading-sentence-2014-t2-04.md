## 原句

> In fact, allowing non-lawyers to own shares in law firms would reduce costs and improve services to customers, by encouraging law firms to use technology and to employ professional managers to focus on improving firms’ efficiency.

出处：2014年考研英语一阅读 Part A，Text 2，第 6 段。[查看试卷原文](/files/english/exams/2014英一.pdf#page=3)。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

“/”分隔意义组，“//”分隔同层并列分句；方括号标出嵌入结构，断句不改变词语归属。

> In fact, / allowing non-lawyers to own shares in law firms / would reduce costs and improve services to customers, / by encouraging law firms / to use technology / and to employ professional managers / to focus on improving firms’ efficiency.

### 主干读法

Allowing non-lawyers to own shares would reduce costs and improve services. by 后说明产生这些好处的方式。

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法身份与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| In fact | 全句评价/衔接状语，转向作者的实际判断。 |
| allowing non-lawyers to own shares in law firms | 主句动名词主语；allow 的宾语 non-lawyers，to own shares 为宾补；in law firms 修饰 shares，指事务所的股份。 |
| would reduce costs and improve services to customers | would 表假设改革后的预期；reduce、improve 并列，共用 would；costs、services 为各自动词宾语，to customers 修饰 services。 |
| by encouraging law firms | by 后接动名词表示方式；encourage 宾语 law firms，其逻辑主体为这项改革/允许持股的安排。 |
| to use technology and to employ professional managers | encourage 的两个并列不定式宾补，逻辑主语均为 law firms；technology 与 professional managers 分别为宾语。 |
| to focus on improving firms’ efficiency | 目的/任务不定式，说明聘请专业经理去做什么，focus 的逻辑主语是 managers；on 为介词，improving 是其动名词宾语；efficiency 为 improve 的宾语，firms’ 所有格限定。 |

### 理解要点

嵌套层级为 allow outsiders to own → encourage firms to use/employ → managers to focus → focus on improving。逐层找逻辑主语，避免把所有不定式都挂到非律师身上。

## 句子直译

事实上，允许非律师持有律师事务所的股份，会降低成本并改善面向客户的服务；实现方式是鼓励事务所使用技术，并聘用专业经理，让他们专注于提高事务所的效率。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。请先独立断句，找出主干及指定结构，再做直译。

### 迁移练习 1

> Allowing students to choose projects would improve motivation by encouraging teachers to use varied materials and to invite experts to guide the work.

任务：标出从句或非谓语边界，说明修饰关系，并直译全句。

**参考答案**

断句：Allowing students to choose projects / would improve motivation / by encouraging teachers / to use varied materials / and to invite experts / to guide the work.

**训练句直译**：允许学生选择项目，会通过鼓励教师使用多样材料并邀请专家指导工作来增强学习动力。

**训练句拆解**：allowing 动名词主语；by 表方式；to use、to invite 的主语 teachers，to guide 的执行者 experts。

### 迁移练习 2

> Training staff to use new tools could save time by enabling teams to focus on solving difficult problems.

任务：标出从句或非谓语边界，说明修饰关系，并直译全句。

**参考答案**

断句：Training staff to use new tools / could save time / by enabling teams to focus on solving difficult problems.

**训练句直译**：培训员工使用新工具，可以通过使团队专注于解决难题来节省时间。

**训练句拆解**：Training ... 作主语；staff 为 use 主语，teams 为 focus 主语；on 后 solving 动名词。

### 考场价值

嵌套层级为 allow outsiders to own → encourage firms to use/employ → managers to focus → focus on improving。逐层找逻辑主语，避免把所有不定式都挂到非律师身上。
