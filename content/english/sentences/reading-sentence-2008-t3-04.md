## 原句

> Moreover, even though humans have been upright for millions of years, our feet and back continue to struggle with bipedal posture and cannot easily withstand repeated strain imposed by oversize limbs.

出处：2008年考研英语一阅读 Part A，Text 3。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2008英一.pdf#page=4)。PDF 第 4 页为该篇文章所在页。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

“/”划分意群，“//”划分并列主句，方括号标出嵌入结构；断句符号用于阅读分析。

> Moreover, / [even though humans have been upright for millions of years], / our feet and back / continue to struggle with bipedal posture / and cannot easily withstand repeated strain [imposed by oversize limbs].

### 主干读法

Our feet and back continue to struggle and cannot withstand strain. even though 说明让步背景。

### 逐项分析

| 原文成分 | 语法作用与理解 |
| --- | --- |
| Moreover | 篇章副词，增加另一项身体限制。 |
| even though humans have been upright | 让步从句；humans 为主语，have been 为现在完成时，upright 为表语。 |
| for millions of years | 持续时间状语，millions of 表数百万。 |
| our feet and back | 主句并列主语，our 指人类的。 |
| continue to struggle with bipedal posture | 第一个谓语组；continue 后接不定式，struggle with 表艰难应对，bipedal 修饰 posture。 |
| and cannot easily withstand | 并列第二谓语，共用主语；cannot 表不能，easily 修饰 withstand。 |
| repeated strain imposed by oversize limbs | 宾语中心 strain；repeated 表反复的，imposed ... 为过去分词后置定语，by 引出施加负荷的来源 limbs，oversize 修饰 limbs。 |

### 理解要点

even though 后持续时间很长，仍不足以取消后面的生物结构限制。imposed 修饰 strain，不能误认作新的主句谓语。

## 句子直译

此外，尽管人类直立已有数百万年，我们的脚和背部仍在艰难地适应双足行走的姿势，也无法轻易承受过大四肢施加的反复负荷。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。


### 迁移练习 1

> Even though the bridge has served the town for decades, its supports continue to bend under heavy traffic and cannot withstand pressure caused by larger vehicles.

请先断句，指出主干及关键修饰关系，再直译。

**参考答案**：[Even though the bridge has served the town for decades], / its supports / continue to bend under heavy traffic / and cannot withstand pressure [caused by larger vehicles].

**训练句直译**：尽管这座桥为城镇服务已有数十年，它的支撑仍在繁重交通下弯曲，也无法承受更大车辆带来的压力。

**训练句拆解**：even though 让步；supports 共用两个并列谓语；caused 修饰 pressure，by 引原因来源。


### 迁移练习 2

> Although the students have studied English for years, they still struggle with complex sentences and often overlook meanings carried by small words.

请先断句，指出主干及关键修饰关系，再直译。

**参考答案**：[Although the students have studied English for years], / they still struggle with complex sentences / and often overlook meanings [carried by small words].

**训练句直译**：尽管学生们学英语已有多年，他们仍难以应对复杂句，而且常常忽略小词承载的含义。

**训练句拆解**：although 让步；struggle 与 overlook 并列；carried 为被动含义的后置定语，修饰 meanings。

### 考场价值

even though 后持续时间很长，仍不足以取消后面的生物结构限制。imposed 修饰 strain，不能误认作新的主句谓语。
