## 原句

> As so often, the past holds the key to the future: we have now identified enough of the long-term patterns shaping the history of the planet, and our species, to make evidence-based forecasts about the situations in which our descendants will find themselves.

出处：2013年考研英语一阅读 Part A，Text 3，第 6 段。[查看试卷原文](/files/english/exams/2013英一.pdf#page=4)。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

“/”分隔意义组，“//”分隔同层并列分句；方括号标出嵌入结构，断句不改变词语归属。

> As so often, / the past holds the key to the future: // we have now identified enough of the long-term patterns / [shaping the history of the planet, and our species], / to make evidence-based forecasts / about the situations [in which our descendants will find themselves].

### 主干读法

The past holds the key ... : we have identified enough patterns to make forecasts. enough ... to ... 表达到足以预测的程度。

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法身份与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| As so often | 省略式状语，相当于 as is so often the case，意为“正如经常发生的那样”。 |
| the past holds the key to the future | 冒号前主句；past 主语，holds 谓语，key 宾语，to the future 修饰 key，表示理解未来的钥匙。 |
| we have now identified enough of the long-term patterns | 冒号后主句；have identified 现在完成时，now 时间副词；enough of ... 作宾语，long-term 修饰 patterns。 |
| shaping the history of the planet, and our species | 现在分词定语修饰 patterns，patterns 是 shaping 的逻辑主语；history 宾语，of the planet and our species 说明地球和人类的历史。 |
| to make evidence-based forecasts | 与 enough 呼应的结果/能力不定式；逻辑主语 we；evidence-based 修饰 forecasts，表示基于证据的预测。 |
| about the situations | 修饰 forecasts，说明预测的内容。 |
| in which our descendants will find themselves | 定语从句修饰 situations；in which 相当于 in those situations，在内部作处境状语；descendants 主语，will find 谓语，themselves 反身宾语，find oneself in ... 表发现自己处于。 |

### 理解要点

抓住 enough ... to ...，再看 patterns 后面的长分词定语。in which 的介词来自 find oneself in a situation，而非随意附加。

## 句子直译

正如经常发生的那样，过去掌握着通向未来的钥匙：我们如今已经识别出足够多的、塑造地球及人类历史的长期模式，因而能够基于证据，对我们的后代将身处的情境作出预测。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。请先独立断句，找出主干及指定结构，再做直译。

### 迁移练习 1

> We have collected enough of the records describing past floods to predict the conditions in which future residents may live.

任务：标出从句或非谓语边界，说明修饰关系，并直译全句。

**参考答案**

断句：We have collected enough of the records / [describing past floods] / to predict the conditions [in which future residents may live].

**训练句直译**：我们已经收集了足够多的描述以往洪水的记录，因而能够预测未来居民可能生活的条件。

**训练句拆解**：describing 修饰 records；enough ... to ... 表足够；in which 来自 live in the conditions。

### 迁移练习 2

> The team has found enough clues pointing to the cause of the failure to design a test in which every factor can be controlled.

任务：标出从句或非谓语边界，说明修饰关系，并直译全句。

**参考答案**

断句：The team has found enough clues / [pointing to the cause of the failure] / to design a test [in which every factor can be controlled].

**训练句直译**：团队已找到足够多的指向故障原因的线索，因而能设计一个每个因素都可受控的试验。

**训练句拆解**：pointing 的主体 clues；to design 与 enough 呼应；in which 修饰 test，内部 can be controlled 被动。
