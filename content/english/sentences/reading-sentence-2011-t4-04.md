## 原句

> But it’s interesting to wonder if the images we see every week of stress-free, happiness-enhancing parenthood aren’t in some small, subconscious way contributing to our own dissatisfactions with the actual experience, in the same way that a small part of us hoped getting “the Rachel” might make us look just a little bit like Jennifer Aniston.

出处：2011年考研英语一阅读 Part A，Text 4，P5。[查看试卷原文](/files/english/exams/2011英一.pdf#page=5)。P 指文章自然段。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> But it’s interesting / to wonder if / the images / we see every week / of stress-free, happiness-enhancing parenthood / aren’t in some small, subconscious way / contributing to our own dissatisfactions / with the actual experience, / in the same way / that a small part of us hoped / getting “the Rachel” / might make us look / just a little bit like Jennifer Aniston.

“/”标出主要成分与嵌入结构的边界；结合下表判断哪些成分处于同一层级。断句不改变原句词序，不要求逐块机械翻译。

### 主干读法

It is interesting to wonder if the images are not contributing to our dissatisfaction, in the same way that part of us hoped a haircut might change our appearance.

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法作用 | 内部结构与理解 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| But it’s interesting | 主句转折与形式主语 | But 承接前文“成年人不至于仅因明星形象就要孩子”的让步；it’s = it is，it 为形式主语，interesting 为表语，真正主语是后面的 to wonder ...。 |
| to wonder if ... | 不定式真正主语 | to wonder 表思考、想知道，if 引导其宾语从句，意为是否，不是条件“如果”。 |
| the images we see every week of ... parenthood | if 从句主语整体 | 中心词 images；we see every week 为省去宾语关系词的定语从句，we 主语、see 谓语，every week 表时间；空位为 see 的宾语。 |
| of stress-free, happiness-enhancing parenthood | images 的分离后置修饰 | of 短语说明形象描绘的内容，虽与 images 被定语从句隔开，仍修饰 images。stress-free 与 happiness-enhancing 并列修饰 parenthood，意为没有压力、增进幸福的养育生活。 |
| aren’t ... contributing to | if 从句谓语 | are not contributing 为现在进行时否定；采用陈述语序 images aren’t，未倒装。否定疑问内容带试探性反思：是否事实上正在促成不满，而非断言完全没有影响。 |
| in some small, subconscious way | 插入的方式状语 | 修饰 contributing，some 限定 way，small 与 subconscious 为并列定语，表示以某种轻微且未意识到的方式；不是修饰 images 的大小。 |
| our own dissatisfactions with the actual experience | 介词 to 的宾语 | dissatisfactions 为中心，our own 强调我们自己的；with the actual experience 修饰不满，说明对实际育儿经历的不满。 |
| in the same way that ... | 比较或方式结构 | in the same way 修饰前述影响方式，that 引导对 way 的说明，意为正如……一样；这里不是另一个表示观点内容的 that 从句。 |
| a small part of us hoped ... | 方式从句内部主谓 | a small part 为单数中心，of us 限定范围；表示内心某个小角落的期待，hoped 为过去时谓语，后面省略宾语从句连接词 that。 |
| getting “the Rachel” might make ... | hoped 的宾语从句 | getting “the Rachel” 是动名词主语，指剪成 Rachel 同款发型；might make 为情态谓语，表当时想象中的可能性，不是确定结果。 |
| us look just a little bit like Jennifer Aniston | make 的宾语与宾补 | us 为 make 的宾语，look 为省去 to 的不定式宾补；like Jennifer Aniston 补足看起来像谁，just a little bit 表只稍微像一点，Jennifer Aniston 为演员姓名。 |

### 理解要点

三个层级依次为 it is interesting to wonder → images aren’t contributing → part of us hoped getting a haircut might make us look ...。作者讨论轻微、潜意识层面的影响，不是说人们会把育儿当作换发型。

## 句子直译

但是，值得思考的是：我们每周看到的那些毫无压力、增进幸福的育儿形象，是否正在以某种轻微、潜意识的方式，加深我们对实际经历的不满；就像我们内心的某个小角落曾希望，剪一个“瑞秋”同款发型，也许能让自己看起来稍微有一点像詹妮弗·安妮斯顿那样。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。请先独立断句，标出主干、从句与非谓语的作用，再作直译。

### 迁移练习 1

> It is worth asking whether the stories we read every day of effortless success are quietly shaping our expectations, in the same way that we once hoped buying a new notebook might make us more organized.

**参考答案**

断句：It is worth asking / whether the stories we read every day of effortless success / are quietly shaping our expectations, / in the same way that we once hoped / buying a new notebook might make us more organized.

**训练句直译**：值得问一问，我们每天读到的轻松成功的故事，是否正在悄悄塑造我们的期望；就像我们曾希望，买一本新笔记本也许能让自己更有条理一样。

**训练句拆解**：of effortless success 修饰 stories，与中心词被 we read every day 隔开。whether 引出疑问内容；hoped 后省 that，buying ... 为内部主语，us 为 make 宾语，organized 为宾补。

### 迁移练习 2

> It is interesting to consider if the photographs we see online of perfect homes are not increasing our dissatisfaction with ordinary rooms, just as we once imagined changing the furniture would transform our lives.

**参考答案**

断句：It is interesting to consider / if the photographs we see online of perfect homes / are not increasing our dissatisfaction with ordinary rooms, / just as we once imagined / changing the furniture would transform our lives.

**训练句直译**：值得思考的是，我们在网上看到的完美住宅照片，是否正在增加我们对普通房间的不满，就像我们曾想象更换家具就会改变生活一样。

**训练句拆解**：to consider 为真正主语；if 表是否，photographs 为其主语，of perfect homes 修饰 photographs。are not increasing 为试探性的否定疑问内容；imagined 后省 that，changing furniture 作宾语从句主语。

### 考场价值

三个层级依次为 it is interesting to wonder → images aren’t contributing → part of us hoped getting a haircut might make us look ...。作者讨论轻微、潜意识层面的影响，不是说人们会把育儿当作换发型。
