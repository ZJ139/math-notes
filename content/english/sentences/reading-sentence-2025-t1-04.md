## 原句

> O’Hanlon said more research would be needed but suggested that Shakespeare’s use of 20,000 words, compared with the everyday 2,000 words, gave a “massive expansion of language into children’s lives”, which was combined with children “using their whole bodies to bring words to life”.

出处：2025年考研英语阅读 Part A，Text 1，P7。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2025英一.pdf#page=2)。P 指文章自然段；PDF 第 2 页为本篇所在页。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> O’Hanlon / said [more research would be needed] / but suggested / [that Shakespeare’s use of 20,000 words, / compared with the everyday 2,000 words, / gave a “massive expansion of language into children’s lives”, / which was combined with children “using their whole bodies / to bring words to life”].

`/` 表示意群边界，`//` 表示同层并列分句边界，方括号表示嵌入结构；不改变原文用词。

### 主干读法

**O’Hanlon said more research would be needed but suggested that Shakespeare’s use gave an expansion.** compared with 插入比较，which 再说明语言拓展与身体参与相结合。

### 逐项分析

| 成分 | 结构与作用 |
| --- | --- |
| O’Hanlon said … but suggested … | 同一主语对应两个并列谓语；but 转折：需要更多研究，同时提出可能的解释。 |
| more research would be needed | said 的宾语从句，省略 that；research 为不可数主语，would be needed 为过去视角的将来被动。 |
| that Shakespeare’s use of 20,000 words | suggested 的宾语从句开头；主语中心 use，Shakespeare’s 为所有格，of 20,000 words 是 use 的内容。 |
| compared with the everyday 2,000 words | 过去分词比较状语；比较莎士比亚使用的词汇量与日常用词量；everyday 为“日常的”，不是 every day“每天”。 |
| gave a “massive expansion of language into children’s lives” | gave 为宾语从句谓语；expansion 为宾语中心；massive 表程度，of language 表拓展内容，into children’s lives 表进入的领域。 |
| which was combined with … | 非限制性关系从句，which 指前述语言拓展；was combined 为被动，与 with 搭配。 |
| children “using their whole bodies …” | with 后是 children + doing 形式的动名词结构，children 为 using 的逻辑主语，their whole bodies 为宾语；their 指 children。 |
| to bring words to life | using 的目的状语；bring … to life 为使……生动起来；words 作宾语，to life 为结果性介词补足。 |

### 理解要点

词汇数字是报道中提出解释的材料；句子先承认还需研究。不要把 suggested 译成已经证明，也不要把 which 错接到 children’s lives。

## 句子直译

O’Hanlon 说还需要更多研究，但她提出，莎士比亚使用两万个词，与日常的两千个词相比，使“大量语言进入了孩子们的生活”，而这种拓展又与孩子们“用整个身体让词语鲜活起来”相结合。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。

### 迁移练习 1

> The tutor said more evidence would be needed but suggested that reading varied texts, compared with repeating one passage, offered a richer vocabulary, which was reinforced by students using it in discussion.

**任务**：划分意群，指出主干及嵌入成分的作用，再作直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The tutor / said [more evidence would be needed] / but suggested / [that reading varied texts, / compared with repeating one passage, / offered a richer vocabulary, / which was reinforced by students using it in discussion].

**训练句直译**：导师说还需要更多证据，但他提出，与重复一篇文章相比，阅读多样文本提供了更丰富的词汇，而学生在讨论中使用这些词汇又加强了学习效果。

**训练句拆解**：reading texts 为主语；compared with 对比学习方式；which 指 richer vocabulary；students 是 using 的逻辑主语，it 指 vocabulary。

### 迁移练习 2

> She admitted that the trial was small but argued that writing for real readers, compared with writing only for marks, created stronger motivation, which was supported by teachers giving detailed feedback.

**任务**：划分意群，指出主干及嵌入成分的作用，再作直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：She / admitted [that the trial was small] / but argued / [that writing for real readers, / compared with writing only for marks, / created stronger motivation, / which was supported by teachers giving detailed feedback].

**训练句直译**：她承认试验规模很小，但认为，与仅为分数写作相比，为真实读者写作产生了更强的动力，而教师给予详细反馈又支持了这种动力。

**训练句拆解**：admitted/argued 并列；两个 writing 表比较；which 指 motivation；teachers 为 giving 的逻辑主语。

### 考场价值

词汇数字是报道中提出解释的材料；句子先承认还需研究。不要把 suggested 译成已经证明，也不要把 which 错接到 children’s lives。
