## 原句

> Social media allows users to experience news events more intimately and immediately while also permitting them to re-share news as a projection of their values and interests.

出处：2018年考研英语阅读 Part A，Text 2。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2018英一.pdf#page=3)。定位：所附试卷 PDF 第 3 页；本句原文已核对。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> Social media allows users / to experience news events more intimately and immediately / while also permitting them / to re-share news as a projection of their values and interests.

“/”划分意群；方括号标出从句或嵌套成分。断句用于辨认结构，不表示每处都必须停顿。

### 主干读法

**主干读法**：Social media allows users to experience news while permitting them to re-share it.

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法作用与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| Social media allows users | 主语 Social media 在此作整体平台概念，allows 为原文单数谓语；users 为宾语。 |
| to experience news events more intimately and immediately | 不定式作宾补，逻辑主语 users；news events 为 experience 的宾语；more 统摄 intimately 和 immediately 两个并列副词。 |
| while also permitting them | while 表同时，permitting 与 allows 并列补充社交媒体的另一功能，逻辑主语 Social media；also 为递进，them 指 users。 |
| to re-share news as a projection of their values and interests. | permit sb to do 结构；re-share 的宾语 news；as a projection 表分享所起作用，of 后 values、interests 并列，their 指用户。 |

### 理解要点

allow 与 permit 都采用“宾语 + to 不定式”；while permitting 的行动者是社交媒体，而非 users。as 说明转发成为价值观和兴趣的外在表达。

## 句子直译

社交媒体让用户更贴近、更即时地体验新闻事件，同时也允许他们重新分享新闻，把这种分享作为自己价值观和兴趣的外在表现。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。建议先遮住参考答案完成断句与翻译。

### 迁移练习 1

> The website allows readers to compare reports while also enabling them to share notes as a record of their thinking.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The website / allows readers to compare reports / while also enabling them to share notes as a record of their thinking.

**训练句直译**：网站让读者比较报道，同时还使他们能够分享笔记，以记录自己的思考。

**训练句拆解**：while enabling 与 allows 共用 website 作逻辑主语；readers/them 为两个不定式的逻辑主语。

### 迁移练习 2

> The course encourages students to discuss ideas openly while permitting them to revise opinions without embarrassment.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The course / encourages students to discuss ideas openly / while permitting them to revise opinions without embarrassment.

**训练句直译**：课程鼓励学生公开讨论观点，同时允许他们不必难堪地修正看法。

**训练句拆解**：encourage/permit 都接宾补不定式；without embarrassment 修饰 revise，表示修正观点时的状态。

### 考场价值
