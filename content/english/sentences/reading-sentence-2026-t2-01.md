## 原句

> The Art Directors Guild, a labor union representing about 3 000 film workers, has suspended a training program and issued a statement explaining that “we cannot in good conscience encourage you to pursue our profession while so many of our members remain unemployed.”

出处：2026年考研英语阅读 Part A，Text 2，P2。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2026英一.pdf#page=3)。P 指文章自然段；PDF 第 3 页为本篇所在页。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> The Art Directors Guild, / a labor union [representing about 3 000 film workers], / has suspended a training program / and issued a statement / [explaining that “we cannot in good conscience encourage you / to pursue our profession / while so many of our members remain unemployed.”]

`/` 表示意群边界，`//` 表示同层并列分句边界，方括号表示嵌入结构；不改变原文用词。

### 主干读法

**The Guild has suspended a program and issued a statement.** statement 后的 explaining 交代声明的具体内容。

### 逐项分析

| 成分 | 结构与作用 |
| --- | --- |
| The Art Directors Guild | 主句主语，机构名称。 |
| a labor union representing about 3 000 film workers | 同位语说明机构性质；representing 分词修饰 union，about 修饰数量，film 作 workers 的行业定语。 |
| has suspended a training program | 第一个现在完成时谓语及宾语；suspend 为暂时停止，training 修饰 program。 |
| and issued a statement | 第二个并列谓语，共用 has，故理解为 has issued；statement 为宾语。 |
| explaining that … | 现在分词短语修饰 statement，说明声明内容；that 引导 explaining 的宾语从句。 |
| we cannot in good conscience encourage you | 引语主谓宾；we 指工会，you 指拟进入行业的受训者；cannot 否定，in good conscience 为方式/立场插入语，表示问心无愧地。 |
| to pursue our profession | encourage somebody to do 的宾语补足语，逻辑主语 you；our profession 指影视行业职业。 |
| while so many of our members remain unemployed | while 引时间兼情境对照，“在如此多会员仍失业的时候”；so 修饰 many，of members 限定群体；remain 系动词，unemployed 表语。 |

### 理解要点

长同位语后，真正谓语 has suspended 才出现。issued 和 suspended 共用 has；explaining 修饰声明，不是第三个并列有限谓语。

## 句子直译

美术指导工会——一个代表约三千名电影工作者的工会组织——已经暂停一项培训计划，并发表声明解释说：“在如此多会员仍然失业的情况下，我们无法问心无愧地鼓励你们从事我们的职业。”

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。

### 迁移练习 1

> The association, a group representing local teachers, has cancelled a course and issued a notice explaining that it cannot responsibly invite new students while existing classes remain understaffed.

**任务**：划分意群，指出主干及嵌入成分的作用，再作直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The association, / a group [representing local teachers], / has cancelled a course / and issued a notice / [explaining that it cannot responsibly invite new students / while existing classes remain understaffed].

**训练句直译**：该协会是一个代表当地教师的团体，已取消一门课程并发布通知，解释说在现有班级仍缺教师时，它无法负责任地招收新学生。

**训练句拆解**：representing 修饰同位语 group；issued 共用 has；explaining 修饰 notice；while 引情境，remain 系表。

### 迁移练习 2

> The center, an organization supporting young artists, has paused recruitment and published a report stating that it cannot offer reliable guidance while many mentors remain unavailable.

**任务**：划分意群，指出主干及嵌入成分的作用，再作直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The center, / an organization [supporting young artists], / has paused recruitment / and published a report / [stating that it cannot offer reliable guidance / while many mentors remain unavailable].

**训练句直译**：该中心是一个支持年轻艺术家的组织，已经暂停招募，并发布报告称在许多导师仍无法参与时，它无法提供可靠指导。

**训练句拆解**：主谓 center has paused/published；stating 修饰 report；it 指 center；while 为背景，mentors 为从句主语。

### 考场价值

长同位语后，真正谓语 has suspended 才出现。issued 和 suspended 共用 has；explaining 修饰声明，不是第三个并列有限谓语。
