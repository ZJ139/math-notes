## 原句

> Now, with record-high home prices and historically low inventory, there’s an increased urgency in such regulation, particularly among those who worry that developers will come in and buy up swaths of housing to flip for a fortune on the short-term rental market.

出处：2023年考研英语阅读 Part A，Text 2。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2023英一.pdf#page=3)。定位：所附试卷 PDF 第 3 页；本句原文已核对。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> Now, with record-high home prices and historically low inventory, / there’s an increased urgency in such regulation, / particularly among those / who worry that developers will come in and buy up swaths of housing / to flip for a fortune on the short-term rental market.

“/”划分意群；方括号标出从句或嵌套成分。断句用于辨认结构，不表示每处都必须停顿。

### 主干读法

**主干读法**：There is an increased urgency in regulation, particularly among people who worry about developers.

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法作用与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| Now, with record-high home prices and historically low inventory, | Now 为时间状语；with 引出背景条件，其并列宾语为 record-high home prices 与 historically low inventory；historically 修饰 low，inventory 在住房语境中指可供房源。 |
| there’s an increased urgency in such regulation, | there is 存在句，increased urgency 为中心；in such regulation 限定紧迫性的领域，such 回指短租监管。 |
| particularly among those | particularly 限定尤其强烈的群体，among those 指在那些人中，后接定语从句。 |
| who worry that developers will come in and buy up swaths of housing | who 修饰 those 并作 worry 主语；worry 后 that 宾语从句，developers 为主语，will come in 与 buy up 并列，swaths of housing 为 buy up 宾语。 |
| to flip for a fortune on the short-term rental market. | 目的不定式，逻辑主语 developers；flip 省略前述住房作宾语，指短期经营/转手获利，for a fortune 为收益，on the short-term rental market 为市场场景。 |

### 理解要点

主句不是 with 后房价，而是 there’s urgency。those→who worry→that developers… 层层嵌套；买入房源为从短租市场获利，并不等于提供长期租住。

## 句子直译

如今，在房价创纪录、可用房源处于历史低位的背景下，这类监管的紧迫性增加了；尤其是那些担心开发商会进入并大量买下住房、借短租市场赚取巨额利润的人，更感到如此。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。建议先遮住参考答案完成断句与翻译。

### 迁移练习 1

> Now, with rents rising and available rooms shrinking, there is growing concern among students who fear that investors will buy entire buildings to make quick profits.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：Now, with rents rising and available rooms shrinking, / there is growing concern among students [who fear that investors will buy entire buildings to make quick profits].

**训练句直译**：如今，租金上涨、可用房间减少，担心投资者整栋买楼以迅速获利的学生越来越忧虑。

**训练句拆解**：with 背景后 there is 主句；who 修饰 students，that 作 fear 宾语；to make 是买楼目的。

### 迁移练习 2

> With demand at a record high, there is pressure for reform, especially among families who believe that current rules favor large companies.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：With demand at a record high, / there is pressure for reform, / especially among families [who believe that current rules favor large companies].

**训练句直译**：需求创下纪录之际，改革压力出现了，尤其来自那些认为现行规定偏向大公司的家庭。

**训练句拆解**：with 是无动词复合结构；there is pressure 主干；who 内嵌 believe that 宾语从句。

### 考场价值

主句不是 with 后房价，而是 there’s urgency。those→who worry→that developers… 层层嵌套；买入房源为从短租市场获利，并不等于提供长期租住。
