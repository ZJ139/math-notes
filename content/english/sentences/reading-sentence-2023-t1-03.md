## 原句

> Another issue is that while climate change is well integrated into some subjects and at some ages- such as earth and space sciences in high schools- it is not as well represented in curricula for younger children and in subjects that are more widely taught, such as biology and chemistry.

出处：2023年考研英语阅读 Part A，Text 1。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2023英一.pdf#page=2)。定位：所附试卷 PDF 第 2 页；本句原文已核对。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> Another issue is / that while climate change is well integrated into some subjects and at some ages / - such as earth and space sciences in high schools / - it is not as well represented in curricula for younger children / and in subjects that are more widely taught, / such as biology and chemistry.

“/”划分意群；方括号标出从句或嵌套成分。断句用于辨认结构，不表示每处都必须停顿。

### 主干读法

**主干读法**：Another issue is that climate change is not as well represented in some curricula.

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法作用与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| Another issue is | 主语 Another issue，is 为系动词，后面需要说明问题内容的表语从句。 |
| that while climate change is well integrated into some subjects and at some ages | 外层 that 表语从句中先嵌 while 让步；climate change 是主语，is integrated 为被动，well 为程度状语；into some subjects 与 at some ages 分别表课程领域和教学年龄。 |
| - such as earth and space sciences in high schools | 破折号举例说明学科与年龄，即高中地球与空间科学；不改变 while 从句层级。 |
| - it is not as well represented in curricula for younger children | 第二条破折号结束例子；it 指 climate change，是表语从句核心判断主语；is not represented 为被动，as well 表不及前述程度；in curricula for younger children 为教学范围。 |
| and in subjects that are more widely taught, | and 并列另一 in 范围；subjects 后 that 定语从句作主语，are taught 被动，more widely 表教学覆盖更广。 |
| such as biology and chemistry. | such as 举例说明上述广泛开设的学科，biology 与 chemistry 并列。 |

### 理解要点

读到 that while 应预期两层：while 给让步事实，后面的 it is not… 才是 that 内容中心。as well 的比较对象来自前文，而不是说年轻学生完全没有相关内容。

## 句子直译

另一个问题是，虽然气候变化在某些学科和某些年龄阶段得到了很好融入，例如高中地球与空间科学，但它在较小年龄儿童的课程以及生物、化学等更广泛教授的学科中，并没有得到同样充分的体现。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。建议先遮住参考答案完成断句与翻译。

### 迁移练习 1

> One problem is that while practical skills are taught well in senior classes, they are not as well represented in lessons for younger students.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：One problem / is [that while practical skills are taught well in senior classes, / they are not as well represented in lessons for younger students].

**训练句直译**：一个问题是，尽管高年级很好地教授实践技能，较小年龄学生的课程却没有同样充分地体现这些技能。

**训练句拆解**：that 表语从句内嵌 while；they 指 practical skills；not as well 与前半句程度对照。

### 迁移练习 2

> The concern is that although media literacy appears in some courses, it is less visible in subjects that most students take.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The concern / is [that although media literacy appears in some courses, / it is less visible in subjects [that most students take]].

**训练句直译**：令人担忧的是，尽管媒介素养出现在某些课程中，在多数学生修读的学科里却不那么明显。

**训练句拆解**：although 让步在 that 内；it 指 media literacy；that most students take 修饰 subjects，关系词作宾语。

### 考场价值

读到 that while 应预期两层：while 给让步事实，后面的 it is not… 才是 that 内容中心。as well 的比较对象来自前文，而不是说年轻学生完全没有相关内容。
