## 原句

> Orin Kerr, a law professor, compares the explosion and accessibility of digital information in the 21st century with the establishment of automobile use as a virtual necessity of life in the 20th: The justices had to specify novel rules for the new personal domain of the passenger car then; they must sort out how the Fourth Amendment applies to digital information now.

## 句子拆解

### 断句

“/”分隔意义组，“//”分隔同层并列分句；方括号标出嵌入结构，断句不改变词语归属。

> Orin Kerr, / [a law professor], / compares the explosion and accessibility of digital information in the 21st century / with the establishment of automobile use as a virtual necessity of life in the 20th: // The justices had to specify novel rules for the new personal domain of the passenger car then; // they must sort out [how the Fourth Amendment applies to digital information now].

### 主干读法

Kerr compares A with B。冒号后两分句对照 then 与 now：过去必须制定汽车领域规则，现在必须确定数字信息规则。

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法身份与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| Orin Kerr, a law professor | Orin Kerr 为主语；a law professor 是同位语，介绍身份。 |
| compares ... with ... | 主句比较结构，把21世纪数字信息状况与20世纪汽车使用相比较。 |
| the explosion and accessibility of digital information in the 21st century | compare 第一对象；explosion 与 accessibility 并列，分别指信息激增与容易获取；of digital information 说明对象，in ... 表时代。 |
| the establishment of automobile use as a virtual necessity of life in the 20th | with 的宾语中心 establishment；of automobile use 说明被确立的事项，as a virtual necessity 说明其地位；virtual 指事实上的，of life 修饰 necessity；20th 省略 century。 |
| The justices had to specify novel rules | 冒号后第一完整句；justices 主语，had to 表过去必要性，specify 谓语，novel rules 宾语。 |
| for the new personal domain of the passenger car then | for 说明规则适用领域；domain 中心，new/personal 修饰，of the passenger car 限定；then 与前半20世纪对应。 |
| they must sort out | 分号后新主句；they 指 justices，must 表现在的责任，sort out 表弄清、理顺。 |
| how the Fourth Amendment applies to digital information now | sort out 的宾语从句；how 作方式状语，Fourth Amendment 主语，applies to 为适用于，digital information 为介词宾语，now 与 then 对照。 |

### 理解要点

比较不是说手机就是汽车，而是说新技术成为生活必需品后，都迫使法院重新说明宪法保护如何适用。冒号后保留两段时间的对应。

## 句子直译

法学教授奥林·克尔把21世纪数字信息的激增及其易于获取，与20世纪汽车使用成为生活中事实上不可或缺之事相比较：大法官们当时必须为乘用汽车这一新的个人领域明确新规则；如今，他们必须理清第四修正案如何适用于数字信息。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。请先独立断句，找出主干及指定结构，再做直译。

### 迁移练习 1

> Maya, a historian, compares the spread of online classes with the rise of public libraries: schools had to broaden access then; they must decide how learning can remain fair now.

任务：标出从句或非谓语边界，说明修饰关系，并直译全句。

**参考答案**

断句：Maya, / [a historian], / compares the spread of online classes / with the rise of public libraries: // schools had to broaden access then; // they must decide [how learning can remain fair now].

**训练句直译**：历史学家玛雅把在线课程的普及与公共图书馆的兴起作比较：学校当时必须扩大获取机会；现在则必须决定如何让学习保持公平。

**训练句拆解**：同位语 Maya 后；compare A with B；冒号说明类比；how 从句为 decide 宾语。

### 迁移练习 2

> The author compares the introduction of electric buses with the replacement of horses: cities solved one set of problems then; they must work out what the new technology requires now.

任务：标出从句或非谓语边界，说明修饰关系，并直译全句。

**参考答案**

断句：The author compares the introduction of electric buses / with the replacement of horses: // cities solved one set of problems then; // they must work out [what the new technology requires now].

**训练句直译**：作者把电动公交的引入与马匹被替代作比较：城市当时解决了一组问题；如今必须弄清新技术需要什么。

**训练句拆解**：with 两端的两个名词短语构成比较；what 为 requires 宾语；then/now 形成类比。

### 考场价值

类比的是“新技术出现要求新法律适用规则”，不是说手机在法律上与汽车完全相同。
