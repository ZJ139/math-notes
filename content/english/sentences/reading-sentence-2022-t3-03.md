## 原句

> As science and technology progressed, and divided into more sub-disciplines, the centre was simultaneously looking to a time when leading researchers could also be artists, writers and poets, and vice versa.

出处：2022年考研英语一阅读 Part A，Text 3。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2022英一.pdf#page=4)（试卷 PDF 第 4 页）。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

“/”划分意群；先找限定谓语，再辨认从句与修饰范围。断句完整保留原文词序。

> As science and technology progressed, / and divided into more sub-disciplines, / the centre was simultaneously looking to a time / when leading researchers could also be artists, writers and poets, / and vice versa.

### 主干读法

As science and technology progressed and divided, the centre was looking to a time when researchers could also be artists. vice versa 反向补出艺术家等也能成为研究者。

### 逐项分析

| 原文成分 | 语法作用与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| As science and technology progressed, | as 引时间／过程状语从句；science 与 technology 并列作主语，progressed 为过去时谓语，表示随着发展。 |
| and divided into more sub-disciplines | and 在 as 从句内部连接第二个谓语 divided，与 progressed 共用主语；into 表分化结果，more 修饰 sub-disciplines。 |
| the centre was simultaneously looking to a time | 主句主语 the centre 指前文研究中心；was looking 为过去进行时，simultaneously 表与此同时；look to 在此表示展望、期待，a time 为介词 to 的宾语。 |
| when leading researchers could also be artists, writers and poets | when 定语从句修饰 a time，内部作时间状语；leading 修饰 researchers，could be 为情态系动词，also 表身份兼具；artists、writers、poets 并列作表语。 |
| and vice versa | 补充反向情形：艺术家、作家、诗人也可以成为研究者；不是将科学与技术的发展顺序反过来。 |

### 理解要点

第一个 and 连接从句中的 progressed 与 divided；后面的主句直到 the centre 才开始。when 修饰所期待的时代；vice versa 要沿着“研究者—艺术创作者”这一关系反向补全。

## 句子直译

随着科学与技术不断发展，并分化为更多子学科，这个中心同时也在展望这样一个时代：杰出的研究者也可以是艺术家、作家和诗人，反过来也一样。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。请先独立作答，再核对参考答案。


### 迁移练习 1

> As the university expanded and divided into new departments, its leaders were looking to a time when scientists could also be skilled communicators and vice versa.

请先断句，指出主干及关键修饰关系，再作直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：As the university expanded / and divided into new departments, / its leaders were looking to a time / when scientists could also be skilled communicators / and vice versa.

**训练句直译**：随着大学扩张并分设新院系，领导者们在展望一个科学家也能成为熟练传播者、反过来传播者也能成为科学家的时代。

**训练句拆解**：as 从句中 expanded/divided 并列；when 修饰 time；vice versa 反向补全 scientists 与 communicators 的身份关系。


### 迁移练习 2

> While the subject became more specialized and developed new methods, the school continued to imagine a future in which engineers could also be designers and designers could be engineers.

请先断句，指出主干及关键修饰关系，再作直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：While the subject became more specialized / and developed new methods, / the school continued to imagine a future / in which engineers could also be designers / and designers could be engineers.

**训练句直译**：当这门学科变得更加专门化并发展出新方法时，学校仍继续设想一种未来：工程师也可以是设计师，设计师也可以是工程师。

**训练句拆解**：while 从句中 became 与 developed 共用 subject；in which 定语从句修饰 future，含两个并列主系表，显式展示反向关系。

### 考场价值

第一个 and 连接从句中的 progressed 与 divided；后面的主句直到 the centre 才开始。when 修饰所期待的时代；vice versa 要沿着“研究者—艺术创作者”这一关系反向补全。
