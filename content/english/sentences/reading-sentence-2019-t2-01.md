## 原句

> Grade inflation — the gradual increase in average GPAs (grade-point averages) over the past few decades — is often considered a product of a consumer era in higher education, in which students are treated like customers to be pleased.

出处：2019年考研英语阅读 Part A，Text 2。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2019英一.pdf#page=3)。定位：所附试卷 PDF 第 3 页；本句原文已核对。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> Grade inflation / — the gradual increase in average GPAs (grade-point averages) over the past few decades — / is often considered a product of a consumer era in higher education, / in which students are treated like customers to be pleased.

“/”划分意群；方括号标出从句或嵌套成分。断句用于辨认结构，不表示每处都必须停顿。

### 主干读法

**主干读法**：Grade inflation is considered a product of a consumer era.

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法作用与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| Grade inflation | 主语，成绩膨胀，即分数普遍上升。 |
| — the gradual increase in average GPAs (grade-point averages) over the past few decades — | 同位说明主语；increase 为中心，gradual 修饰，in average GPAs 表增长对象，括号解释缩写；over the past few decades 表时间跨度。 |
| is often considered a product of a consumer era in higher education, | 被动谓语 is considered，often 为频度状语；a product 为主语补足语，of a consumer era 表产物来源，in higher education 限定时代所在领域。 |
| in which students are treated like customers to be pleased. | in which 非限制性定语从句修饰 a consumer era；which 作 in 的宾语，整体表在该时代中；students 为主语，are treated 被动，like customers 表对待方式；to be pleased 为被动不定式修饰 customers。 |

### 理解要点

把破折号整段暂括起来，就能连接 grade inflation 与 is considered。to be pleased 的受事是 customers；学生被看作需要取悦的顾客，而不是学生自己感到高兴就足够。

## 句子直译

成绩膨胀——过去几十年里平均绩点的逐渐上升——常被视为高等教育消费时代的产物；在这个时代，学生被当作需要取悦的顾客对待。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。建议先遮住参考答案完成断句与翻译。

### 迁移练习 1

> The shift — a gradual move toward online services — is seen as part of an era in which speed is treated as a necessity.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The shift / — a gradual move toward online services — / is seen as part of an era [in which speed is treated as a necessity].

**训练句直译**：这种转变——逐渐转向线上服务——被视为一个时代的一部分，在这个时代，速度被当作必需品。

**训练句拆解**：破折号解释 shift；in which 修饰 era；is treated as 为被动评价结构。

### 迁移练习 2

> The policy is a product of a system in which applicants are regarded as customers to be satisfied.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The policy / is a product of a system [in which applicants are regarded as customers to be satisfied].

**训练句直译**：这项政策是某种制度的产物，在该制度中，申请者被视为需要使其满意的顾客。

**训练句拆解**：in which 指 system；applicants are regarded as；to be satisfied 被动修饰 customers。
