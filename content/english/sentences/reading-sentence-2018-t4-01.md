## 原句

> Fundamentally, the USPS is in a historic squeeze between technological change that has permanently decreased demand for its bread-and-butter product, first-class mail, and a regulatory structure that denies management the flexibility to adjust its operations to the new reality.

出处：2018年考研英语阅读 Part A，Text 4。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2018英一.pdf#page=5)。定位：所附试卷 PDF 第 5 页；本句原文已核对。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> Fundamentally, the USPS is in a historic squeeze / between technological change [that has permanently decreased demand / for its bread-and-butter product, first-class mail,] / and a regulatory structure [that denies management the flexibility / to adjust its operations to the new reality].

“/”划分意群；方括号标出从句或嵌套成分。断句用于辨认结构，不表示每处都必须停顿。

### 主干读法

**主干读法**：The USPS is squeezed between technological change and a regulatory structure.

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法作用与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| Fundamentally, the USPS is in a historic squeeze | Fundamentally 为角度副词；USPS 主语，is 系动词，in a historic squeeze 表困境状态，historic 修饰 squeeze。 |
| between technological change [that has permanently decreased demand | between 第一宾语为 technological change；that 作定语从句主语，has decreased 为完成谓语，permanently 为状语，demand 为宾语。 |
| for its bread-and-butter product, first-class mail,] | for 短语修饰 demand，its 指 USPS；bread-and-butter 修饰 product，first-class mail 为同位说明。 |
| and a regulatory structure [that denies management the flexibility | and 连接 between 的第二宾语 structure，非连接主句谓语；that 作定语从句主语，denies 为谓语，management 与 flexibility 分别为间接、直接宾语。 |
| to adjust its operations to the new reality]. | 不定式修饰 flexibility，说明调整能力；逻辑主体为 management；its 指 USPS，operations 为 adjust 宾语，to the new reality 表适应目标。 |

### 理解要点

先标出 between A and B：A 技术变化减少需求，B 监管结构限制调整。deny sb sth 为不给某人某物，不能把 management the flexibility 拆成两个新分句。

## 句子直译

从根本上说，美国邮政局处于一场历史性的双重挤压中：一边是技术变化，它永久性地减少了对其主要收入产品——一类邮件——的需求；另一边是监管结构，它使管理层缺少根据新现实调整业务的灵活性。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。建议先遮住参考答案完成断句与翻译。

### 迁移练习 1

> The college is caught between rising costs that reduce its budget and strict rules that deny staff the freedom to change courses.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The college / is caught between rising costs [that reduce its budget] / and strict rules [that deny staff the freedom to change courses].

**训练句直译**：学院被夹在不断上涨、压缩预算的成本和不给教职员调整课程自由的严格规定之间。

**训练句拆解**：between 两个长名词项并列；deny staff freedom 为双宾语，to change 修饰 freedom。

### 迁移练习 2

> The firm faces a market that demands lower prices and a system that denies managers the power to cut costs.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The firm / faces a market [that demands lower prices] / and a system [that denies managers the power to cut costs].

**训练句直译**：公司面对一个要求更低价格的市场，以及一个不给管理者削减成本权力的体制。

**训练句拆解**：and 并列 faces 的宾语；两个 that 分别作从句主语；to cut 说明 power 的内容。
