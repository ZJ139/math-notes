## 原句

> To encourage innovation and competition, the report calls for increased investment in research, the crafting of coherent curricula that improve students’ ability to solve problems and communicate effectively in the 21st century, increased funding for teachers and the encouragement of scholars to bring their learning to bear on the great challenges of the day.

出处：2014年考研英语一阅读 Part A，Text 4，第 3 段。[查看试卷原文](/files/english/exams/2014英一.pdf#page=5)。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

“/”分隔意义组，“//”分隔同层并列分句；方括号标出嵌入结构，断句不改变词语归属。

> To encourage innovation and competition, / the report calls for / increased investment in research, / the crafting of coherent curricula [that improve students’ ability / to solve problems and communicate effectively in the 21st century], / increased funding for teachers / and the encouragement of scholars / to bring their learning to bear on the great challenges of the day.

### 主干读法

The report calls for A, B, C and D. 四项依次为研究投资、课程制定、教师经费、鼓励学者运用学识。

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法身份与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| To encourage innovation and competition | 句首目的不定式，两个并列宾语 innovation、competition；报告通过其建议促进二者。 |
| the report calls for | 主句主语及谓语搭配，call for 表呼吁，需要。 |
| increased investment in research | 第一项介词宾语；increased 分词定语，in research 指投资领域。 |
| the crafting of coherent curricula | 第二项宾语，中心 crafting 为制定，of 后说明制定的对象；coherent 修饰 curricula，后者是 curriculum 的复数。 |
| that improve students’ ability | 限制性定语从句修饰 curricula；that 主语，improve 谓语，ability 宾语，students’ 所有格。 |
| to solve problems and communicate effectively in the 21st century | 不定式修饰 ability，两个并列动词 solve 与 communicate 共用 to，逻辑主语 students；effectively 修饰 communicate，in ... 为时代背景。 |
| increased funding for teachers | 第三项宾语，increased 修饰 funding，for teachers 表经费用途/受益者。 |
| and the encouragement of scholars | 第四项宾语，中心 encouragement，of scholars 指鼓励学者。 |
| to bring their learning to bear on the great challenges of the day | 不定式说明鼓励内容，逻辑主语 scholars；bring A to bear on B 表运用 A 应对 B；their learning 为学识，great 修饰 challenges，of the day 指当今。 |

### 理解要点

逗号串联的是 call for 的四项并列对象，不是四个谓语。第二项很长，需跨过整个 that 从句才接回第三项 increased funding。

## 句子直译

为鼓励创新和竞争，报告呼吁增加研究投入，制定连贯的课程以提高学生在21世纪解决问题和有效交流的能力，增加教师经费，并鼓励学者运用其学识应对当今的重大挑战。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。请先独立断句，找出主干及指定结构，再做直译。

### 迁移练习 1

> To improve learning, the plan calls for better libraries, the design of courses that develop students’ ability to judge evidence, and support for teachers to share their experience.

任务：标出从句或非谓语边界，说明修饰关系，并直译全句。

**参考答案**

断句：To improve learning, / the plan calls for / better libraries, / the design of courses [that develop students’ ability to judge evidence], / and support for teachers to share their experience.

**训练句直译**：为改善学习，计划呼吁建设更好的图书馆，设计能培养学生判断证据能力的课程，并支持教师分享经验。

**训练句拆解**：call for 后三个并列宾语；that 修饰 courses；to judge 逻辑主语 students；to share 主语 teachers。

### 迁移练习 2

> The programme encourages engineers to bring their experience to bear on problems that local residents cannot solve alone.

任务：标出从句或非谓语边界，说明修饰关系，并直译全句。

**参考答案**

断句：The programme encourages engineers / to bring their experience to bear on problems [that local residents cannot solve alone].

**训练句直译**：该项目鼓励工程师运用经验解决当地居民无法独自解决的问题。

**训练句拆解**：engineers 是不定式逻辑主语；bring A to bear on B 固定搭配；that 作 solve 的宾语。

### 考场价值

逗号串联的是 call for 的四项并列对象，不是四个谓语。第二项很长，需跨过整个 that 从句才接回第三项 increased funding。

## 重要单词

以约 4000 词词汇量为参照；“可能陌生”也包括熟词在本句中的特殊搭配。

| 词语 | 类别 | 本句含义与用法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| call for | 核心 | 呼吁，要求 |
| crafting | 可能陌生 | 精心制定 |
| coherent | 可能陌生 | 连贯一致的 |
| curricula | 可能陌生 | 课程，curriculum 的复数 |
| funding | 核心 | 经费，资助 |
| bring ... to bear on | 可能陌生 | 把……运用于 |
| learning | 核心 | 此处为学问、学识 |
