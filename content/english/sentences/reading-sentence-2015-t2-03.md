## 原句

> California has asked the justices to refrain from a sweeping ruling, particularly one that upsets the old assumption that authorities may search through the possessions of suspects at the time of their arrest.

出处：2015年考研英语一阅读 Part A，Text 2，第 2 段。[查看试卷原文](/files/english/exams/2015英一.pdf#page=3)。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

“/”分隔意义组，“//”分隔同层并列分句；方括号标出嵌入结构，断句不改变词语归属。

> California has asked the justices / to refrain from a sweeping ruling, / particularly one [that upsets the old assumption [that authorities may search through the possessions of suspects / at the time of their arrest]].

### 主干读法

California has asked the justices to refrain from a ruling. one 替代 ruling，后有定语从句；assumption 后有内容从句。

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法身份与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| California has asked the justices | 主句主语 California 指州政府；has asked 现在完成时，the justices 宾语。 |
| to refrain from a sweeping ruling | 不定式宾补，逻辑主语 justices；refrain from 为避免，from 后名词 ruling；sweeping 表范围广泛。 |
| particularly one | 补充聚焦于某一种裁决；particularly 副词，one 替代前面的 ruling，不是数字一。 |
| that upsets the old assumption | 限制性定语从句修饰 one；that 作 upsets 的主语；old assumption 宾语，upset 表推翻/动摇。 |
| that authorities may search through ... | assumption 的同位语/内容从句；that 不作成分，authorities 主语，may search through 表可搜查，may 指许可。 |
| the possessions of suspects | through 的宾语；possessions 为财物，of suspects 限定所有者。 |
| at the time of their arrest | 时间状语，修饰 search；their 指 suspects；of their arrest 说明哪一时刻。 |

### 理解要点

两个 that 逐层追溯：one that ... 缺主语，是定语从句；assumption that ... 内部完整，是内容从句。不要把 justices 误作搜索财物的人。

## 句子直译

加利福尼亚州已经要求大法官们避免作出范围广泛的裁决，尤其是那种会推翻旧有假设的裁决；这一旧假设认为，当局可以在逮捕嫌疑人时搜查其随身财物。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。请先独立断句，找出主干及指定结构，再做直译。

### 迁移练习 1

> The school has asked teachers to avoid a rigid rule, especially one that supports the assumption that all students learn at the same speed.

任务：标出从句或非谓语边界，说明修饰关系，并直译全句。

**参考答案**

断句：The school has asked teachers / to avoid a rigid rule, / especially one [that supports the assumption [that all students learn at the same speed]].

**训练句直译**：学校要求教师避免僵硬的规则，尤其是支持“所有学生学习速度相同”这一假设的规则。

**训练句拆解**：one 替代 rule；第一 that 作 supports 主语；第二 that 引出 assumption 的内容。

### 迁移练习 2

> The report urges officials to reject proposals that rely on the belief that more data always lead to better decisions.

任务：标出从句或非谓语边界，说明修饰关系，并直译全句。

**参考答案**

断句：The report urges officials / to reject proposals [that rely on the belief [that more data always lead to better decisions]].

**训练句直译**：报告敦促官员拒绝那些依赖“更多数据总能带来更好决策”这一信念的提案。

**训练句拆解**：officials 是 reject 的逻辑主语；proposals 后定语从句，belief 后内容从句；always 限定后者断言范围。

### 考场价值

两个 that 逐层追溯：one that ... 缺主语，是定语从句；assumption that ... 内部完整，是内容从句。不要把 justices 误作搜索财物的人。
