## 原句

> Though several fast-fashion companies have made efforts to curb their impact on labor and the environment — including H&M, with its green Conscious Collection line — Cline believes lasting change can only be effected by the customer.

出处：2013年考研英语一阅读 Part A，Text 1，第 6 段。[查看试卷原文](/files/english/exams/2013英一.pdf#page=2)。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

“/”分隔意义组，“//”分隔同层并列分句；方括号标出嵌入结构，断句不改变词语归属。

> Though [several fast-fashion companies have made efforts / to curb their impact on labor and the environment / — including H&M, [with its green Conscious Collection line] —], / Cline believes [lasting change can only be effected by the customer].

### 主干读法

Though companies have made efforts, Cline believes change can only be effected by the customer.

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法身份与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| Though | 让步连词，标示前半公司的努力不足以推翻后半判断。 |
| several fast-fashion companies have made efforts | 让步从句主干；several 为数量限定，fast-fashion 复合定语；have made 现在完成时，efforts 宾语。 |
| to curb their impact on labor and the environment | 不定式限定 efforts 的内容/目的；their 指公司，impact 宾语，on labor and the environment 修饰 impact，表示受影响对象。 |
| including H&M | 介词性 including 引出前述 companies 中的例子。 |
| with its green Conscious Collection line | 补充 H&M 所拥有的环保系列；with 表具有，its 指 H&M，line 是产品系列，green 表环保。 |
| Cline believes | 主句主语与谓语；believes 后省略连接词 that。 |
| lasting change can only be effected by the customer | 宾语从句：lasting change 主语；can be effected 情态被动；only 限定实现途径，by the customer 为施事。effect 是“实现、促成”，不同于 affect“影响”。 |

### 理解要点

主张的焦点是“谁能促成持久改变”，不是否认企业已采取任何行动。effect 作动词容易被误读成名词效果或 affect。

## 句子直译

尽管几家快时尚公司已经努力遏制它们对劳工和环境的影响——其中包括推出绿色“环保自觉系列”的 H&M——克莱因仍认为，持久的改变只能由消费者促成。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。请先独立断句，找出主干及指定结构，再做直译。

### 迁移练习 1

> Though several schools have tried to reduce waste — including ours, with its new recycling scheme — the teacher believes lasting progress must be supported by students.

任务：标出从句或非谓语边界，说明修饰关系，并直译全句。

**参考答案**

断句：Though [several schools have tried to reduce waste / — including ours, [with its new recycling scheme] —], / the teacher believes [lasting progress must be supported by students].

**训练句直译**：虽然几所学校已尝试减少浪费——包括有新回收方案的我们学校——老师仍认为，持久进步必须得到学生支持。

**训练句拆解**：though 从句包含插入例子；ours 指 our school；宾语从句 must be supported 为情态被动。

### 迁移练习 2

> Although the firm has taken steps to improve safety, the report argues that real change can only be secured by independent checks.

任务：标出从句或非谓语边界，说明修饰关系，并直译全句。

**参考答案**

断句：Although [the firm has taken steps to improve safety], / the report argues [that real change can only be secured by independent checks].

**训练句直译**：虽然公司已采取措施改善安全，报告仍认为，真正的改变只能通过独立检查来保障。

**训练句拆解**：to improve 修饰 steps；that 引导宾语从句；only 突出 by 后的保障手段。

### 考场价值

主张的焦点是“谁能促成持久改变”，不是否认企业已采取任何行动。effect 作动词容易被误读成名词效果或 affect。
