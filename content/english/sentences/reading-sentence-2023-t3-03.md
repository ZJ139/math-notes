## 原句

> It comes at a time when authors are already worried about the consequences of the proposed merger between PRH and another big publisher, Simon & Schuster—the reduction in the number of unaligned UK publishers is likely to lead to fewer bidding wars, lower advances, and more conformity in terms of what is published.

出处：2023年考研英语阅读 Part A，Text 3。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2023英一.pdf#page=4)。定位：所附试卷 PDF 第 4 页；本句原文已核对。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> It comes at a time / when authors are already worried about the consequences of the proposed merger / between PRH and another big publisher, Simon & Schuster / —the reduction in the number of unaligned UK publishers / is likely to lead to fewer bidding wars, lower advances, and more conformity / in terms of what is published.

“/”划分意群；方括号标出从句或嵌套成分。断句用于辨认结构，不表示每处都必须停顿。

### 主干读法

**主干读法**：The dispute comes when authors worry about the merger; the reduction in publishers is likely to lead to several consequences.

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法作用与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| It comes at a time | 主句 It 指前述书商与出版商争端，comes 为谓语，at a time 为时间状语。 |
| when authors are already worried about the consequences of the proposed merger | when 定语从句修饰 time；authors 为主语，are worried 为系表/被动状态；already 为状语；about the consequences of the proposed merger 为担忧对象，of 引出合并。 |
| between PRH and another big publisher, Simon & Schuster | between A and B 修饰 merger，说明合并双方为 PRH 与另一出版商；Simon & Schuster 是后者名称。 |
| —the reduction in the number of unaligned UK publishers | 破折号后独立解释合并的后果；主语中心 reduction，in the number 指数量减少，of unaligned UK publishers 指独立于这些集团的英国出版商。 |
| is likely to lead to fewer bidding wars, lower advances, and more conformity | is likely to lead to 为主句谓语结构，表可能导致；fewer bidding wars、lower advances、more conformity 三项并列，分别为竞价减少、预付版税降低、出版趋同。 |
| in terms of what is published. | in terms of 表就……而言；what is published 为名词性从句作 of 的宾语，what 为被动 is published 的主语。 |

### 理解要点

破折号前是争端出现的时机，后面分析合并后独立出版商减少的影响。advances 是预付版税，不能译成进步；what is published 指获出版的作品内容。

## 句子直译

这一争端发生之时，作者们已经在担忧企鹅兰登书屋与另一大型出版商西蒙与舒斯特的拟议合并所带来的后果：英国未归入这些集团的出版商数量减少，可能导致竞价争夺减少、预付版税降低，以及出版内容更趋一致。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。建议先遮住参考答案完成断句与翻译。

### 迁移练习 1

> The dispute comes at a time when writers worry about a merger — the loss of independent publishers may lead to less variety in what readers can buy.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The dispute / comes at a time [when writers worry about a merger] / — the loss of independent publishers may lead to less variety in what readers can buy.

**训练句直译**：争端发生之时，作家正担忧一场合并；独立出版商的流失可能导致读者可购买的作品种类减少。

**训练句拆解**：when 修饰 time；破折号后独立主句；what readers can buy 作 in 的宾语，what 作 buy 宾语。

### 迁移练习 2

> The change arrives when schools are already facing cuts — fewer teachers are likely to mean less choice in what students can study.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The change / arrives when schools are already facing cuts / — fewer teachers are likely to mean less choice in what students can study.

**训练句直译**：这一变化到来时，学校已面临削减；教师减少可能意味着学生可学习的内容选择变少。

**训练句拆解**：when 时间从句；破折号说明后果；are likely to 保留可能性；what 作 study 的宾语。

### 考场价值

破折号前是争端出现的时机，后面分析合并后独立出版商减少的影响。advances 是预付版税，不能译成进步；what is published 指获出版的作品内容。
