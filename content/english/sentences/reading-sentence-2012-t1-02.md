## 原句

> But in her new book Join the Club, Tina Rosenberg contends that peer pressure can also be a positive force through what she calls the social cure, in which organizations and officials use the power of group dynamics to help individuals improve their lives and possibly the world.

出处：2012年考研英语一阅读 Part A，Text 1，第 1 段。[查看试卷原文](/files/english/exams/2012英一.pdf#page=2)。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

“/”分隔意义组，“//”分隔同层并列分句；方括号标出嵌入结构，断句不改变词语归属。

> But / in her new book Join the Club, / Tina Rosenberg contends [that peer pressure can also be a positive force / through [what she calls the social cure], / [in which organizations and officials use the power of group dynamics / to help individuals improve their lives and possibly the world]].

### 主干读法

Tina Rosenberg contends that peer pressure can also be a positive force. 后半句解释这种积极作用的实现方式。

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法身份与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| But / in her new book Join the Club | but 承接上文的消极评价并转折；in ... 是观点所在出处，her 限定 book，书名是同位名称。 |
| Tina Rosenberg contends | 主句主语及谓语；contend 在此是“主张、认为”，不是竞争。 |
| that peer pressure can also be a positive force | contends 的宾语从句；that 只连接不作成分；peer pressure 主语，can be 谓语，a positive force 表语，also 表“也”。 |
| through what she calls the social cure | through 引出途径；what 从句作其宾语。she 为 calls 的主语，what 是宾语，the social cure 是宾语补足语：她把这个东西称作社会疗法。 |
| in which | 引导非限制性定语从句，先行词为 the social cure；相当于 in the social cure，说明在这种疗法中如何操作。 |
| organizations and officials use the power of group dynamics | 定语从句主干：并列主语 organizations and officials，谓语 use，宾语 the power；of group dynamics 修饰 power，指群体互动的力量。 |
| to help individuals improve their lives and possibly the world | 目的不定式，逻辑主语是 organizations and officials；help 的宾语 individuals，省略 to 的 improve 作宾补；their lives 与 the world 为 improve 的并列宾语，possibly 限定第二项。 |

### 理解要点

that 引导“认为”的内容，what 同时承担 calls 的宾语，which 则回指一个已出现的名词。三类连接词的作用不同。

## 句子直译

但是，在新书《加入俱乐部》中，蒂娜·罗森伯格主张，同伴压力也能够通过她所称的“社会疗法”成为一种积极力量；在这种疗法中，组织和官员利用群体互动的力量，帮助个人改善他们的生活，甚至可能改善世界。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。请先独立断句，找出主干及指定结构，再做直译。

### 迁移练习 1

> The teacher believes that competition can be useful through what she calls guided teamwork, in which students use their different skills to solve shared problems.

任务：标出从句或非谓语边界，说明修饰关系，并直译全句。

**参考答案**

断句：The teacher believes [that competition can be useful / through [what she calls guided teamwork], / [in which students use their different skills / to solve shared problems]].

**训练句直译**：老师认为，竞争可以通过她所称的“有指导的团队合作”发挥作用；在这种合作中，学生运用各自不同的技能解决共同问题。

**训练句拆解**：that 为 believes 的宾语从句；what 作 calls 的宾语，guided teamwork 作宾补；in which 回指 teamwork；to solve 表目的。

### 迁移练习 2

> We learned about what the guide called a living library, in which visitors talk to local people to understand the town.

任务：标出从句或非谓语边界，说明修饰关系，并直译全句。

**参考答案**

断句：We learned about [what the guide called a living library], / [in which visitors talk to local people / to understand the town].

**训练句直译**：我们了解了导游称为“活图书馆”的东西；在其中，游客与当地人交谈，以了解这座城镇。

**训练句拆解**：about 的宾语为 what 从句；what 是 called 的宾语；in which 修饰 library；to understand 的逻辑主语是 visitors。

### 考场价值

that 引导“认为”的内容，what 同时承担 calls 的宾语，which 则回指一个已出现的名词。三类连接词的作用不同。
