## 原句

> The cases the court overturned said that if a business was shipping a customer’s purchase to a state where the business didn’t have a physical presence such as a warehouse or office, the business didn’t have to collect sales tax for the state.

出处：2019年考研英语阅读 Part A，Text 4。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2019英一.pdf#page=5)。定位：所附试卷 PDF 第 5 页；本句原文已核对。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> The cases [the court overturned] said / that [if a business was shipping a customer’s purchase / to a state [where the business didn’t have a physical presence / such as a warehouse or office]], / the business didn’t have to collect sales tax for the state.

“/”划分意群；方括号标出从句或嵌套成分。断句用于辨认结构，不表示每处都必须停顿。

### 主干读法

**主干读法**：The cases said that if a business lacked a physical presence in a state, it did not have to collect that state’s sales tax.

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法作用与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| The cases [the court overturned] said | 主句主语 cases 指判例，the court overturned 为省略宾语关系词的定语从句；said 为主谓语，意为原判例规定。 |
| that [if a business was shipping a customer’s purchase | that 引导 said 的宾语从句，先嵌 if 条件；business 为条件主语，was shipping 过去进行谓语，a customer’s purchase 指客户所购商品。 |
| to a state [where the business didn’t have a physical presence | to a state 为发货目的地；where 定语从句修饰 state，business 为主语，didn’t have 为谓语，a physical presence 为宾语。 |
| such as a warehouse or office]], | such as 举例说明实体地点；warehouse 与 office 并列；此处同时结束地点从句及 if 条件。 |
| the business didn’t have to collect sales tax for the state. | that 宾语从句的核心结论；business 主语，didn’t have to 表没有义务，并非禁止；collect 谓语内容，sales tax 宾语，for the state 表代该州收取。 |

### 理解要点

said 的内容要一直读到末尾；if 从句内部又套 where。did not have to 表“不必”，与 must not 的“不得”不同，保留这一强度才能读准旧规则。

## 句子直译

法院推翻的那些判例曾规定：如果一家企业把客户购买的商品运往某个州，而它在该州没有仓库或办公室等实体经营场所，那么该企业就不必代该州收取销售税。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。建议先遮住参考答案完成断句与翻译。

### 迁移练习 1

> The rules the board replaced stated that if a supplier delivered to a region where it had no office, it did not have to appoint a local manager.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The rules [the board replaced] / stated [that if a supplier delivered to a region [where it had no office], / it did not have to appoint a local manager].

**训练句直译**：董事会替换的规定曾写明：如果供应商向一个自己没有办公室的地区送货，就不必任命当地经理。

**训练句拆解**：定语 the board replaced 修饰 rules；that 内容内嵌 if 条件，再嵌 where 地点定语；did not have to 不表禁止。

### 迁移练习 2

> The policy says that if a student studies in a city where public transport is free, the school need not provide a travel allowance.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The policy / says [that if a student studies in a city [where public transport is free], / the school need not provide a travel allowance].

**训练句直译**：政策规定，如果学生在公共交通免费的城市学习，学校就不必提供交通补贴。

**训练句拆解**：主句 policy says；if 条件从句中，city 是介词 in 的宾语，后接 where 定语从句；need not 是无必要而非不能提供。

### 考场价值

said 的内容要一直读到末尾；if 从句内部又套 where。did not have to 表“不必”，与 must not 的“不得”不同，保留这一强度才能读准旧规则。
