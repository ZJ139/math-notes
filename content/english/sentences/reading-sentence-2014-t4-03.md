## 原句

> In 2010, leading congressional Democrats and Republicans sent letters to the AAAS asking that it identify actions that could be taken by “federal, state and local governments, universities, foundations, educators, individual benefactors and others” to “maintain national excellence in humanities and social scientific scholarship and education.”

出处：2014年考研英语一阅读 Part A，Text 4，第 2 段。[查看试卷原文](/files/english/exams/2014英一.pdf#page=5)。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

“/”分隔意义组，“//”分隔同层并列分句；方括号标出嵌入结构，断句不改变词语归属。

> In 2010, / leading congressional Democrats and Republicans / sent letters to the AAAS / asking [that it identify actions [that could be taken / by “federal, state and local governments, universities, foundations, educators, individual benefactors and others” / to “maintain national excellence in humanities and social scientific scholarship and education.”]].

### 主干读法

Democrats and Republicans sent letters asking that the AAAS identify actions. actions 后的关系从句说明哪些主体可采取何种行动。

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法身份与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| In 2010 | 主句时间状语。 |
| leading congressional Democrats and Republicans | 主句并列主语；leading 表重要的，congressional 表国会中的，共同修饰两党成员。 |
| sent letters to the AAAS | 主句谓语 sent，letters 宾语，to AAAS 表收信方。 |
| asking that it identify actions | asking 是分词，说明发信者的请求；it 指 AAAS，identify 为要求型虚拟语气，省略 should，不能改成 identifies；actions 为 identify 宾语。 |
| that could be taken | 第二个 that 为关系代词，修饰 actions，且作被动从句的主语；could be taken 表可以采取的。 |
| by “federal, state and local governments, universities, foundations, educators, individual benefactors and others” | 被动施事长列表；federal/state/local 修饰 governments，其后各项是并列施事；individual 修饰 benefactors，others 概括其他主体。 |
| to “maintain national excellence | 目的不定式，说明采取行动的目标；maintain 宾语 national excellence。 |
| in humanities and social scientific scholarship and education” | in 短语限定卓越所处领域，即人文学科与社会科学的学术研究和教育；不能把整个并列列成四个互不相关目的。 |

### 理解要点

先区分两个 that：asking that 后内容完整，是要求型宾语从句；actions that 后缺少被动动作主语，that 就是关系主语。长施事列表可整体括起。

## 句子直译

2010年，国会中重要的民主党和共和党人士致信美国艺术与科学院，要求它找出“联邦、州和地方政府、大学、基金会、教育者、个人捐助者及其他主体”可以采取哪些行动，以“维持国家在人文学科和社会科学学术研究及教育方面的卓越水平”。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。请先独立断句，找出主干及指定结构，再做直译。

### 迁移练习 1

> The committee sent a letter asking that the college identify measures that could be taken by teachers and parents to improve student safety.

任务：标出从句或非谓语边界，说明修饰关系，并直译全句。

**参考答案**

断句：The committee sent a letter / asking [that the college identify measures [that could be taken by teachers and parents / to improve student safety]].

**训练句直译**：委员会发函，要求学院找出教师和家长可以采取哪些措施来改善学生安全。

**训练句拆解**：第一个 that 引要求型从句，identify 原形；第二个 that 作被动关系从句主语；to improve 表目的。

### 迁移练习 2

> Researchers issued a statement requesting that the government support projects that could be developed by local teams to protect rivers.

任务：标出从句或非谓语边界，说明修饰关系，并直译全句。

**参考答案**

断句：Researchers issued a statement / requesting [that the government support projects [that could be developed by local teams / to protect rivers]].

**训练句直译**：研究者发表声明，要求政府支持可由本地团队开发、用于保护河流的项目。

**训练句拆解**：requesting 的逻辑主语 researchers；support 省略 should；第二个 that 指 projects，在 could be developed 中作主语；to protect 为目的。

### 考场价值

先区分两个 that：asking that 后内容完整，是要求型宾语从句；actions that 后缺少被动动作主语，that 就是关系主语。长施事列表可整体括起。
