## 原句

> The trade magazine The Bookseller reported that Waterstones branch managers were being told to remove PRH books from prominent areas such as tables, display spaces and windows, and were “quietly retiring them to their relevant sections” .

出处：2023年考研英语阅读 Part A，Text 3。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2023英一.pdf#page=4)。定位：所附试卷 PDF 第 4 页；本句原文已核对。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> The trade magazine The Bookseller reported / that Waterstones branch managers were being told / to remove PRH books from prominent areas / such as tables, display spaces and windows, / and were “quietly retiring them to their relevant sections” .

“/”划分意群；方括号标出从句或嵌套成分。断句用于辨认结构，不表示每处都必须停顿。

### 主干读法

**主干读法**：The magazine reported that branch managers were being told to remove books and were retiring them to their sections.

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法作用与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| The trade magazine The Bookseller reported | 主句主语 The trade magazine The Bookseller，其中刊名为同位说明；reported 为过去式谓语。 |
| that Waterstones branch managers were being told | 宾语从句由 that 引出；Waterstones branch managers 为主语，were being told 为过去进行被动，意为当时正被告知。 |
| to remove PRH books from prominent areas | 不定式作被动 tell 结构的补足语，逻辑主语 managers；remove PRH books from prominent areas 为把书移离显眼区域。 |
| such as tables, display spaces and windows, | such as 举例说明 areas，tables、display spaces、windows 三个名词并列。 |
| and were “quietly retiring them to their relevant sections” . | and 连接宾语从句内第二谓语 were retiring，承前同一主语 managers；quietly 表方式，them 指 PRH books，to their relevant sections 为移回所属区域。 |

### 理解要点

were being told 是被动，were retiring 是主动，不能因都有 were 就都译成被动。retire 在此为把书撤回通常书架，不是经理退休；and 没有跳出 reported 的内容。

## 句子直译

行业杂志《书商》报道，水石书店的分店经理当时正被要求把企鹅兰登书屋的书从桌面、展示区和橱窗等显眼位置移开，并且正“悄悄把它们撤回所属书架区域”。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。建议先遮住参考答案完成断句与翻译。

### 迁移练习 1

> The paper reported that staff were being asked to remove posters from entrances and were quietly moving them to notice boards.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The paper / reported [that staff were being asked to remove posters from entrances / and were quietly moving them to notice boards].

**训练句直译**：报纸报道，工作人员正被要求把海报从入口移开，并正悄悄将它们移到公告栏上。

**训练句拆解**：reported that 中两谓语共用 staff；were being asked 被动，were moving 主动；them 指 posters。

### 迁移练习 2

> The review found that librarians were being trained to use the system and were testing it with small groups.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The review / found [that librarians were being trained to use the system / and were testing it with small groups].

**训练句直译**：评述发现，图书管理员当时正接受系统使用培训，并正与小组一起测试系统。

**训练句拆解**：were being trained 是受训，were testing 是主动测试；to use 逻辑主语 librarians；it 指 system。

### 考场价值

were being told 是被动，were retiring 是主动，不能因都有 were 就都译成被动。retire 在此为把书撤回通常书架，不是经理退休；and 没有跳出 reported 的内容。
