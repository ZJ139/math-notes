## 原句

> A study last year by the National Center for Science Education, a non-profit group of scientists and teachers, looking at how state public schools across the country address climate change in science classes, gave barely half of US states a grade B+ or higher.

出处：2023年考研英语阅读 Part A，Text 1。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2023英一.pdf#page=2)。定位：所附试卷 PDF 第 2 页；本句原文已核对。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> A study last year / by the National Center for Science Education, / a non-profit group of scientists and teachers, / looking at how state public schools across the country address climate change in science classes, / gave barely half of US states a grade B+ or higher.

“/”划分意群；方括号标出从句或嵌套成分。断句用于辨认结构，不表示每处都必须停顿。

### 主干读法

**主干读法**：A study gave barely half of US states a grade B+ or higher.

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法作用与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| A study last year | 主语中心 A study；last year 后置表该研究进行的时间，主句谓语尚未出现。 |
| by the National Center for Science Education, | by 引出研究实施者 National Center for Science Education；其后逗号进入机构说明。 |
| a non-profit group of scientists and teachers, | 同位名词短语 a non-profit group 解释中心，of scientists and teachers 指成员；不参与主句主谓对应。 |
| looking at how state public schools across the country address climate change in science classes, | looking 为主动分词修饰 study；at 后 how 间接疑问从句作介词宾语，state public schools 为主语，address 为谓语，climate change 为宾语，across the country 修饰 schools，in science classes 表授课场景。 |
| gave barely half of US states a grade B+ or higher. | gave 才是主句谓语；barely half of US states 为间接宾语（获评对象），a grade B+ or higher 为直接宾语（所获评分）；barely 表仅仅。 |

### 理解要点

长主语内先有研究机构、机构同位语，再有研究内容；都不能把 gave 前的 looking 看成主谓语。give A B 表给 A 评 B，不是研究获得 B+。

## 句子直译

去年，美国国家科学教育中心——一个由科学家和教师组成的非营利团体——进行了一项研究，考察全国各州公立学校如何在科学课中处理气候变化议题；该研究给出的 B+ 或更高评分，仅覆盖美国约一半的州。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。建议先遮住参考答案完成断句与翻译。

### 迁移练习 1

> A report by the council, an elected local body, examining how schools use public funds, gave only three districts a high rating.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：A report by the council, / an elected local body, / examining how schools use public funds, / gave only three districts a high rating.

**训练句直译**：由议会这一地方民选机构提出的一份报告考察学校如何使用公共资金，只给三个学区较高评级。

**训练句拆解**：主干 report gave districts a rating；examining 修饰 report，how 从句作 examining 的宾语；local body 为 council 同位语。

### 迁移练习 2

> A survey last month, looking at how students find reliable information, gave fewer than half of the participants full marks.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：A survey last month, / looking at how students find reliable information, / gave fewer than half of the participants full marks.

**训练句直译**：上个月一项考察学生如何找到可靠信息的调查，给不到一半的参与者打了满分。

**训练句拆解**：looking at 修饰 survey；gave 后 participants 与 full marks 双宾语；fewer than 限定获满分人数。

### 考场价值

长主语内先有研究机构、机构同位语，再有研究内容；都不能把 gave 前的 looking 看成主谓语。give A B 表给 A 评 B，不是研究获得 B+。
