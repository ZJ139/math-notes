## 原句

> Certain artifacts are especially vulnerable because some pioneers in plastic art didn’t always know how to mix ingredients properly, says Thea van Oosten, a polymer chemist who, until retiring a few years ago, worked for decades at the Cultural Heritage Agency of the Netherlands.

出处：2022年考研英语一阅读 Part A，Text 1。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2022英一.pdf#page=2)（试卷 PDF 第 2 页）。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

“/”划分意群；先找限定谓语，再辨认从句与修饰范围。断句完整保留原文词序。

> Certain artifacts are especially vulnerable / because some pioneers in plastic art didn’t always know / how to mix ingredients properly, / says Thea van Oosten, / a polymer chemist / who, until retiring a few years ago, / worked for decades at the Cultural Heritage Agency of the Netherlands.

### 主干读法

Certain artifacts are vulnerable because some pioneers did not always know how to mix ingredients. says Thea van Oosten 是后置报道语，后面又有身份同位语和 who 从句。

### 逐项分析

| 原文成分 | 语法作用与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| Certain artifacts are especially vulnerable | 被报道判断的主系表；Certain 表某些，artifacts 为主语，are 为系动词，especially 修饰表语 vulnerable。 |
| because some pioneers in plastic art | because 引导原因从句；some pioneers 作主语，in plastic art 后置修饰 pioneers，说明从事塑料艺术的先驱。 |
| didn’t always know | 过去时否定谓语；not always 是否定“总是”，即并非总能或未必清楚，并非从来不知道。 |
| how to mix ingredients properly | 疑问词加不定式结构，整体作 know 的宾语；how 表方式，mix 的宾语 ingredients，properly 修饰 mix；逻辑执行者为 pioneers。 |
| says Thea van Oosten | 后置报道语，采用谓语 says 在前、说话者在后的语序；指出整段判断来自此人，不能将 Thea 当作 mix 的宾语。 |
| a polymer chemist | 与说话者同位的名词短语，说明她是一名聚合物化学家。 |
| who, until retiring a few years ago, | who 引非限制性定语从句，指 Thea 并作主语；until 后接动名词短语，retiring 的逻辑主语也是她，a few years ago 为退休时间。 |
| worked for decades at the Cultural Heritage Agency of the Netherlands | who 从句谓语 worked；for decades 表任职时长，at 后是工作机构，of the Netherlands 限定机构所属国家。 |

### 理解要点

先找两层报道：艺术品为何脆弱，是 van Oosten 的判断；who 后说明的是她的职业经历。didn’t always 只否定完全、始终掌握配方，不能译成“从不懂”。

## 句子直译

聚合物化学家西娅·范奥斯滕说，某些艺术品尤其脆弱，因为一些塑料艺术的先驱并不总清楚如何正确调配原料；她在几年前退休之前，曾在荷兰文化遗产机构工作数十年。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。请先独立作答，再核对参考答案。


### 迁移练习 1

> Some old photographs are especially fragile because early photographers did not always know how to store them safely, says a conservator who worked at the archive for twenty years.

请先断句，指出主干及关键修饰关系，再作直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：Some old photographs / are especially fragile / because early photographers did not always know / how to store them safely, / says a conservator / who worked at the archive for twenty years.

**训练句直译**：一位在档案馆工作了二十年的文物保护人员说，一些老照片尤其脆弱，因为早期摄影师并不总清楚如何安全保存它们。

**训练句拆解**：because 原因从句；how to store 作 know 的宾语；says conservator 为后置报道语；who 从句修饰 conservator。


### 迁移练习 2

> The records remain useful because their authors knew how to describe local events clearly, explains a historian who, until moving abroad last year, taught at the university.

请先断句，指出主干及关键修饰关系，再作直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The records / remain useful / because their authors knew / how to describe local events clearly, / explains a historian / who, until moving abroad last year, / taught at the university.

**训练句直译**：一位历史学家解释说，这些记录仍然有用，因为作者知道如何清楚描述当地事件；这位历史学家在去年移居国外之前，曾在这所大学任教。

**训练句拆解**：remain 是系动词；how 不定式结构作 knew 宾语；explains 后置；until moving 的逻辑主语 historian，与 who 相同。

### 考场价值

先找两层报道：艺术品为何脆弱，是 van Oosten 的判断；who 后说明的是她的职业经历。didn’t always 只否定完全、始终掌握配方，不能译成“从不懂”。
