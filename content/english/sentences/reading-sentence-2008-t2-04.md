## 原句

> Other models exist that are hybrids of these three, such as delayed open-access, where journals allow only subscribers to read a paper for the first six months, before making it freely available to everyone who wishes to see it.

出处：2008年考研英语一阅读 Part A，Text 2。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2008英一.pdf#page=3)。PDF 第 3 页为该篇文章所在页。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

“/”划分意群，“//”划分并列主句，方括号标出嵌入结构；断句符号用于阅读分析。

> Other models / exist / [that are hybrids of these three], / such as delayed open-access, / [where journals allow only subscribers / to read a paper for the first six months, / before making it freely available to everyone [who wishes to see it]].

### 主干读法

Other models exist that are hybrids. 其后用一种延迟开放模式举例。

### 逐项分析

| 原文成分 | 语法作用与理解 |
| --- | --- |
| Other models exist | 主句，models 为主语，exist 不及物。 |
| that are hybrids of these three | 定语从句被 exist 与先行词 models 分隔；that 作主语，hybrids 为表语，these three 指前述三种模式。 |
| such as delayed open-access | 举例说明 models，delayed 为形容词化过去分词。 |
| where journals allow only subscribers | where 引导定语从句修饰 delayed open-access，表示‘在这种模式下’；journals 为主语，allow 为谓语，only 限定宾语 subscribers。 |
| to read a paper for the first six months | 不定式作 allow 的宾补，逻辑主语 subscribers；for 表持续时段。 |
| before making it freely available | before + 动名词，逻辑主语为 journals；making 的宾语 it 指 paper，available 为宾补，freely 修饰 available。 |
| to everyone who wishes to see it | to everyone 补充 available 的对象；who 定语从句修饰 everyone，wishes 后接 to see，末尾 it 仍指 paper。 |

### 理解要点

exist 后的 that 仍修饰 models。only 限定能在最初六个月阅读的人；过后开放给所有想读的人。两个 it 均指 paper。

## 句子直译

还存在其他模式，它们是这三种模式的混合，例如延迟开放获取：在这种模式下，期刊在最初六个月只允许订阅者阅读一篇论文，之后再让所有想阅读它的人都能免费获取。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。


### 迁移练习 1

> New plans exist that combine both approaches, such as a shared service where libraries lend devices before making them available to schools that need them.

请先断句，指出主干及关键修饰关系，再直译。

**参考答案**：New plans / exist / [that combine both approaches], / such as a shared service [where libraries lend devices / before making them available to schools [that need them]].

**训练句直译**：还有结合两种方法的新计划，例如一种共享服务，在其中图书馆先出借设备，再将设备提供给有需要的学校。

**训练句拆解**：第一个 that 分离修饰 plans；where 修饰 service；making 的主语 libraries，末个 that 修饰 schools。


### 迁移练习 2

> Several options remain that cost very little, including an online course where students read a chapter before discussing it with anyone who has completed the task.

请先断句，指出主干及关键修饰关系，再直译。

**参考答案**：Several options / remain / [that cost very little], / including an online course [where students read a chapter / before discussing it with anyone [who has completed the task]].

**训练句直译**：仍有几种成本很低的选择，包括一种在线课程，在其中学生先读一章，再与任何已经完成任务的人讨论它。

**训练句拆解**：that 修饰 options；where 修饰 course；discussing 的逻辑主语 students，it 指 chapter，who 修饰 anyone。

### 考场价值

exist 后的 that 仍修饰 models。only 限定能在最初六个月阅读的人；过后开放给所有想读的人。两个 it 均指 paper。
