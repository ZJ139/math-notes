## 原句

> And interest groups ranging from postal unions to greeting-card makers exert self-interested pressure on the USPS’s ultimate overseer — Congress — insisting that whatever else happens to the Postal Service, aspects of the status quo they depend on get protected.

出处：2018年考研英语阅读 Part A，Text 4。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2018英一.pdf#page=5)。定位：所附试卷 PDF 第 5 页；本句原文已核对。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> And interest groups [ranging from postal unions to greeting-card makers] / exert self-interested pressure on the USPS’s ultimate overseer / — Congress — / insisting that [whatever else happens to the Postal Service], / aspects of the status quo [they depend on] get protected.

“/”划分意群；方括号标出从句或嵌套成分。断句用于辨认结构，不表示每处都必须停顿。

### 主干读法

**主干读法**：Interest groups exert pressure on Congress, insisting that aspects of the status quo get protected.

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法作用与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| And interest groups [ranging from postal unions to greeting-card makers] | And 承上；主语 interest groups，ranging 分词定语说明范围，from postal unions to greeting-card makers 是两端。 |
| exert self-interested pressure on the USPS’s ultimate overseer | exert 为主谓语，self-interested pressure 为宾语；on 引出施压对象，ultimate 修饰 overseer。 |
| — Congress — | 同位语，解释最终监督者是国会。 |
| insisting that [whatever else happens to the Postal Service], | insisting 为伴随分词，逻辑主语 interest groups；that 引导 insist 的内容从句；其中先嵌 whatever 让步状语，whatever 作 happens 的主语，else 表其他事情，to 引出遭遇者。 |
| aspects of the status quo [they depend on] get protected. | insist 内容从句主语为 aspects；of the status quo 指现状的某些方面；they depend on 是省略宾语关系词的定语从句，限定 aspects of the status quo 所说的“它们赖以维持利益的现状及其方面”；省略的关系词作 on 的宾语，they 指利益集团。get protected 为 get + 过去分词的被动结构；insist 在这里表要求，整个内容从句表达这些方面应当受到保护。 |

### 理解要点

whatever else… 相当于 no matter what else…，不作 insisting 的直接宾语；insisting that 的核心内容是 aspects get protected。they 指利益集团；depend on 限定它们依赖的现状。

## 句子直译

而从邮政工会到贺卡制造商的利益集团，向美国邮政局的最终监督者——国会——施加出于自身利益的压力，坚持要求：无论邮政服务还发生什么变化，它们所依赖的现状中的那些方面都要受到保护。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。建议先遮住参考答案完成断句与翻译。

### 迁移练习 1

> Local groups press the council, insisting that whatever changes occur, the services they rely on remain available.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：Local groups / press the council, / insisting [that whatever changes occur, / the services [they rely on] remain available].

**训练句直译**：当地团体向议会施压，坚持要求无论发生什么变化，他们依赖的服务都必须继续可用。

**训练句拆解**：insisting 逻辑主语 groups；whatever 让步嵌入 that 内容从句；they rely on 省略介词宾语关系词。

### 迁移练习 2

> The workers support reform, provided that whatever else is changed, the rights they have earned are respected.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The workers / support reform, / provided that whatever else is changed, / the rights [they have earned] are respected.

**训练句直译**：工人支持改革，条件是无论其他什么被改变，他们已经争取到的权利都得到尊重。

**训练句拆解**：provided that 引导条件；内部 whatever 让步；they have earned 修饰 rights，are respected 是条件中的核心谓语。

### 考场价值

whatever else… 相当于 no matter what else…，不作 insisting 的直接宾语；insisting that 的核心内容是 aspects get protected。they 指利益集团；depend on 限定它们依赖的现状。
