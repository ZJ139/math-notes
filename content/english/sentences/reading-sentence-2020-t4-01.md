## 原句

> But it has already sparked significant controversy, with the United States trade representative opening an investigation into whether the tax discriminates against American companies, which in turn could lead to trade sanctions against France.

出处：2020年考研英语阅读 Part A，Text 4，P2。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2020英一.pdf#page=5)。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> But it has already sparked significant controversy / with the United States trade representative opening an investigation / into whether the tax discriminates against American companies / which in turn could lead to trade sanctions against France.

### 主干读法

It has already sparked significant controversy. 主句说明已经发生的争议；with结构交代调查，从句说明可能后果。

### 逐项分析

| 成分 | 结构与作用 |
| --- | --- |
| But | 转折连接语：政策尚未生效，却已经引起争议。 |
| it | 主语，指法国数字服务税。 |
| has already sparked significant controversy | 现在完成时谓语has sparked；already为时间副词；significant修饰宾语controversy，表示重大争议。 |
| with the United States trade representative opening an investigation | with复合结构补充伴随情况；代表为opening的逻辑主语，opening为现在分词，an investigation为其宾语。 |
| into whether the tax discriminates against American companies | into引出调查的内容；whether引导介词宾语从句，在从句内不作成分。the tax为主语，discriminates against为谓语搭配，American companies为against的宾语。 |
| which in turn could lead to trade sanctions against France | 非限制性关系从句；which承接前述调查及其可能查明的歧视问题，不指American companies。which为从句主语；in turn表示继而，could lead to表示可能导致；sanctions为to的宾语，trade修饰sanctions，against France说明制裁对象。 |

### 理解要点

已经引发争议是事实，调查是否存在歧视仍是待查问题，制裁只是可能后果。不能把whether译成“因为”，也不能把could译成“已经”。

## 句子直译

但它已经引发了重大争议，美国贸易代表正在就该税是否歧视美国公司展开调查，而这继而可能导致对法国的贸易制裁。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。

### 迁移练习 1

> The rule has caused concern, with local officials starting a review into whether it treats small firms unfairly, which could eventually lead to changes.

**任务**：断句，分析with结构和从句，给出直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The rule has caused concern, / with local officials starting a review / into whether it treats small firms unfairly, / which could eventually lead to changes.

**训练句直译**：该规则引发了担忧，地方官员正在启动一项审查，调查它是否不公平地对待小企业，而这最终可能导致变化。

**训练句拆解**：with复合结构中officials为starting的逻辑主语；whether从句作into的宾语；which承接审查所涉及的问题与过程，could保留可能性。

### 迁移练习 2

> The decision has attracted criticism, with students asking whether the new fees are reasonable, which may prompt a public discussion.

**任务**：断句，分析with结构和从句，给出直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：The decision has attracted criticism, / with students asking / whether the new fees are reasonable, / which may prompt a public discussion.

**训练句直译**：这一决定招致了批评，学生们正在询问新费用是否合理，而这可能引起公开讨论。

**训练句拆解**：主句decision has attracted criticism；students为asking的逻辑主语；whether宾语从句中fees为主语、are为系动词、reasonable为表语；which承接质疑事件。

## 重要单词

| 词语 | 类别 | 本句含义与用法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| spark | 核心（熟词义） | 引发，并非名词“火花”。 |
| controversy | 可能陌生 | 公开的争议、争论。 |
| representative | 核心 | 代表，此处为贸易代表。 |
| investigation into | 核心搭配 | 对某事的调查。 |
| discriminate against | 可能陌生 | 歧视、不公平地对待。 |
| sanction | 可能陌生（语境义） | 本句复数指制裁，并非批准。 |
| in turn | 核心 | 继而、转而。 |
