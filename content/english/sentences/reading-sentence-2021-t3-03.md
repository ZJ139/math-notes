## 原句

> But exposure times were much quicker by the 1880s, and the introduction of the Box Brownie and other portable cameras meant that, though slow by today’s digital standards, the exposure was almost instantaneous.

出处：2021年考研英语一阅读 Part A，Text 3，P4。[查看试卷原文](/files/english/exams/2021英一.pdf#page=4)。P 指文章自然段。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> But exposure times were much quicker by the 1880s, / and the introduction / of the Box Brownie and other portable cameras / meant that, / though slow by today’s digital standards, / the exposure / was almost instantaneous.

“/”标出主要成分与嵌入结构的边界；结合下表判断哪些成分处于同一层级。断句不改变原句词序，不要求逐块机械翻译。

### 主干读法

Exposure times were quicker, and the introduction of portable cameras meant that exposure was almost instantaneous.

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法作用 | 内部结构与理解 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| But exposure times were much quicker by the 1880s | 第一主句 | But 与前文早期曝光时间很长形成转折；exposure times 为主语，were 为系动词，quicker 为比较级表语，much 加强比较，by the 1880s 表到十九世纪八十年代时。 |
| and the introduction of the Box Brownie and other portable cameras | 第二主句主语 | 第一个 and 连接两个主句；introduction 为单数中心，of 后 Box Brownie 与 other portable cameras 是并列引进对象，portable 修饰 cameras。 |
| meant that ... | 第二主句谓语与宾语从句 | meant 是 mean 的过去式，that 引出其内容；不是表主观意图想要，而是意味着某种技术后果。 |
| though slow by today’s digital standards | 宾语从句内让步省略 | though 后省略 the exposure was；slow 为表语，by ... 表按何种标准衡量，today’s 与 digital 修饰 standards。与主干 almost instantaneous 构成今昔标准对照。 |
| the exposure was almost instantaneous | 宾语从句主干 | the exposure 为主语，was 为系动词，instantaneous 为表语，almost 限定“近乎瞬间”，避免绝对化。 |

### 理解要点

两个时间参照不同：按当时标准曝光已几乎瞬间完成，按今天数码标准仍慢。though 插入到 that 后，不改变宾语从句主干 exposure was instantaneous。

## 句子直译

但是，到十九世纪八十年代，曝光已经快得多；“布朗尼”盒式相机和其他便携相机的出现意味着，尽管按今天的数码标准看仍慢，曝光当时已几乎能瞬间完成。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。请先独立断句，标出主干、从句与非谓语的作用，再作直译。

### 迁移练习 1

> The machines were much smaller by the 1990s, and the introduction of portable models meant that, though heavy by current standards, the equipment could be carried by one person.

**参考答案**

断句：The machines were much smaller by the 1990s, / and the introduction of portable models / meant that, / though heavy by current standards, / the equipment could be carried by one person.

**训练句直译**：到二十世纪九十年代，这些机器已经小得多；便携型号的出现意味着，尽管按目前标准看仍很重，设备当时已能由一个人携带。

**训练句拆解**：and 连接主句；that 引出 meant 内容，though 省略 equipment was。主干 equipment could be carried 为情态被动，by one person 表施事，by current standards 表评价标准。

### 迁移练习 2

> The new timetable meant that, although inconvenient for some residents, the service was available throughout the day.

**参考答案**

断句：The new timetable / meant that, / although inconvenient for some residents, / the service was available throughout the day.

**训练句直译**：新时刻表意味着，尽管对一些居民不方便，这项服务当时已能覆盖全天。

**训练句拆解**：although 省略 the service was，inconvenient 为表语，for residents 为受影响者；that 从句主干是 service was available，throughout day 为时间范围。

### 考场价值

两个时间参照不同：按当时标准曝光已几乎瞬间完成，按今天数码标准仍慢。though 插入到 that 后，不改变宾语从句主干 exposure was instantaneous。
