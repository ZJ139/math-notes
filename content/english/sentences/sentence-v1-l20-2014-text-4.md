## 原句

> Today, professors routinely treat the progressive interpretation of history and progressive public policy as the proper subject of study while portraying conservative or classical liberal ideas—such as free markets and self-reliance—as falling outside the boundaries of routine, and sometimes legitimate, intellectual investigation.

## 句子拆解

### 断句

“/”分隔意义组，“//”分隔同层并列分句；方括号标出嵌入结构，断句不改变词语归属。

> Today, / professors routinely treat the progressive interpretation of history and progressive public policy / as the proper subject of study / while portraying conservative or classical liberal ideas / — [such as free markets and self-reliance] — / as falling outside the boundaries of routine, [and sometimes legitimate], intellectual investigation.

### 主干读法

Professors treat A as the proper subject while portraying B as falling outside boundaries. 两组“把……看作……”构成对照。

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法身份与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| Today / professors routinely | Today 时间状语；professors 主语；routinely 副词，修饰后面经常性的对待方式。 |
| treat the progressive interpretation of history and progressive public policy | 主句谓语 treat；两个并列宾语为 interpretation 与 public policy；of history 修饰 interpretation，progressive 分别修饰解释和公共政策。 |
| as the proper subject of study | treat A as B 的补足部分；proper 修饰 subject，of study 修饰 subject，意为正当的研究对象。 |
| while portraying | while 引导省略主语及 be 的状语结构，可还原 while they are portraying；逻辑主语 professors，兼具同时与对照意味。 |
| conservative or classical liberal ideas | portraying 的宾语；conservative 与 classical liberal 两类思想由 or 连接，共同修饰 ideas。 |
| such as free markets and self-reliance | 破折号内举例说明 ideas；free 修饰 markets，self-reliance 指自立。 |
| as falling outside the boundaries | portray A as B；falling 为动名词/分词性补足，逻辑主语是 ideas；outside 为介词，boundaries 为宾语。 |
| of routine, and sometimes legitimate, intellectual investigation | of 短语限定 boundaries；中心 investigation，intellectual 修饰研究性质，routine 与插入的 sometimes legitimate 分别为常规的、甚至合法正当的。 |

### 理解要点

while 后仍是教授的行为，falling 的主体却是被描述的思想。作者批评一种思想被列为正当研究对象、另一种被排除的双重标准。

## 句子直译

如今，教授们通常把对历史的进步主义解释和进步主义公共政策当作正当的研究对象，同时却把保守主义或古典自由主义思想——例如自由市场和自力更生——描绘成超出常规学术探究的范围，有时甚至将其排除在正当学术探究的范围之外。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。请先独立断句，找出主干及指定结构，再做直译。

### 迁移练习 1

> Teachers sometimes treat familiar methods as reliable tools while portraying new approaches—such as peer review—as falling outside normal classroom practice.

任务：标出从句或非谓语边界，说明修饰关系，并直译全句。

**参考答案**

断句：Teachers sometimes treat familiar methods as reliable tools / while portraying new approaches / — [such as peer review] — / as falling outside normal classroom practice.

**训练句直译**：教师有时把熟悉的方法视为可靠工具，却把新方法——例如同伴评议——描绘成不属于正常课堂实践。

**训练句拆解**：主句 treat A as B；while 的逻辑主语 teachers；falling 的逻辑主语 approaches。

### 迁移练习 2

> The report presents large firms as sources of innovation while describing small businesses as lacking the resources needed for research.

任务：标出从句或非谓语边界，说明修饰关系，并直译全句。

**参考答案**

断句：The report presents large firms as sources of innovation / while describing small businesses as lacking the resources [needed for research].

**训练句直译**：报告把大公司描绘为创新来源，却把小企业描述为缺乏研究所需资源。

**训练句拆解**：while 省略相同主语；lacking 的主体 small businesses；needed 被动分词修饰 resources。

### 考场价值

选项如果只看到某种思想被研究，却忽略另一种思想被排除，就会错判作者批评的核心。
