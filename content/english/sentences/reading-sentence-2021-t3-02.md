## 原句

> Of course, I need to concede that my collection of ‘Smiling Victorians’ makes up only a tiny percentage of the vast catalogue of photographic portraiture created between 1840 and 1900, the majority of which show sitters posing miserably and stiffly in front of painted backdrops, or staring absently into the middle distance.

出处：2021年考研英语一阅读 Part A，Text 3，P2。[查看试卷原文](/files/english/exams/2021英一.pdf#page=4)。P 指文章自然段。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> Of course, I need to concede / that my collection of ‘Smiling Victorians’ / makes up only a tiny percentage / of the vast catalogue of photographic portraiture / created between 1840 and 1900, / the majority of which / show sitters / posing miserably and stiffly / in front of painted backdrops, / or staring absently / into the middle distance.

“/”标出主要成分与嵌入结构的边界；结合下表判断哪些成分处于同一层级。断句不改变原句词序，不要求逐块机械翻译。

### 主干读法

I need to concede that my collection makes up only a tiny percentage of the portraits, most of which show solemn sitters.

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法作用 | 内部结构与理解 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Of course, I need to concede | 主句及话语让步 | Of course 表当然；I 为主语，need 为谓语，to concede 补足需要做的事，指承认一种限制，而非撤回全部发现。 |
| that my collection of ‘Smiling Victorians’ makes up ... | concede 的宾语从句 | that 引出承认的内容；collection 为单数主语中心，of 后是其收藏内容的名称；makes up 为谓语，表构成、占到。 |
| only a tiny percentage of the vast catalogue of photographic portraiture | 比例宾语 | only 限定 a tiny percentage，表仅占很小比例；of catalogue 限定总体，vast 表庞大，of photographic portraiture 说明整个目录收集人像摄影作品。 |
| created between 1840 and 1900 | 过去分词后置定语 | 修饰摄影肖像作品，表示创作于1840至1900年之间；between ... and ... 连接时间端点。 |
| the majority of which show ... | 非限制性定语从句 | the majority of which 整体作主语；which 语义上回指前面目录中的摄影肖像作品，show 的复数形式也对应这些照片，并非抽象的目录编排本身。 |
| sitters posing miserably and stiffly | show 的宾语与宾补 | sitters 指被拍摄的人，为 show 的宾语；posing 为现在分词宾补，miserably 与 stiffly 并列修饰 posing，描绘愁苦而僵硬的摆姿。 |
| in front of painted backdrops | 摆姿地点 | in front of 为复合介词，painted 是过去分词定语修饰 backdrops，指画出来的摄影背景。 |
| or staring absently into the middle distance | 并列分词宾补 | or 连接 posing 与 staring，两者逻辑主语均为 sitters；absently 修饰 staring，into the middle distance 表目光朝向不远不近的空处。 |

### 理解要点

作者承认样本少，而不是说笑脸照片不存在。show 后的人物同时受 posing/staring 两种动作描述；the majority of which 的 which 要按前文摄影作品集合还原。

## 句子直译

当然，我需要承认，我收藏的“微笑的维多利亚人”只占1840至1900年间创作的庞大人像摄影作品集中的极小一部分；其中大多数照片显示，被摄者愁苦而僵硬地摆姿，站在绘制的背景前，或者心不在焉地凝视不远不近的地方。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。请先独立断句，标出主干、从句与非谓语的作用，再作直译。

### 迁移练习 1

> I must admit that my examples represent only a small part of the letters preserved in the archive, most of which show writers describing ordinary concerns.

**参考答案**

断句：I must admit / that my examples represent only a small part / of the letters preserved in the archive, / most of which show writers / describing ordinary concerns.

**训练句直译**：我必须承认，我的例子只代表档案中保存的信件的一小部分，其中大多数信件显示写信人在描述日常关切。

**训练句拆解**：that 引出 admit 内容；preserved 修饰 letters，which 指 letters。most of which 为 show 主语，writers 为宾语，describing 为分词宾补。

### 迁移练习 2

> The collection contains hundreds of photographs taken during the journey, many of which show travelers resting beside roads or waiting at stations.

**参考答案**

断句：The collection contains hundreds of photographs / taken during the journey, / many of which show travelers / resting beside roads or waiting at stations.

**训练句直译**：这份收藏包含旅途中拍摄的数百张照片，其中许多显示旅人坐在路旁休息，或在车站等待。

**训练句拆解**：taken 被动修饰 photographs；many of which 整体作关系从句主语。resting 与 waiting 并列为 show 的宾补，逻辑主语同为 travelers。
