## 原句

> Joana Lia Ferreira, an assistant professor of conservation and restoration at the NOVA School of Science and Technology, notes that archaeologists first defined the great material ages of human history- Stone Age, Iron Age, and so on- after examining artifacts in museums.

出处：2022年考研英语一阅读 Part A，Text 1。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2022英一.pdf#page=2)（试卷 PDF 第 2 页）。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

“/”划分意群；先找限定谓语，再辨认从句与修饰范围。断句完整保留原文词序。

> Joana Lia Ferreira, / an assistant professor of conservation and restoration / at the NOVA School of Science and Technology, / notes that archaeologists first defined / the great material ages of human history- / Stone Age, Iron Age, and so on- / after examining artifacts in museums.

### 主干读法

Joana Lia Ferreira notes that archaeologists defined material ages after examining artifacts. 人名后的身份说明很长，notes 才是主句谓语。

### 逐项分析

| 原文成分 | 语法作用与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| Joana Lia Ferreira | 主句主语，人名。 |
| an assistant professor of conservation and restoration | 同位语说明其职位；assistant professor 为助理教授；of 后 conservation 与 restoration 并列，说明文物保护和修复领域。 |
| at the NOVA School of Science and Technology | 后置补充任职单位；Science 与 Technology 并列属于校名。 |
| notes that archaeologists first defined | notes 为主句谓语；that 引宾语从句，archaeologists 为从句主语，first 修饰过去时 defined，意为起初确定、界定。 |
| the great material ages of human history | defined 的宾语；great、material 修饰 ages；of human history 限定时代划分所属范围，即依主要使用材料划分的人类历史时代。 |
| Stone Age, Iron Age, and so on | 插入列举前述 material ages；and so on 为等等，原文短横线用于隔开说明，不另含谓语。 |
| after examining artifacts in museums | after 接动名词短语作时间状语；examining 的逻辑主语为 archaeologists，artifacts 作宾语，in museums 说明文物所在／考察地点。 |

### 理解要点

身份同位语不能打断 Joana…notes 的主谓联系。after examining 的主体是考古学家，不是前面的助理教授本人；本句论证博物馆保存的实物会影响后人怎样划分历史。

## 句子直译

NOVA科技学院文物保护与修复专业助理教授若阿娜·莉娅·费雷拉指出，考古学家最初是在考察博物馆中的文物之后，才界定出人类历史上的重大物质时代——石器时代、铁器时代，等等。

## 原创拓展

### 迁移练习 1

> Mira Chen, a lecturer in cultural history at a small university, explains that researchers identified the trade routes after examining coins in local collections.

请先断句，指出主干及关键修饰关系，再作直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：Mira Chen, / a lecturer in cultural history at a small university, / explains / that researchers identified the trade routes / after examining coins in local collections.

**训练句直译**：一所小型大学的文化史讲师米拉·陈解释说，研究人员是在考察当地收藏中的钱币后确定了这些贸易路线。

**训练句拆解**：同位语说明 Mira 身份；explains 接 that 宾语从句；after examining 的逻辑主语 researchers，coins 为宾语。


### 迁移练习 2

> A museum specialist, whose work focuses on old tools, notes that scholars reconstructed farming practices after comparing objects from several regions.

请先断句，指出主干及关键修饰关系，再作直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：A museum specialist, / whose work focuses on old tools, / notes / that scholars reconstructed farming practices / after comparing objects from several regions.

**训练句直译**：一位研究重点为古老工具的博物馆专家指出，学者们比较了几个地区的器物后，重建了当时的耕作方式。

**训练句拆解**：whose 定语从句修饰 specialist；that 从句主语 scholars；after comparing 表时间，from several regions 修饰 objects。

### 考场价值
