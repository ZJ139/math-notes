## 原句

> Building on the basic truth about interpersonal influence, the researchers studied the dynamics of social influence by conducting thousands of computer simulations of populations, manipulating a number of variables relating to people’s ability to influence others and their tendency to be influenced.

出处：2010年考研英语一阅读 Part A，Text 3。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2010英一.pdf#page=4)（试卷 PDF 第 4 页）。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

“/”划分意群；先辨认限定谓语，再把从句与修饰语接回所属成分。断句完整保留原句词序。

> Building on the basic truth about interpersonal influence, / the researchers studied / the dynamics of social influence / by conducting thousands of computer simulations / of populations, / manipulating a number of variables / relating to people’s ability to influence others / and their tendency to be influenced.

### 主干读法

The researchers studied the dynamics by conducting simulations. Building 与 manipulating 的逻辑主语相同；relating 修饰 variables，层级更低。

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 作用及内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| Building on the basic truth about interpersonal influence, | Building on…为前置现在分词短语，逻辑主语是 researchers，表在已有事实基础上展开研究；on 是 build on 的介词，about interpersonal influence 后置限定 truth。 |
| the researchers studied | 主句主语 researchers、谓语 studied；不要把 Building 当作主句谓语，真正有限动词只有 studied。 |
| the dynamics of social influence | studied 的宾语；dynamics 指事物发展运行的机制，of social influence 补充研究对象。 |
| by conducting thousands of computer simulations | by 引出方法，conducting 为动名词，thousands of computer simulations 为宾语；computer 修饰 simulations，意为计算机模拟。 |
| of populations, | of populations 后置限定 simulations，说明模拟的是人群；不是说明研究者来自哪些人群。 |
| manipulating a number of variables | manipulating…为补充研究操作的分词短语，逻辑主语仍为 researchers；a number of variables 为宾语，a number of 意为若干。 |
| relating to people’s ability to influence others | relating to…为现在分词短语，后置修饰 variables；to 为介词，其第一个宾语是 people’s ability，to influence others 为不定式，说明能力的内容。 |
| and their tendency to be influenced. | and 并列 relating to 的第二个宾语 their tendency；their 回指 people，to be influenced 为被动不定式，说明倾向的内容，与前面的主动 influence 对照。 |

### 理解要点

三种 -ing 不在同一层：Building/manipulating 修饰研究行为，conducting 受介词 by 支配，relating 则直接修饰变量。末尾主动与被动不定式必须译出方向差异。

## 句子直译

在关于人际影响的这一基本事实的基础上，研究者通过对人群进行数千次计算机模拟，研究了社会影响的运行机制，并操控了一系列与人们影响他人的能力及受到他人影响的倾向有关的变量。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。先独立作答，再核对参考答案。


### 迁移练习 1

> Drawing on earlier findings, the team tested the model by running several simulations, changing variables related to users’ ability to remember and their tendency to forget.

请独立断句，指出与原句对应的结构，再直译。

**参考答案**

断句：Drawing on earlier findings, / the team tested the model / by running several simulations, / changing variables / related to users’ ability to remember / and their tendency to forget.

**训练句直译**：借助先前的发现，团队通过运行数次模拟检验模型，改变了与用户记忆能力及遗忘倾向有关的变量。

**训练句拆解**：Drawing/changing 的逻辑主语 team；running 为介词宾语；related 为过去分词修饰 variables，ability/tendency 是 to 的并列宾语。


### 迁移练习 2

> Building on classroom observations, the researchers compared the groups by measuring participation, focusing on factors relating to students’ confidence and their willingness to ask questions.

请独立断句，指出与原句对应的结构，再直译。

**参考答案**

断句：Building on classroom observations, / the researchers compared the groups / by measuring participation, / focusing on factors / relating to students’ confidence / and their willingness to ask questions.

**训练句直译**：在课堂观察的基础上，研究者通过测量参与情况比较这些组，重点考察与学生信心及提问意愿有关的因素。

**训练句拆解**：Building/focusing 修饰研究者的研究动作；relating 修饰 factors；to ask questions 说明 willingness 的内容。

### 考场价值

三种 -ing 不在同一层：Building/manipulating 修饰研究行为，conducting 受介词 by 支配，relating 则直接修饰变量。末尾主动与被动不定式必须译出方向差异。
