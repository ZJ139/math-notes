## 原句

> It is against that background that the information commissioner, Elizabeth Denham, has issued her damning verdict against the Royal Free hospital trust under the NHS, which handed over to DeepMind the records of 1.6 million patients in 2015 on the basis of a vague agreement which took far too little account of the patients’ rights and their expectations of privacy.

出处：2018年考研英语阅读 Part A，Text 3。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2018英一.pdf#page=4)。定位：所附试卷 PDF 第 4 页；本句原文已核对。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> It is against that background that / the information commissioner, Elizabeth Denham, / has issued her damning verdict / against the Royal Free hospital trust under the NHS, / [which handed over to DeepMind the records of 1.6 million patients / in 2015 on the basis of a vague agreement / [which took far too little account of the patients’ rights and their expectations of privacy]].

“/”划分意群；方括号标出从句或嵌套成分。断句用于辨认结构，不表示每处都必须停顿。

### 主干读法

**主干读法**：Against that background, the information commissioner has issued her verdict against the hospital trust.

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法作用与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| It is against that background that | 强调结构 It is…that，强调背景状语 against that background；that background 回指医疗潜力巨大而权力可能更集中这一背景。 |
| the information commissioner, Elizabeth Denham, | 被强调句还原后的主语 commissioner，Elizabeth Denham 为同位语姓名；information 说明职位领域。 |
| has issued her damning verdict | 现在完成时谓语 has issued；her 指 Denham，damning 修饰 verdict，整体作宾语。 |
| against the Royal Free hospital trust under the NHS, | against 说明判定针对的机构；under the NHS 后置修饰 trust，指其隶属关系。 |
| [which handed over to DeepMind the records of 1.6 million patients | 非限制性定语从句，which 指 hospital trust 并作主语；handed over 为谓语；to DeepMind 为接收者；较长直接宾语 the records…置后，of 1.6 million patients 限定记录归属。 |
| in 2015 on the basis of a vague agreement | in 2015 为时间状语；on the basis of 为依据，vague 修饰 agreement。 |
| [which took far too little account of the patients’ rights and their expectations of privacy]]. | 内层 which 修饰 agreement 并作主语；took account of 为“考虑”，far too little 表远远不够；of 后 rights 与 expectations 并列，their 指患者，of privacy 限定 expectations 的内容。 |

### 理解要点

先去掉 It is…that 还原强调句，再区分两个 which：外层说明 NHS 机构交数据，内层说明协议没有充分考虑患者权益。handed over 的宾语延后，不要误把 DeepMind 当记录的所有者。

## 句子直译

正是在这一背景下，信息专员伊丽莎白·德纳姆对英国国民保健制度下的皇家自由医院信托机构作出了严厉判定。该机构于 2015 年依据一份模糊协议，把 160 万名患者的记录交给了 DeepMind，而这份协议对患者的权利及其隐私期待考虑得远远不够。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。建议先遮住参考答案完成断句与翻译。

### 迁移练习 1

> It is in this context that the director has criticized the agency, which sent researchers the files under an agreement that gave participants little control.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：It is in this context that / the director has criticized the agency, / which sent researchers the files / under an agreement [that gave participants little control].

**训练句直译**：正是在这一背景下，主任批评了该机构；它依据一份几乎不给参与者控制权的协议，把文件发送给了研究人员。

**训练句拆解**：强调状语 in this context；which 指 agency，that 指 agreement；participants 为 gave 的间接宾语。

### 迁移练习 2

> It was after the review that the committee rejected the plan, which relied on data that took little account of local needs.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：It was after the review that / the committee rejected the plan, / which relied on data [that took little account of local needs].

**训练句直译**：正是在审查之后，委员会否决了该计划；该计划依赖的数据很少顾及当地需求。

**训练句拆解**：It was…that 强调时间；外层 which 修饰 plan；内层 that 修饰 data，take account of 表考虑。

### 考场价值

先去掉 It is…that 还原强调句，再区分两个 which：外层说明 NHS 机构交数据，内层说明协议没有充分考虑患者权益。handed over 的宾语延后，不要误把 DeepMind 当记录的所有者。
