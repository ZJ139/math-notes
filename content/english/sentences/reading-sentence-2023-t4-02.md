## 原句

> Recently, another expression of this predatory behavior has emerged: so-called support service consultancies that provide language and other editorial support to individual authors and to journals sometimes advise contributors to add a number of citations to their articles.

出处：2023年考研英语阅读 Part A，Text 4。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2023英一.pdf#page=5)。定位：所附试卷 PDF 第 5 页；本句原文已核对。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> Recently, another expression of this predatory behavior has emerged: / so-called support service consultancies / that provide language and other editorial support to individual authors and to journals / sometimes advise contributors to add a number of citations to their articles.

“/”划分意群；方括号标出从句或嵌套成分。断句用于辨认结构，不表示每处都必须停顿。

### 主干读法

**主干读法**：Another expression has emerged: support consultancies sometimes advise contributors to add citations.

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 语法作用与内部结构 |
| --- | --- |
| Recently, another expression of this predatory behavior has emerged: | Recently 时间状语；主语 another expression，of this predatory behavior 限定表现形式所属；has emerged 为现在完成谓语；冒号后解释这种新形式。 |
| so-called support service consultancies | 冒号后新分句主语中心 consultancies，so-called 表所谓的，support service 修饰咨询机构的业务类型。 |
| that provide language and other editorial support to individual authors and to journals | 限制性定语从句修饰 consultancies；that 作主语，provide 为谓语，language and other editorial support 为宾语；to individual authors 和 to journals 并列接收者。 |
| sometimes advise contributors to add a number of citations to their articles. | 冒号后主句谓语 advise，sometimes 为频度状语；contributors 为宾语，to add…为宾补；a number of citations 为 add 的宾语，to their articles 为添加位置，their 指 contributors。 |

### 理解要点

that provide… 很长，但主句动作在 sometimes advise。作者讨论不当增加引用这一掠夺性表现；不是说一切语言润色都不正当，sometimes 也不能省成总是。

## 句子直译

最近，这种掠夺行为又出现了一种表现：一些所谓的支持服务咨询机构，为个人作者和期刊提供语言及其他编辑支持，有时会建议投稿者往文章中加入若干引用。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。建议先遮住参考答案完成断句与翻译。

### 迁移练习 1

> A new problem has appeared: agencies that offer editing help to students sometimes advise clients to cite unrelated sources.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：A new problem / has appeared: agencies [that offer editing help to students] / sometimes advise clients to cite unrelated sources.

**训练句直译**：一个新问题出现了：为学生提供编辑帮助的机构，有时建议客户引用无关来源。

**训练句拆解**：冒号后 agencies 为主语，that 定语仅介绍业务；advise clients to cite 是真正行为。

### 迁移练习 2

> Another practice has spread: firms that provide technical support to publishers occasionally ask writers to add unnecessary links.

请先断句，指出主干、从句或非谓语的作用，再直译。

**参考答案**

**断句**：Another practice / has spread: firms [that provide technical support to publishers] / occasionally ask writers to add unnecessary links.

**训练句直译**：另一种做法传播开来：向出版商提供技术支持的公司，偶尔要求作者加入不必要的链接。

**训练句拆解**：that provide 修饰 firms；occasionally ask 主谓语，writers 是 to add 的逻辑主语。
