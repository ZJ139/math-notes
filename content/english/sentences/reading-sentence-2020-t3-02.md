## 原句

> Writing in The New Republic, Alice Lee notes that increasing the number of opportunities for board membership without increasing the pool of qualified women to serve on such boards has led to a “golden skirt” phenomenon, where the same elite women scoop up multiple seats on a variety of boards.

出处：2020年考研英语一阅读 Part A，Text 3。[查看原文上下文](/files/english/exams/2020英一.pdf#page=4)（PDF 第 4 页，以本句英文定位）。

## 句子拆解

### 断句

> Writing in The New Republic, / Alice Lee notes / that increasing the number of opportunities for board membership / without increasing the pool of qualified women to serve on such boards / has led to a “golden skirt” phenomenon, / where the same elite women scoop up multiple seats on a variety of boards.

“/”分隔意群，“//”分隔同层并列分句或独立分句；方括号标出嵌入的从句或修饰部分。断句不改变原句成分之间的归属。

### 主干读法

Alice Lee notes that increasing opportunities without increasing the pool has led to a phenomenon.

### 逐项分析

| 原句成分 | 结构、作用与内部关系 |
| --- | --- |
| Writing in The New Republic, | Writing 为现在分词状语，逻辑主语 Alice Lee；in The New Republic 表发表言论的刊物。 |
| Alice Lee notes | Alice Lee 是主句主语；notes 为谓语，意为指出。 |
| that increasing the number of opportunities for board membership | that 引导宾语从句；increasing…membership 为动名词主语；the number 是 increasing 的宾语；of opportunities 修饰 number；for board membership 修饰 opportunities。 |
| without increasing the pool of qualified women to serve on such boards | without 接 increasing 动名词，作伴随限制；the pool 为宾语，of qualified women 修饰 pool；to serve on such boards 修饰 women，说明她们能担任的职责，on 表在董事会任职。 |
| has led to a “golden skirt” phenomenon, | has led 为从句谓语，单数与整个 increasing 行为一致；to 为介词，后接 a…phenomenon；golden skirt 是对此现象的比喻命名。 |
| where the same elite women scoop up multiple seats on a variety of boards. | where 引导修饰 phenomenon 的关系从句，表“在这种现象中”；the same elite women 为主语，scoop up 为谓语，multiple seats 为宾语；on a variety of boards 说明这些职位分属不同董事会。 |

### 理解要点

不是说所有女性都受益，而是同一批精英女性获得多席。without 限定增加机会时未同步扩充合格人选。

## 句子直译

艾丽斯·李在《新共和》撰文指出，增加董事会任职机会却不增加能够在这些董事会任职的合格女性人选，已导致一种“金裙”现象：同一批精英女性包揽了多个不同董事会的许多席位。

## 原创拓展

以下两句为原创练习，不是历年试题原句。

### 迁移练习 1

> Writing in the school paper, Chen notes that increasing places without training more teachers has created a situation where classes become larger.

**练习要求**：给句子断句，标明主干与修饰关系，再作直译。

**参考答案**

断句：Writing in the school paper, / Chen notes / that increasing places without training more teachers / has created a situation / where classes become larger.

**训练句直译**：陈在校报撰文指出，增加学位而不培养更多教师，造成了班级规模扩大的局面。

**训练句拆解**：Writing 主语是 Chen；increasing 动名词作宾语从句主语；where 修饰 situation。

### 迁移练习 2

> Speaking at the meeting, Lin explains that adding projects without increasing staff has led to a pattern where the same workers take on every task.

**练习要求**：给句子断句，标明主干与修饰关系，再作直译。

**参考答案**

断句：Speaking at the meeting, / Lin explains / that adding projects without increasing staff / has led to a pattern / where the same workers take on every task.

**训练句直译**：林在会上解释说，增加项目却不增加人员，形成了由同一批工作人员承担所有任务的模式。

**训练句拆解**：Speaking 作状语；that 从句中 adding 为主语，has led 为谓语；where 引导对模式的说明。

### 考场价值

不是说所有女性都受益，而是同一批精英女性获得多席。without 限定增加机会时未同步扩充合格人选。
