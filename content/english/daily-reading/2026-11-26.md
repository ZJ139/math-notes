## 一、英文阅读

### The Right Trees in the Right Places

On a hot afternoon, walking from a bare street into the shade of a tree can bring immediate relief. It is therefore easy to assume that planting more trees will always make a city cooler. Trees certainly have an important role to play, but their effects are less simple than this assumption suggests. A review published in Communications Earth & Environment examined 182 studies covering 110 cities or regions and 17 types of climate. Its central message is that the benefits of trees depend on where and how they are planted.

Trees cool their surroundings in two main ways. Their leaves block sunlight, reducing the energy that reaches roads, walls and people. They also release water through their leaves, a process called transpiration, which removes heat. Yet the strength of these effects varies with local conditions. Dry air can encourage water loss and cooling, while very high temperatures and a shortage of water can cause trees to close the tiny openings in their leaves. A tree under stress may therefore provide less cooling than planners expect.

The shape of a neighbourhood matters as well. In an open area, a broad tree crown can shade surfaces that would otherwise receive direct sunlight. Between closely packed buildings, however, the same planting plan may have different results. Dense leaves can restrict the movement of air, and at night they may prevent some heat from escaping. This does not mean that crowded streets should have no trees. It means that the arrangement of trees must be considered together with the space between buildings and the paths along which air can move.

Choosing suitable species adds another layer to the problem. Trees differ in the size and shape of their leaves, their ability to cope with heat, and whether they keep their leaves throughout the year. In some climates, a mixture of species can provide useful shade across different seasons. In dry regions, resistance to drought becomes especially important. Rather than copying a successful project from another city, planners need to ask whether its trees and planting pattern suit their own climate. A design that works well in one place is not a universal recipe.

The practical lesson is to treat urban planting as a matter of careful design, not simply a race to increase tree numbers. Local climate, street layout and tree characteristics need to be considered together. Trees remain a valuable response to urban heat, but their benefits should be assessed under the conditions in which people actually live. Protecting and expanding urban greenery can make cities more comfortable; doing so effectively requires attention to the relationship between living plants and the spaces around them.

## 二、篇章解读

### 主旨与作者态度

**主旨：** 城市植树可以缓解高温，但降温效果受到气候、街区布局和树木特征的共同影响，规划必须因地制宜。英文标题 The Right Trees in the Right Places 同时强调“选什么树”与“种在哪里”。

**态度：** 肯定植树的价值，同时对“一多就好、到处照搬”的思路保持审慎。第 1 段的 certainly、第 3 段的 This does not mean 和末段的 remain a valuable response 共同说明，作者不是反对植树；but、however、yet 用来限定结论的适用条件。

### 逐段要点与逻辑关系

| 段落 | 内容要点 | 在论证中的作用 | 关键信号 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 第 1 段 | 从树荫下的舒适体验引出“多种树总能降温”的直觉，随后用研究提出条件 | 提出问题，亮出全文中心 | therefore；but；depend on |
| 第 2 段 | 解释遮阴与蒸腾两种机制，再指出缺水、高温会限制效果 | 先说明为什么有效，再说明何时受限 | two main ways；Yet；while |
| 第 3 段 | 比较开阔地带与建筑密集的街道，讨论通风和夜间散热 | 从气候推进到空间布局，并排除过度解读 | as well；however；This does not mean |
| 第 4 段 | 树种的叶片、耐热性、季节性与耐旱性不同 | 从“在哪里种”推进到“种什么”，反对机械照搬 | another layer；Rather than；whether |
| 第 5 段 | 把三类因素合并，提出应重视设计与实际效果 | 回扣中心并形成行动原则 | practical lesson；together；but |

**论证链：** 直观经验提出一般判断；研究提醒效果有条件；两种机制解释基本收益；气候、布局、树种解释收益为何不同；最后据此提出因地制宜的规划原则。

第 2—4 段属于并列因素展开，但各段内部又有因果或对比。阅读时可分别抓住“气候条件”“空间条件”“树种条件”，避免把它们读成三个彼此无关的例子。

### 证据与结论的边界

182 项研究、110 个城市或地区、17 类气候是原论文的综述范围，不是本网站自行开展的实验。正文没有把论文报道的某个最大降温值解释成所有城市都能达到的平均效果。夜间可能滞热也只是特定条件下的限制，不能推出“树木总体会使城市更热”。“反对只追求数量”是根据论文因地制宜原则作出的教学概括，不是研究中另行测量的指标。

## 三、长难句精读

### 1. 用 that 从句承接核心观点

> Its central message is that the benefits of trees depend on where and how they are planted.

**结构：** 主干为 Its central message is …；that 引导表语从句，说明 message 的具体内容。从句主干为 the benefits … depend on …；where and how they are planted 是介词 on 后面的嵌入疑问结构，表示种植地点与种植方式。they 指 trees。

**译文：** 这项研究的核心观点是：树木带来的益处取决于它们种在哪里以及如何种植。

**迁移：** The value of a method depends on how it is used. 方法的价值取决于使用方式。介词后用陈述语序 how it is used，不能写成 how is it used。

### 2. 定语从句中的 otherwise

> In an open area, a broad tree crown can shade surfaces that would otherwise receive direct sunlight.

**结构：** In an open area 是地点状语；主干为 a broad tree crown can shade surfaces；that 引导定语从句并作 receive 的主语，修饰 surfaces。otherwise 表示“若没有前面所说的遮阴”，would 用于描述这一假设下的结果。

**译文：** 在开阔地带，宽大的树冠可以遮蔽那些原本会受到阳光直射的表面。

**阅读要点：** otherwise 必须回扣上文的条件，不能仅译成孤立的“否则”。shade 此处是动词“遮蔽”，不是名词“阴凉处”。

### 3. whether 从句与并列主语

> Rather than copying a successful project from another city, planners need to ask whether its trees and planting pattern suit their own climate.

**结构：** Rather than copying … 提出不宜直接采用的做法；主干为 planners need to ask …；whether 引导宾语从句。该从句的主语为 its trees and planting pattern，谓语为 suit。its 指另一个城市的项目，their 指 planners。

**译文：** 规划者不应直接照搬另一个城市的成功项目，而需要问：该项目采用的树木和种植布局是否适合自己所在城市的气候。

**迁移：** We need to ask whether the plan suits local needs. 我们需要考虑这个方案是否适合当地需求。whether 表“是否”，不是“无论”；suit 在此意为“适合”，不能按名词“套装”理解。

## 五、读后检查

**1. 全文最合适的主旨是什么？**

A. All cities should plant the same kinds of trees.

B. Urban tree planting should respond to local conditions.

C. Trees usually make crowded streets hotter during the day.

**答案：B。** A 与第 4 段反对照搬的观点相反；C 把第 3 段特定情况下的通风及夜间散热问题扩大为白天的普遍结论。

**2. 第 3 段为什么专门补充 This does not mean …？**

它限制前面反例的推论范围：有些布局可能降低收益，不等于建筑密集的街道不该种树。其后的 It means … 给出作者真正认可的结论——把植树布局与建筑间距、通风路径一起考虑。

**3. 用一句话复述文章。**

Trees can reduce urban heat, but effective planting requires attention to local climate, street layout and suitable species.

**本期可迁移的方法：** 遇到“常见做法 + but/however + 若干条件”的文章，先区分作者是在否定做法，还是在限定效果。再将并列因素合并到主旨中，避免只用其中一个段落概括全文。
