## 小作文

主题：全球化国际会议志愿者招募通知。

### 审题与构思

以研究生会名义，为一场全球化主题的国际会议招募志愿者。通知应写申请者基本资格和其他必要信息。约 100 词；落款必须为 Postgraduates’ Association，不能用 Li Ming。

1. 通知格式：标题 Notice，正文直接说明为哪场会议招人；不写 Dear ...。
2. 主体信息：英语能力、合作能力、到岗时间三项条件，配上接待和会场服务职责、培训安排。
3. 行动入口：提交什么、交到哪里、何时截止、留下什么联系方式。6 月 12—14 日、6 月 1 日截止和地点、职责均为写作补充，先截止再开会，时间自洽。

### 范文

正文 **101 词**；连同称呼／标题和落款共 **104 词**。原题要求约 100 词。

> Notice
>
> Volunteers are needed for an international conference on globalization, which will take place at our university from June 12 to 14.
>
> Applicants should have a good command of English and be able to work well with others. They must also be available throughout the conference. Volunteers will welcome guests, provide information and help prepare meeting rooms. Previous experience is welcome, but training will be provided for everyone.
>
> Those who are interested should submit a brief personal introduction to the association office before June 1. Please include your phone number so that we can contact you. We look forward to your participation.
>
> Postgraduates' Association

### 可背句式

以下原句均出自上面的范文；骨架中的内容需按新题替换。

**1. Volunteers are needed for an international conference on globalization, which will take place at our university from June 12 to 14.**

含义：一场全球化主题国际会议需要志愿者，该会议将在本校于六月十二日至十四日举行。

可迁移骨架：Volunteers are needed for + 活动, which will take place + 时间地点.

用法：被动语态开门见山，which 从句补充会议信息。on globalization 表示会议主题，不能漏掉。

**2. Applicants should have a good command of English and be able to work well with others.**

含义：申请者应熟练掌握英语，并能与他人良好合作。

可迁移骨架：Applicants should have ... and be able to ...

用法：have 和 be 由 should 共同支配，都用原形。资格要求要与实际岗位有关，不必强行使用倒装。

**3. Those who are interested should submit a brief personal introduction to the association office before June 1.**

含义：有兴趣者应在六月一日前向研究生会办公室提交简短的个人介绍。

可迁移骨架：Those who ... should submit + 材料 + to + 地点 + before + 截止时间.

用法：those who 表示“……的人”，who 从句谓语用复数 are。整句可迁移到活动招募或报名通知。

### 词汇与搭配

| 表达 | 中文含义 | 背诵搭配与用法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| applicant | 申请者 | applicants should ... |
| have a good command of | 熟练掌握 | have a good command of English |
| be available | 有空；能够到岗 | be available throughout the conference |
| submit | 提交 | submit a brief personal introduction |
| participation | 参与 | look forward to your participation |

### 思路总结与易错点

这是一则能让读者完成报名的通知，应具备“活动—资格与职责—申请办法”的信息链。题干只要求约 100 词，不需要扩成大型招聘广告。最容易丢分的是误署 Li Ming 或把会议主题省略。

## 大作文

主题：文化交流与多样性。

### 审题与构思

图中以火锅容纳中国与外国的多种文化元素，例如功夫、京剧、莎士比亚。要求描述、解释寓意并评论，160—200 词。文化混合与交流是喻义，不能写成饮食营养说明。

1. 描图：概括“火锅里放着不同文化元素”，选择两三个看得清、好表达的例子即可。
2. 分析：交流让人接触新观念，也让别人理解中国；随后说明开放学习并不等于放弃本土传统，形成有分寸的论证。
3. 评论：从阅读、观影、跨文化交谈入手，强调好奇心、耐心和独立判断。读书与交谈是提出的行动，不是图中事实。

### 范文

全文 **179 词**，符合题目 160—200 词要求。三段依次完成描图、解释和评论。

> The drawing presents a large hot pot filled with cultural ingredients. Chinese kung fu and Beijing opera appear alongside Shakespeare and other elements from abroad. By placing them in one pot, the artist suggests that cultural exchange can enrich our lives.
>
> Different cultures offer different ways of understanding the world. When we read foreign literature or enjoy music from another country, we may discover ideas that broaden our view. Meanwhile, introducing Chinese traditions to others can deepen their understanding of China. It is through such exchanges that people learn to respect differences and recognize shared values. Cultural exchange does not mean accepting everything without thought or abandoning our own traditions. Instead, it encourages us to learn from others while preserving what makes our culture special.
>
> As young people, we can begin with books, films and conversations with people from different backgrounds. We should approach unfamiliar ideas with curiosity and explain our own customs with patience. Only by combining openness with independent judgment can we benefit fully from cultural exchange. A rich cultural life grows from both learning and sharing.

### 可背句式

以下原句均出自上面的范文；骨架中的内容需按新题替换。

**1. When we read foreign literature or enjoy music from another country, we may discover ideas that broaden our view.**

含义：阅读外国文学或欣赏他国音乐时，我们可能发现拓宽视野的思想。

可迁移骨架：When we ..., we may discover ... that ...

用法：that 从句修饰 ideas；用具体活动引出好处，比空写 broaden our view 更有说服力。

**2. It is through such exchanges that people learn to respect differences and recognize shared values.**

含义：正是通过这类交流，人们学会尊重差异并认识共同价值。

可迁移骨架：It is through + 方式 + that + 主语 + 谓语.

用法：强调 through such exchanges 这一方式；learn to 后的 respect 和 recognize 并列。

**3. Only by combining openness with independent judgment can we benefit fully from cultural exchange.**

含义：只有把开放态度与独立判断结合起来，我们才能充分受益于文化交流。

可迁移骨架：Only by combining A with B can we ...

用法：A、B 应写成同类语法成分，此处都是名词短语。can we benefit 是部分倒装。

### 词汇与搭配

| 表达 | 中文含义 | 背诵搭配与用法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cultural exchange | 文化交流 | benefit from cultural exchange |
| enrich our lives | 丰富生活 | enrich 比 make ... richer 更简练 |
| broaden our view | 拓宽视野 | ideas that broaden our view |
| preserve | 保留；保护 | preserve our traditions |
| independent judgment | 独立判断 | combine openness with independent judgment |
| ingredient | 原料；组成要素 | cultural ingredients；此处配合火锅比喻 |

### 思路总结与易错点

主线是“交流扩大认识—尊重差异—保持判断”。描图只需代表性元素，不必把原图上的每个专有名词翻译出来。结尾的“开放＋判断”使评论不止停留在文化交流很好。

## 练习与导航

先只看题意，用中文写出三段各自要完成的任务；再选两种熟悉的句式，用自己的内容写一遍。最后核对身份、要点、逻辑、语法和字数，不必在每篇里同时塞入全部复杂结构。
