## 小作文

主题：向校长建议改善学生体质。

### 审题与构思

给本校校长写信，建议怎样改善学生身体状况，并补充必要细节。约 100 词；称呼可用 Dear President，署名 Li Ming，不写地址。

1. 开头提出目的：改善学生体质；用学习时间长、运动少说明建议的出发点。这是合理情境补充，不引用虚构调查数字。
2. 建议由学校能执行的措施构成：延长运动设施开放时间；组织适合初学者的社团；提供饮食与睡眠讲座。
3. 结尾简要说明这些措施让健康选择更容易，并感谢校长考虑。具体安排为自拟建议，不是题面已给政策。

### 范文

正文 **102 词**；连同称呼／标题和落款共 **108 词**。原题要求约 100 词。

> Dear President,
>
> I am writing to suggest some ways to improve students' physical condition. Many students spend long hours studying and have little regular exercise, which may affect both their health and their studies.
>
> First, I suggest that the university extend the opening hours of sports facilities, so that students can exercise after evening classes. Second, more beginner-friendly sports clubs could be organized to encourage wider participation. Short workshops on healthy eating and sleep would also help students develop better habits. These measures would make healthy choices easier in daily life.
>
> I would appreciate your consideration of these suggestions. Thank you for your time.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> Li Ming

### 可背句式

以下原句均出自上面的范文；骨架中的内容需按新题替换。

**1. Many students spend long hours studying and have little regular exercise, which may affect both their health and their studies.**

含义：许多学生长时间学习、缺少规律运动，这可能影响健康和学业。

可迁移骨架：主语 + 情况, which may affect both A and B.

用法：which 在这里指前面整个情况，并非只修饰 exercise。用 may 留出合理余地，避免过度断言。

**2. First, I suggest that the university extend the opening hours of sports facilities, so that students can exercise after evening classes.**

含义：首先，我建议学校延长体育设施开放时间，以便学生在晚课后锻炼。

可迁移骨架：I suggest that + 主语 + (should) + 动词原形, so that ...

用法：表示建议时从句可省略 should，故用 extend 而非 extends；so that 说明建议的用途。

**3. These measures would make healthy choices easier in daily life.**

含义：这些措施会让学生在日常生活中更容易作出健康选择。

可迁移骨架：These measures would make + 宾语 + 形容词比较级.

用法：make + 宾语 + 宾语补足语；这里用 easier，不写 more easier。适合概括多项建议的共同效果。

### 词汇与搭配

| 表达 | 中文含义 | 背诵搭配与用法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| physical condition | 身体状况 | improve students’ physical condition |
| sports facilities | 体育设施 | extend the opening hours of sports facilities |
| participation | 参与 | encourage wider participation |
| workshop | 专题研习活动；小型讲座 | workshops on healthy eating |
| consideration | 考虑 | appreciate your consideration of these suggestions |

### 思路总结与易错点

给校长的建议应落在学校可实施的安排上，不能整封信只劝学生“多运动”。每条措施补一个用途，形成“困难—措施—好处”的短链条；不编造百分比来制造说服力。

## 大作文

主题：亲情相携与关爱父母。

### 审题与构思

两幅图相隔三十年：过去母亲牵年幼的女儿，现在成年女儿扶年迈的母亲。要求描述、解释寓意并评论，160—200 词。要写出照顾角色的转换，以及成年子女对父母的关爱。

1. 描图：按先后时间写两幅画，点明母女身份和互相搀扶；用“角色变了，亲情仍在”概括。
2. 分析：父母曾付出关爱，年老后也需要支持；用交谈、探望、日常帮助说明关爱不只等于给钱。
3. 评论：即使忙碌也安排时间，倾听并实际帮助，同时尊重父母选择。探望与电话等日常行动属于论证补充，不是图画附加细节。

### 范文

全文 **182 词**，符合题目 160—200 词要求。三段依次完成描图、解释和评论。

> The two pictures show a mother and her daughter thirty years apart. In the first, the mother holds her little daughter's hand. In the second, the grown daughter supports her elderly mother as they walk together. Time has changed their roles, but their close bond remains.
>
> The pictures suggest that the care we receive should inspire us to care for others. Parents devote years to helping children grow, and they may need support as they become older. Such support involves more than sending money. A patient conversation, a regular visit or help with daily tasks can make a real difference. It is our steady attention that makes parents feel valued. Those who once guided us through childhood deserve understanding and respect when their own lives become more difficult.
>
> As young adults, we should make time for our parents even when work or study keeps us busy. We can listen to their concerns and offer practical help while respecting their choices. Only when love is expressed through everyday actions can it become lasting support. Caring for family begins with what we do today.

### 可背句式

以下原句均出自上面的范文；骨架中的内容需按新题替换。

**1. It is our steady attention that makes parents feel valued.**

含义：正是我们持续的关注，让父母感到受到重视。

可迁移骨架：It is + 具体行动 / 品质 + that makes someone feel + 形容词 / 过去分词.

用法：强调 steady attention；makes 后的 feel 用原形。valued 在此表示“受到重视的”。

**2. Those who once guided us through childhood deserve understanding and respect when their own lives become more difficult.**

含义：那些曾引导我们度过童年的人，在生活变得更困难时，理应得到理解与尊重。

可迁移骨架：Those who once ... deserve ... when ...

用法：who 从句修饰 Those，deserve 为主句谓语，when 从句补充时机。别被较长的主语干扰而漏掉 deserve。

**3. Only when love is expressed through everyday actions can it become lasting support.**

含义：只有通过日常行动表达出来，爱才能成为持久的支持。

可迁移骨架：Only when + 抽象品质 + is expressed through + 行动 + can it ...

用法：用具体行动承接抽象主题，主句 can it become 部分倒装；when 从句正常语序。

### 词汇与搭配

| 表达 | 中文含义 | 背诵搭配与用法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| elderly | 年老的 | her elderly mother；比 old 更礼貌 |
| devote ... to ... | 把……投入…… | devote years to helping children grow；to 为介词 |
| involve | 包含；涉及 | support involves more than sending money |
| feel valued | 感到被重视 | make parents feel valued |
| practical help | 切实的帮助 | offer practical help |
| respect their choices | 尊重他们的选择 | help while respecting their choices |

### 思路总结与易错点

由母女“角色互换”推出“关爱延续”，再用日常行动说明怎样落实。避免只赞美母爱而不写成年子女的责任，也不要把照顾父母写成替父母决定一切。

## 练习与导航
