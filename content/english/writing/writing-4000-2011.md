## 小作文

主题：向朋友推荐电影。

### 审题与构思

给朋友写信，推荐自己最喜欢的一部电影，并说明推荐理由。约 100 词；署名 Li Ming，不写地址。电影由考生自行选择。本文选用 The Pursuit of Happyness（《当幸福来敲门》），朋友名 Tom 为合理补充。

1. 开头：直接说推荐哪一部电影，用一句话交代父子努力改善生活的故事，不复述完整剧情。
2. 理由：一是面对困难的毅力，二是温暖的父子关系；再说明朋友在灰心时看这部片会得到什么启发。
3. 结尾：建议周末观看并交流感想，保持朋友间的自然语气。周末观看是自拟建议。

### 范文

正文 **101 词**；连同称呼／标题和落款共 **106 词**。原题要求约 100 词。

> Dear Tom,
>
> I would like to recommend The Pursuit of Happyness, one of my favorite films. It tells the story of a father who struggles to build a better life for himself and his young son.
>
> What moves me most is his determination to keep trying despite repeated difficulties. The relationship between father and son is also warm and touching. Not only does the film offer an inspiring story, but it also reminds us to value our families. I think you will enjoy it, especially when you feel discouraged.
>
> Why not watch it this weekend? I would love to hear your thoughts afterwards.
>
> Yours,
> Li Ming

### 可背句式

以下原句均出自上面的范文；骨架中的内容需按新题替换。

**1. It tells the story of a father who struggles to build a better life for himself and his young son.**

含义：电影讲述一位父亲努力为自己和年幼的儿子创造更好生活的故事。

可迁移骨架：It tells the story of a person who ...

用法：who 从句修饰 father，适合简述电影、小说内容。struggles 与单数 father 一致。

**2. What moves me most is his determination to keep trying despite repeated difficulties.**

含义：最打动我的是他面对重重困难仍继续尝试的决心。

可迁移骨架：What moves / impresses me most is + 名词短语.

用法：What moves me most 是主语从句，整体按单数用 is；despite 后接名词短语，不直接接完整从句。

**3. Not only does the film offer an inspiring story, but it also reminds us to value our families.**

含义：电影不仅呈现励志的故事，也提醒我们珍惜家人。

可迁移骨架：Not only does + 单数主语 + 动词原形, but it also + 谓语.

用法：Not only 置于分句开头时，前半句部分倒装；does 后用 offer，后半句正常语序用 reminds。两个优点必须真实不同。

### 词汇与搭配

| 表达 | 中文含义 | 背诵搭配与用法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| recommend | 推荐 | recommend a film to a friend；记住 recommend A to B |
| determination | 决心；毅力 | determination to keep trying |
| inspiring | 鼓舞人心的 | an inspiring story |
| touching | 感人的 | a warm and touching relationship |
| discouraged | 灰心的 | feel discouraged；描述人的感受 |

### 思路总结与易错点

电影简介服务于推荐理由，不能让剧情占满全文。可背“推荐对象—两个理由—对收信人的价值—观看建议”。片名中的 Happyness 是影片原名的特殊拼写，不应自行改成 Happiness；平时写普通名词仍用 happiness。

## 大作文

主题：文明旅游与环境责任。

### 审题与构思

画面中游客坐船游览，船边水面漂浮着垃圾，突出旅途留下的污染。要求描述图画、解释寓意并评论，160—200 词。主题是文明旅游和环境责任，不能把垃圾说成图中未显示的工业排放。

1. 描图：用游客的快乐与水面的污染构成反差，快速落到不负责任的旅游行为。
2. 分析：解释“一人觉得少量无害—多人累计造成问题—景观与生态受损”的过程，说明每个人都与环境相关。
3. 评论：个人带走垃圾、少用一次性用品；景区提供垃圾桶、指导和提醒。两类主体各承担具体责任。动物受影响是论证推演，不是图中直接画出的事实。

### 范文

全文 **184 词**，符合题目 160—200 词要求。三段依次完成描图、解释和评论。

> In the picture, tourists are enjoying a boat trip while rubbish floats on the water around them. The contrast between their pleasure and the polluted surroundings draws attention to the environmental cost of careless behavior. What should be a pleasant journey has left an unpleasant mark.
>
> The drawing reminds us that enjoying nature carries a responsibility to protect it. Some travelers may think that a single bottle or plastic bag does little harm. However, when many visitors act in this way, small amounts of rubbish become a serious problem. Waste spoils the view and may also threaten animals living in the water. A clean environment, which every visitor hopes to enjoy, depends on everyone's daily choices. Convenience cannot be an excuse for leaving waste behind.
>
> To improve the situation, tourists should carry their rubbish away and use fewer disposable products. Scenic areas should provide convenient bins and clear guidance, while staff should remind visitors to follow the rules. Only when personal responsibility is supported by effective management can tourism develop sustainably. We should leave beautiful memories of our journeys, rather than piles of waste.

### 可背句式

以下原句均出自上面的范文；骨架中的内容需按新题替换。

**1. What should be a pleasant journey has left an unpleasant mark.**

含义：本应愉快的旅程，却留下了令人不快的痕迹。

可迁移骨架：What should be + 正面名词短语 + 谓语 + 负面结果.

用法：What should be a pleasant journey 是主语从句，has 与其一致。pleasant / unpleasant 的对照呼应画面的反差。

**2. A clean environment, which every visitor hopes to enjoy, depends on everyone's daily choices.**

含义：每位游客都希望享受的干净环境，取决于每个人日常的选择。

可迁移骨架：名词, which + 补充说明, + 谓语.

用法：去掉逗号间从句，主干仍是 A clean environment depends ...；which 在从句中作 enjoy 的宾语。

**3. Only when personal responsibility is supported by effective management can tourism develop sustainably.**

含义：只有个人责任得到有效管理的支持，旅游业才能持续发展。

可迁移骨架：Only when A is supported by B can C ...

用法：主句 can tourism develop 倒装；从句 is supported 不倒装。用制度支持个人行动，使结尾不只有道德呼吁。

### 词汇与搭配

| 表达 | 中文含义 | 背诵搭配与用法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| environmental cost | 环境代价 | the environmental cost of careless behavior |
| spoil the view | 破坏景观 | spoil 作动词，比 destroy the scenery 语气更合适 |
| disposable products | 一次性用品 | use fewer disposable products；可改为 products used only once |
| effective management | 有效管理 | supported by effective management |
| sustainably | 以可持续的方式 | develop sustainably；可改为 develop without harming nature |

### 思路总结与易错点

从“不文明行为”写到“累计后果”，再写个人与管理者的解决办法，逻辑比笼统说环保重要更完整。倒装句保留一个即可；重点是措施与前面的原因对应。
