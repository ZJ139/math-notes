## 小作文

主题：邀请外教担任英语演讲比赛评委。

### 审题与构思

给本校一位外籍教师写邮件，邀请其担任即将举行的英语演讲比赛评委，并补充必要信息。约 100 词；署名 Li Ming，不写地址。Professor Smith 是为称呼补充的姓名。

1. 邀请目的：第一句说明“邀请您担任评委”，不能只邀请对方来观看。
2. 必要安排：时间、地点、比赛主题；再写每人演讲时长、问答安排和评委职责，让对方能判断是否参加。
3. 结尾：请求周三前回复是否出席，为周五活动留出准备时间。全部具体时间、主题和赛制均为合理自拟；不写题干未提供的真实联系方式。

### 范文

正文 **102 词**；连同称呼／标题和落款共 **109 词**。原题要求约 100 词。

> Dear Professor Smith,
>
> I am writing to invite you to serve as a judge for our upcoming English speech contest. The event will take place in the university hall from two to five next Friday afternoon.
>
> The theme is My University Life. Each contestant will give a five-minute speech, followed by a short question period. We would be grateful if you could assess the speeches and offer some advice to the participants. Your experience in English teaching would be of great value to them.
>
> Please let us know by Wednesday whether you will be able to attend. We sincerely hope you will accept our invitation.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> Li Ming

### 可背句式

以下原句均出自上面的范文；骨架中的内容需按新题替换。

**1. I am writing to invite you to serve as a judge for our upcoming English speech contest.**

含义：我写信邀请您担任即将举行的英语演讲比赛评委。

可迁移骨架：I am writing to invite you to serve as + 身份 + for + 活动.

用法：serve as 表示担任；明确受邀者的工作，比泛泛的 invite you to our contest 更完整。

**2. We would be grateful if you could assess the speeches and offer some advice to the participants.**

含义：如果您能评价演讲并向参赛者提出建议，我们将十分感激。

可迁移骨架：We would be grateful if you could + 动词原形.

用法：用于礼貌请求；assess 和 offer 并列。advice 不可数，写 some advice，不写 advices。

**3. Your experience in English teaching would be of great value to them.**

含义：您的英语教学经验将对他们很有价值。

可迁移骨架：Your experience in + 领域 + would be of great value to + 对象.

用法：be of great value 相当于 be very valuable，适度提升表达；them 指 participants，不另引入新对象。

### 词汇与搭配

| 表达 | 中文含义 | 背诵搭配与用法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| serve as | 担任 | serve as a judge |
| upcoming | 即将举行的 | the upcoming contest |
| contestant | 参赛者 | each contestant will give a speech |
| assess | 评价；评定 | assess the speeches；也可用 judge the speeches |
| be of great value | 很有价值 | be of great value to the participants |

### 思路总结与易错点

邀请信的核心是让对方知道“邀请我做什么、什么时候做、要承担什么工作、怎样回复”。内容自洽比使用复杂的客套话更重要；提供比赛细节时不要挤掉“担任评委”这一核心任务。

## 大作文

主题：毕业选择与自主规划。

### 审题与构思

毕业生站在人生路口，面前有就业、考研、出国和创业等方向。要求简述画面、解释寓意并评论，160—200 词。讨论怎样选择适合自己的方向，不把某一条路写成所有人唯一正确的答案。

1. 描图：交代毕业生、路口和四种方向，用“人生转折点”引出选择主题。
2. 分析：提出兴趣、能力、实际处境三个判断标准；用喜欢研究和希望实践两类学生说明差异，再指出盲从的问题。
3. 评论：搜集信息、认识优势、设定现实目标；选择后持续努力，并根据情况调整。例子和建议均是原创发挥。

### 范文

全文 **179 词**，符合题目 160—200 词要求。三段依次完成描图、解释和评论。

> The drawing shows a group of graduates facing several paths. The signs point towards employment, further study, studying abroad and starting a business. Standing at this turning point, the young people must decide which direction to take. The picture captures a choice that many students eventually face.
>
> There is no single path that suits everyone. A wise decision should reflect one's interests, abilities and actual circumstances. For example, a student who enjoys research may benefit from further study, while someone eager to gain practical experience may prefer employment. Following the most popular choice without careful thought can lead to disappointment. What matters most is whether a path offers suitable opportunities for growth. Advice from teachers and family can be useful, but the final decision requires personal judgment and responsibility.
>
> Therefore, graduates should gather reliable information, examine their strengths and set realistic goals before making a choice. Once a direction is chosen, they should work steadily while remaining willing to adjust their plans. Only by combining thoughtful decisions with consistent effort can they build a future that truly fits them.

### 可背句式

以下原句均出自上面的范文；骨架中的内容需按新题替换。

**1. There is no single path that suits everyone.**

含义：没有一条道路适合所有人。

可迁移骨架：There is no single + 方法 / 道路 + that suits everyone.

用法：that 从句修饰 path；单数 path 对应 suits。适用于选择、学习方法、职业规划等主题。

**2. What matters most is whether a path offers suitable opportunities for growth.**

含义：最重要的是，这条道路是否提供适合自己的成长机会。

可迁移骨架：What matters most is whether + 主语 + 谓语.

用法：What matters most 是主语从句；whether 从句作表语。whether 后用陈述语序，不写 whether does a path offer。

**3. Only by combining thoughtful decisions with consistent effort can they build a future that truly fits them.**

含义：只有把深思熟虑的决定与持续的努力结合起来，他们才能创造适合自己的未来。

可迁移骨架：Only by combining A with B can people ... that ...

用法：主句倒装，that 从句修饰 future。A、B 在本题对应“选择＋行动”，不能只讨论选哪条路而忽略后续努力。

### 词汇与搭配

| 表达 | 中文含义 | 背诵搭配与用法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| turning point | 转折点 | at this turning point |
| actual circumstances | 实际处境 | reflect one’s actual circumstances |
| personal judgment | 个人判断 | require personal judgment |
| reliable information | 可靠信息 | gather reliable information |
| realistic goals | 切合实际的目标 | set realistic goals |
| consistent effort | 持续的努力 | combine decisions with consistent effort |

### 思路总结与易错点

把“选择很多”推进到“依据什么选”和“选后怎样做”。全文不预测某条道路收入更高，也不把流行选择等同于适合自己；用匹配个人情况的标准建立论证。

## 练习与导航

先只看题意，用中文写出三段各自要完成的任务；再选两种熟悉的句式，用自己的内容写一遍。最后核对身份、要点、逻辑、语法和字数，不必在每篇里同时塞入全部复杂结构。
