## Text 1

报纸艺术评论的衰落

本篇词汇取自阅读正文；按学习价值筛选，词义以本文语境为准。

**一、核心词与重要表达**

| 词语 | 词性与文中义 | 原文搭配 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| inexorable | adj. 不可阻挡的 | inexorable decline |
| scope | n. 范围 | the scope and seriousness |
| criticism | n. 评论；批评 | high-quality arts criticism |
| considerable | adj. 相当大的 | a considerable number |
| marvel | v. 感到惊叹 | marvel at the fact |
| deem | v. 认为；视为 | deemed suitable |
| ornament | n. 装饰；增光之物 | an ornament to the publications |
| contempt | n. 轻蔑 | a term of contempt |
| virtually | adv. 几乎 | virtually forgotten |
| foremost | adj. 最重要的；最杰出的 | foremost classical-music critics |
| revival | n. 复兴；重新流行 | enjoy a revival |
| prospect | n. 前景；可能性 | The prospect seems remote |
| retreat | n. 退却；衰退 | in headlong retreat |
| at length | phr. 详细地；长篇地 | write in detail and at length |

| 词语 | 常见义 | 本篇用法 | 原文搭配 |

| coverage | 覆盖范围 | 新闻报道 | arts coverage |
| calling | 呼叫 | 使命；天职 | journalism as a calling |
| press | 按压 | 报界；报刊 | the daily press |
| body | 身体 | 一整批；全部作品 | his vast body of writings |
| save | 拯救；节省 | 除⋯⋯以外（介词） | unknown save to specialists |
| remote | 遥远的 | 可能性很小的 | The prospect seems remote |
