## Text 1

| indictment | n. 控诉；谴责 | indictment of fast fashion |

| turnaround | n. 周转；完成周期 | Quicker turnarounds |
| inventory | n. 库存 | wasted inventory |

| wardrobe | n. 衣橱；全部衣物 | renew their wardrobe |

| massive | adj. 大量的；巨大的 | massive amounts |
| durable | adj. 耐用的 | non-durable and wasteful |
| excess | n. 过量 | this excess leads to waste |

| vanity | n. 虚荣心 | Vanity is a constant |
