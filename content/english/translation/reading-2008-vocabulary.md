## Text 1

压力反应中的性别差异

本篇词汇取自阅读正文；按学习价值筛选，词义以本文语境为准。

**一、核心词与重要表达**

| 词语 | 词性与文中义 | 原文搭配 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| susceptible | adj. 易受影响的；易患病的 | susceptible to developing depression |
| depression | n. 抑郁（症） | depression and anxiety disorders |
| anxiety | n. 焦虑 | anxiety disorders |
| hormone | n. 激素 | sex hormones |
| trigger | adj./n. 起触发作用的；诱因 | trigger chemicals |
| capacity | n. 能力；容量 | capacity for tolerating stress |
| tolerate | v. 忍受；承受 | tolerating stress |
| chronic | adj. 长期的；慢性的 | a chronic or repeated nature |
| domestic | adj. 家庭的；国内的 | domestic situations |
| devastating | adj. 破坏性极强的 | quite devastating |
| frustration | n. 挫折；沮丧 | so much frustration |
| obligation | n. 责任；义务 | a lot of obligations |
| cope with | phr. 应对；处理 | so much more to cope with |
| wear out | phr. 使精疲力竭 | become worn out |

**二、熟词僻义与易误读搭配**

常见义用于对照；本篇应按右侧语境理解。

| 词语 | 常见义 | 本篇用法 | 原文搭配 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| strain | 拉紧；扭伤 | 精神压力；负担 | feeling the strain |
| break | 打破 | 短暂休息；喘息机会 | with few breaks |
| nature | 自然界 | 性质；特点 | of a chronic or repeated nature |
| function | 功能 | 正常运作；正常生活（动词） | your ability to function |
| expose | 揭露 | 使遭受；使接触到 | are exposed to combat stress |
| observe | 观察 | 说；评论；指出 | she observes |
