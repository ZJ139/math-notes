## Text 1

失业救济改革背后的预设

| --- | --- | --- |
| dependency | n. 依赖 | reduce dependency |
| scheme | n. 方案；计划 | upfront work search scheme |
| eligible | adj. 有资格的 | eligible for benefit |
| fortnightly | adv. 每两周一次 | rather than fortnightly |
| allowance | n. 津贴 | jobseeker’s allowance |
| indulgent | adj. 纵容的 | an obviously indulgent system |
| subsidise | v. 补贴；资助 | subsidises laziness |
| zeal | n. 热忱 | his zeal for fundamental fairness |
| claimant | n. 申请领取者 | deserving claimants |
| exclude | v. 排除 | excluded from the work environment |
| crucial | adj. 至关重要的 | the crucial income |
| unconditional | adj. 无条件的 | unconditional payments |
| contribution | n. 缴款；贡献 | national insurance contributions |
| entitlement | n. 应得的权利 | no entitlement |

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| benefit | 好处 | 福利金；救济金 | eligible for benefit |
| sign on | 签名加入 | 登记领取失业救济 | looking to sign on |
| skip | 跳过 | 轻快地蹦跳着走 | skip down to the jobcentre |
| structure | 建筑结构 | 生活的秩序和安排 | offers purpose and structure |
| only too | 只有；也 | 非常；过于 | only too ready to indulge |
| conditional on | 条件句 | 以⋯⋯为条件 | conditional on actively seeking a job |
