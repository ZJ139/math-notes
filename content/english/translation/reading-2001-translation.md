## Text 1

**第 1 段**

Specialisation can be seen as a response to the problem of an increasing accumulation of scientific knowledge. By splitting up the subject matter into smaller units, one man could continue to handle the information and use it as the basis for further research. But specialisation was only one of a series of related developments in science affecting the process of communication. Another was the growing professionalisation of scientific activity.

**直译**　专业分工可以被看作对科学知识不断积累这一问题的回应。把研究内容划分成更小的单元，一个人就仍然能够处理这些信息，并把它们用作进一步研究的基础。但专业分工只是科学中一系列影响交流过程的相关发展之一。另一项发展，是科学活动日益职业化。

**第 2 段**

No clear-cut distinction can be drawn between professionals and amateurs in science: exceptions can be found to any rule. Nevertheless, the word “amateur” does carry a connotation that the person concerned is not fully integrated into the scientific community and, in particular, may not fully share its values. The growth of specialisation in the nineteenth century, with its consequent requirement of a longer, more complex training, implied greater problems for amateur participation in science. The trend was naturally most obvious in those areas of science based especially on a mathematical or laboratory training, and can be illustrated in terms of the development of geology in the United Kingdom.

**直译**　在科学领域，专业人员与业余爱好者之间无法划出明确的界线：任何规则都能找到例外。不过，“业余爱好者” 一词确实带有一种含义，即有关人员没有完全融入科学共同体，尤其可能没有完全认同其价值观。19 世纪专业分工的发展，随之要求更长期、更复杂的训练，这意味着业余爱好者参与科学会遇到更多问题。这一趋势自然在那些特别依赖数学或实验室训练的科学领域最明显，可以借英国地质学的发展加以说明。

**第 3 段**

A comparison of British geological publications over the last century and a half reveals not simply an increasing emphasis on the primacy of research, but also a changing definition of what constitutes an acceptable research paper. Thus, in the nineteenth century, local geological studies represented worthwhile research in their own right; but, in the twentieth century, local studies have increasingly become acceptable to professionals only if they incorporate, and reflect on, the wider geological picture. Amateurs, on the other hand, have continued to pursue local studies in the old way. The overall result has been to make entrance to professional geological journals harder for amateurs, a result that has been reinforced by the widespread introduction of refereeing, first by national journals in the nineteenth century and then by several local geological journals in the twentieth century.

**直译**　比较过去一个半世纪英国的地质学出版物，就能发现，人们不仅越来越强调研究的首要地位，而且对什么构成可接受的研究论文，定义也在变化。因此，在19 世纪，地方地质研究本身就被认为是有价值的研究；但在20 世纪，专业人员越来越只接受那些纳入并思考更广泛地质图景的地方研究。另一方面，业余爱好者仍以旧方式进行地方研究。总体结果是，业余爱好者更难进入专业地质学期刊发表作品；审稿制度的广泛引入又强化了这一结果，该制度先由19 世纪的全国性期刊采用，随后由20 世纪的一些地方地质学期刊采用。

**第 4 段**

As a logical consequence of this development, separate journals have now appeared aimed mainly towards either professional or amateur readership. A rather similar process of differentiation has led to professional geologists coming together nationally within one or two specific societies, whereas the amateurs have tended either to remain in local societies or to come together nationally in a different way.

**直译**　这一发展的合乎逻辑的后果是，如今出现了分别主要面向专业读者或业余读者的期刊。一种相当类似的分化过程，使专业地质学家在全国范围内聚集到一两个专门学会中，而业余爱好者往往要么留在地方学会，要么以不同的方式在全国范围内组织起来。

**第 5 段**

Although the process of professionalisation and specialisation was already well under way in British geology during the nineteenth century, its full consequences were thus delayed until the twentieth century. In science generally, however, the nineteenth century must be reckoned as the crucial period for this change in the structure of science.

**直译**　因此，虽然英国地质学的职业化和专业分工过程在19 世纪已取得相当进展，其全部后果却直到20 世纪才显现出来。不过，就整个科学而言，19 世纪必须被视为科学结构发生这种变化的关键时期。篇章简析主旨：专业分工和职业化提高训练与发表门槛，使专业研究者与业余爱好者逐渐分流。第3 段not simply ... but also ... 并列两种变化；末段区分过程开始与后果充分显现的时间。specialisation 指研究分工，professionalisation 指职业化，译文分别处理。

## Text 2

**第 1 段**

A great deal of attention is being paid today to the so-called digital divide — the division of the world into the info (information) rich and the info poor. And that divide does exist today. My wife and I lectured about this looming danger twenty years ago. What was less visible then, however, were the new, positive forces that work against the digital divide. There are reasons to be optimistic.

**直译**　今天，人们正大量关注所谓的数字鸿沟，即世界被划分为信息富有者与信息贫困者。而这一鸿沟今天确实存在。二十年前，我和妻子就曾就这一逐渐逼近的危险作过演讲。然而，当时不那么显眼的是那些对抗数字鸿沟的新的积极力量。我们有理由感到乐观。

**第 2 段**

There are technological reasons to hope the digital divide will narrow. As the Internet becomes more and more commercialized, it is in the interest of business to universalize access — after all, the more people online, the more potential customers there are. More and more governments, afraid their countries will be left behind, want to spread Internet access. Within the next decade or two, one to two billion people on the planet will be netted together. As a result, I now believe the digital divide will narrow rather than widen in the years ahead. And that is very good news because the Internet may well be the most powerful tool for combating world poverty that we’ve ever had.

**直译**　从技术上说，我们有理由希望数字鸿沟会缩小。随着互联网越来越商业化，让人人都能接入网络符合企业利益；毕竟，上网的人越多，潜在顾客就越多。越来越多的政府担心本国被落在后面，希望普及互联网接入。在未来一二十年内，全球将有十亿至二十亿人通过网络连接起来。因此，我现在相信，未来几年数字鸿沟会缩小，而不是扩大。这是非常好的消息，因为互联网很可能是我们有史以来拥有的最强大的抗击世界贫困的工具。

**第 3 段**

Of course, the use of the Internet isn’t the only way to defeat poverty. And the Internet is not the only tool we have. But it has enormous potential.

**直译**　当然，使用互联网不是战胜贫困的唯一方法，互联网也不是我们拥有的唯一工具。但它具有巨大的潜力。

**第 4 段**

To take advantage of this tool, some impoverished countries will have to get over their outdated anti-colonial prejudices with respect to foreign investment. Countries that still think foreign investment is an invasion of their sovereignty might well study the history of infrastructure (the basic structural foundations of a society) in the United States. When the United States built its industrial infrastructure, it didn’t have the capital to do so. And that is why America’s Second Wave infrastructure-including roads, harbors, highways, ports and so on-were built with foreign investment. The English, the Germans, the Dutch and the French were investing in Britain’s former colony. They financed them. Immigrant Americans built them. Guess who owns them now? The Americans. I believe the same thing would be true in places like Brazil or anywhere else for that matter. The more foreign capital you have helping you build your Third Wave infrastructure, which today is an electronic infrastructure, the better off you’re going to be. That doesn’t mean lying down and becoming fooled, or letting foreign corporations run uncontrolled. But it does mean recognizing how important they can be in building the energy and telecom infrastructures needed to take full advantage of the Internet.

**直译**　为了利用这个工具，一些贫困国家必须克服其在外国投资问题上过时的反殖民偏见。那些仍把外国投资看作对本国主权侵犯的国家，不妨研究一下美国基础设施—— 即一个社会的基本结构基础—— 的历史。美国建设工业基础设施时，没有足够资本。因此，美国第二次浪潮的基础设施，包括道路、港湾、公路、港口等，是用外国投资建设的。英国人、德国人、荷兰人和法国人都在这个昔日的英国殖民地投资。他们提供资金，移民到美国的人负责建设。猜猜现在谁拥有它们？美国人。我相信，在巴西这样的地方，或就这一点而言在其他任何地方，也会如此。帮助你建设第三次浪潮基础设施—— 如今即电子基础设施—— 的外国资本越多，你就会越富裕。这并不意味着躺倒任人欺骗，或让外国公司不受控制地经营。但它确实意味着，必须认识到它们在建设能源与电信基础设施方面能有多么重要，而这些设施是充分利用互联网所必需的。篇章简析主旨：作者对缩小数字鸿沟持审慎乐观态度，强调商业、技术和外资建设基础设施的作用。第4 段以美国历史作类比；That doesn’t mean ... But it does mean ... 明确保留对外资的监管边界。文中关于未来联网规模的说法是当时预测。

## Text 3

**第 1 段**

Why do so many Americans distrust what they read in their newspapers? The American Society of Newspaper Editors is trying to answer this painful question. The organization is deep into a long self-analysis known as the journalism credibility project.

**直译**　为什么有那么多美国人不信任自己在报纸上读到的内容？美国报纸编辑协会正试图回答这个令人痛苦的问题。该组织正深入进行一项长期的自我分析，称为新闻可信度项目。

**第 2 段**

Sad to say, this project has turned out to be mostly low-level findings about factual errors and spelling and grammar mistakes, combined with lots of head-scratching puzzlement about what in the world those readers really want.

**直译**　遗憾的是，这个项目的成果大多只是关于事实错误、拼写和语法错误的浅层发现，同时还充满了让人挠头的困惑：那些读者究竟真正想要什么？

**第 3 段**

But the sources of distrust go way deeper. Most journalists learn to see the world through a set of standard templates (patterns) into which they plug each day’s events. In other words, there is a conventional story line in the newsroom culture that provides a backbone and a ready-made narrative structure for otherwise confusing news.

**直译**　但不信任的根源要深刻得多。大多数记者学会通过一套标准模板看世界，把每天发生的事件塞进模板中。换句话说，新闻编辑室文化中存在一种惯常的故事线索，为本来会显得混乱的新闻提供骨架和现成的叙事结构。

**第 4 段**

There exists a social and cultural disconnect between journalists and their readers which helps explain why the “standard templates” of the newsroom seem alien to many readers. In a recent survey, questionnaires were sent to reporters in five middle size cities around the country, plus one large metropolitan area. Then residents in these communities were phoned at random and asked the same questions.

**直译**　记者与读者之间存在社会和文化上的脱节，这有助于解释，为什么编辑室的“标准模板” 令许多读者感到陌生。在最近一项调查中，问卷被寄给全国五个中等城市及一个大都市地区的记者。随后，研究者随机打电话给这些社区的居民，问了同样的问题。

**第 5 段**

Replies show that compared with other Americans, journalists are more likely to live in upscale neighborhoods, have maids, own Mercedeses, and trade stocks, and they’re less likely to go to church, do volunteer work, or put down roots in community.

**直译**　回答显示，与其他美国人相比，记者更可能住在高档社区、雇用家政人员、拥有奔驰车和买卖股票；他们则更少去教堂、做志愿工作或在社区中扎根。

**第 6 段**

Reporters tend to be part of a broadly defined social and cultural elite, so their work tends to reflect the conventional values of this elite. The astonishing distrust of the news media isn’t rooted in inaccuracy or poor reportorial skills but in the daily clash of world views between reporters and their readers.

**直译**　记者往往属于广义的社会文化精英，因此他们的工作往往反映这一精英阶层的惯常价值观。人们对新闻媒体惊人程度的不信任，并非根源于报道不准确或技能低下，而是根源于记者与读者之间日常的世界观冲突。

**第 7 段**

This is an explosive situation for any industry, particularly a declining one. Here is a troubled business that keeps hiring employees whose attitudes vastly annoy the customers. Then it sponsors lots of symposiums and a credibility project dedicated to wondering why customers are annoyed and fleeing in large numbers. But it never seems to get around to noticing the cultural and class biases that so many former buyers are complaining about. If it did, it would open up its diversity program, now focused narrowly on race and gender, and look for reporters who differ broadly by outlook, values, education, and class.

**直译**　对任何行业而言，这都是一触即发的局面，对一个正在衰落的行业尤其如此。这里有一家陷入困境的企业，却不断雇用态度令顾客极为恼火的员工。然后，它资助大量研讨会和一个可信度项目，专门研究顾客为什么恼火并大批离开。但它似乎始终没有顾得上注意，许多昔日购买者所抱怨的文化和阶级偏见。如果注意到了，它就会扩大目前仅狭窄地关注种族与性别的多样性计划，寻找在观点、价值观、教育和阶级方面都有广泛差异的记者。篇章简析主旨：作者认为新闻不信任主要源于记者与读者的社会文化差距，而非单纯技术错误。结构：批评调查停留表层— 解释新闻模板— 列调查证据— 提出扩展多样性的建议。末句If it did, it would ... 是对未实行改进的假设。

## Text 4

**第 1 段**

The world is going through the biggest wave of mergers and acquisitions ever witnessed. The process sweeps from hyperactive America to Europe and reaches the emerging countries with unsurpassed might. Many in these countries are looking at this process and worrying: “Won’t the wave of business concentration turn into an uncontrollable anti-competitive force?”

**直译**　世界正在经历有史以来最大的一轮兼并与收购浪潮。这一进程从异常活跃的美国席卷到欧洲，又以无与伦比的力量扩展到新兴国家。这些国家中的许多人看到这一进程，担忧地问：“企业集中化的浪潮难道不会变成一种无法控制、损害竞争的力量吗？”

**第 2 段**

There’s no question that the big are getting bigger and more powerful. Multinational corporations accounted for less than 20% of international trade in 1982. Today the figure is more than 25% and growing rapidly. International affiliates account for a fast-growing segment of production in economies that open up and welcome foreign investment. In Argentina, for instance, after the reforms of the early 1990s, multinationals went from 43% to almost 70% of the industrial production of the 200 largest firms. This phenomenon has created serious concerns over the role of smaller economic firms, of national businessmen and over the ultimate stability of the world economy.

**直译**　毫无疑问，大企业正在变得更大、更强。1982 年，跨国公司所占国际贸易份额还不到20%。如今，这一数字已超过25%，并在迅速增长。在那些开放并欢迎外国投资的经济体中，跨国公司的附属企业占据着一个快速增长的生产部分。例如，在阿根廷，20 世纪90 年代初改革之后，跨国公司在最大200 家企业工业产出中所占的份额，从43% 上升到接近70%。这一现象引发了对较小企业、本国商人所能发挥的作用，以及世界经济最终稳定性的严重担忧。

**第 3 段**

I believe that the most important forces behind the massive M&A wave are the same that underlie the globalization process: falling transportation and communication costs, lower trade and investment barriers and enlarged markets that require enlarged operations capable of meeting customers’ demands. All these are beneficial, not detrimental, to consumers. As productivity grows, the world’s wealth increases.

**直译**　我认为，推动大规模并购浪潮的最重要力量，与推动全球化的力量相同：运输和通信成本下降、贸易与投资壁垒降低，以及扩大的市场要求扩大经营规模，以满足顾客需求。所有这些对消费者都有利，而非有害。随着生产率增长，世界财富也在增加。

**第 4 段**

Examples of benefits or costs of the current concentration wave are scanty. Yet it is hard to imagine that the merger of a few oil firms today could re-create the same threats to competition that were feared nearly a century ago in the U.S., when the Standard Oil trust was broken up. The mergers of telecom companies, such as WorldCom, hardly seem to bring higher prices for consumers or a reduction in the pace of technical progress. On the contrary, the price of communications is coming down fast. In cars, too, concentration is increasing-witness Daimler and Chrysler, Renault and Nissan-but it does not appear that consumers are being hurt.

**直译**　关于当前集中化浪潮究竟带来收益还是成本的实例还很少。但很难想象，今天几家石油公司的合并，会重新造成近一个世纪前美国人所担忧的那种竞争威胁；当时，标准石油托拉斯被拆分了。世通等电信公司的合并，似乎几乎没有导致消费者支付更高价格，也没有使技术进步放缓。相反，通信价格正在迅速下降。汽车业的集中化也在加剧—— 看看戴姆勒与克莱斯勒、雷诺与日产—— 但看来消费者并未因此受损。

**第 5 段**

Yet the fact remains that the merger movement must be watched. A few weeks ago, Alan Greenspan warned against the megamergers in the banking industry. Who is going to supervise, regulate and operate as lender of last resort with the gigantic banks that are being created? Won’t multinationals shift production from one place to another when a nation gets too strict about infringements to fair competition? And should one country take upon itself the role of “defending competition” on issues that affect many other nations, as in the U.S. vs. Microsoft case?

**直译**　然而，兼并运动必须受到关注，这一事实仍然成立。几周前，艾伦·格林斯潘对银行业的巨型兼并提出了警告。面对正在形成的巨型银行，谁来监督、监管并担当最后贷款人？当某个国家对违反公平竞争的行为管得过严时，跨国公司难道不会把生产从一个地方转移到另一个地方吗？而在影响许多其他国家的问题上，一个国家是否应该独自承担“捍卫竞争” 的角色，正如美国诉微软案所涉及的那样？篇章简析主旨：作者总体认可全球化驱动的并购对效率与消费者的好处，同时强调跨国监管难题。第3 段正面判断与末段Yet 构成重要转折，不能把作者理解为无条件支持。第4 段例子用于反驳并购必然损害消费者。整理说明：依据所附年度试卷提取Text 1–Text 4；每段英文后接中文直译。已修复明显断词、粘连、错字和标点损坏。文中数据、事件及观点均置于原文章的历史语境中理解。篇章简析为本次整理所加。
