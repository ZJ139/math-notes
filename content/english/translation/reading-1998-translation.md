## Text 1

**第 1 段**

Few creations of big technology capture the imagination like giant dams. Perhaps it is humankind’s long suffering at the mercy of flood and drought that makes the idea of forcing the waters to do our bidding so fascinating. But to be fascinated is also, sometimes, to be blind. Several giant dam projects threaten to do more harm than good.

**直译**　很少有大型技术的造物能像巨型水坝那样激发人的想象力。也许正是人类长久以来任凭洪水和干旱摆布的苦难，使迫使水流听从我们支配这一想法如此令人着迷。但着迷有时也意味着盲目。几项巨型水坝工程可能弊大于利。

**第 2 段**

The lesson from dams is that big is not always beautiful. It doesn’t help that building a big, powerful dam has become a symbol of achievement for nations and people striving to assert themselves. Egypt’s leadership in the Arab world was cemented by the Aswan High Dam. Turkey’s bid for First World status includes the giant Ataturk Dam.

**直译**　水坝给人的教训是，大并不总是美。对那些力图确立自身地位的国家和人民而言，修建一座宏伟有力的大坝已经成为成就的象征，这种情况并无助益。阿斯旺大坝巩固了埃及在阿拉伯世界的领导地位。土耳其为争取进入第一世界所作的努力，也包括修建巨大的阿塔图尔克大坝。

**第 3 段**

But big dams tend not to work as intended. The Aswan Dam, for example, stopped the Nile flooding but deprived Egypt of the fertile silt that floods left – all in return for a giant reservoir of disease which is now so full of silt that it barely generates electricity.

**直译**　但大型水坝往往不能按预期发挥作用。例如，阿斯旺大坝阻止了尼罗河泛滥，却使埃及失去了洪水留下的肥沃淤泥；这一切换来的，却是一座滋生疾病的巨大水库，如今库中淤泥多得几乎无法发电。

**第 4 段**

And yet, the myth of controlling the waters persists. This week, in the heart of civilized Europe, Slovaks and Hungarians stopped just short of sending in the troops in their contention over a dam on the Danube. The huge complex will probably have all the usual problems of big dams. But Slovakia is bidding for independence from the Czechs, and now needs a dam to prove itself.

**直译**　然而，控制水流的神话依然存在。本周，在文明欧洲的腹地，斯洛伐克人与匈牙利人为多瑙河上的一座大坝发生争执，差一点就派出军队。这个庞大的综合工程很可能会出现大型水坝通常具有的所有问题。但斯洛伐克正在争取脱离捷克而独立，现在需要一座大坝来证明自己。

**第 5 段**

Meanwhile, in India, the World Bank has given the go-ahead to the even more wrong-headed Narmada Dam. And the bank has done this even though its advisors say the dam will cause hardship for the powerless and environmental destruction. The benefits are for the powerful, but they are far from guaranteed.

**直译**　与此同时，在印度，世界银行已批准兴建决策更为失当的纳尔马达大坝。该银行这样做，尽管它的顾问表示，这座大坝会使无权无势者陷入困境，并破坏环境。利益是给有权有势者的，但这些利益也远不能得到保证。

**第 6 段**

Proper, scientific study of the impacts of dams and of the cost and benefits of controlling water can help to resolve these conflicts. Hydroelectric power and flood control and irrigation are possible without building monster dams. But when you are dealing with myths, it is hard to be either proper, or scientific. It is time that the world learned the lessons of Aswan. You don’t need a dam to be saved.

**直译**　对水坝的影响以及控制水流的成本和收益进行恰当、科学的研究，有助于解决这些冲突。不修建巨型水坝，也可以实现水力发电、防洪和灌溉。但在应对神话时，要做到恰当或科学都很困难。世界是时候吸取阿斯旺的教训了。要获得拯救，并不需要一座大坝。篇章简析主旨：批评把巨型水坝等同于国家成就的迷思，主张按实际成本与收益评估工程。结构：提出风险—列举政治象征与工程弊端— 提出科学评估。第1 段it is ... that ... 是强调结构；第3 段all in return for带有强烈反讽。末句的saved 指免于水患等困境。

## Text 2

**第 1 段**

Well, no gain without pain, they say. But what about pain without gain? Everywhere you go in America, you hear tales of corporate revival. What is harder to establish is whether the productivity revolution that businessmen assume they are presiding over is for real.

**直译**　人们说，不劳无获。但付出了劳苦却没有收获，又该怎么说呢？在美国，无论走到哪里，你都能听到企业复苏的故事。更难确定的是，商界人士自认为正在主导的生产率革命是否确有其事。

**第 2 段**

The official statistics are mildly discouraging. They show that, if you lump manufacturing and services together, productivity has grown on average by 1.2% since 1987. That is somewhat faster than the average during the previous decade. And since 1991, productivity has increased by about 2% a year, which is more than twice the 1978-87 average. The trouble is that part of the recent acceleration is due to the usual rebound that occurs at this point in a business cycle, and so is not conclusive evidence of a revival in the underlying trend. There is, as Robert Rubin, the treasury secretary, says, a “disjunction” between the mass of business anecdote that points to a leap in productivity and the picture reflected by the statistics.

**直译**　官方统计数字有些令人泄气。它们表明，如果把制造业和服务业合在一起计算，生产率自1987年以来平均增长了1.2%。这比此前十年的平均增速略快。而自1991 年以来，生产率每年增长约2%，超过1978—1987 年平均增速的两倍。问题在于，最近的加速部分源自经济周期在这一阶段通常出现的反弹，因此并不能确凿证明基本趋势已经回升。正如财政部长罗伯特·鲁宾所说，大量表明生产率跃升的企业个案，与统计数字反映的情形之间存在一种“脱节”。

**第 3 段**

Some of this can be easily explained. New ways of organizing the workplace – all that re-engineering and downsizing – are only one contribution to the overall productivity of an economy, which is driven by many other factors such as joint investment in equipment and machinery, new technology, and investment in education and training. Moreover, most of the changes that companies make are intended to keep them profitable, and this need not always mean increasing productivity: switching to new markets or improving quality can matter just as much.

**直译**　其中一些现象很容易解释。组织工作场所的新方式—— 所有那些流程再造和缩减规模措施—— 只是提高一个经济体总体生产率的一项因素；还有许多其他因素推动生产率，如在设备和机器上的共同投资、新技术，以及教育和培训投资。此外，公司作出的大多数改变旨在维持盈利，而这并不总意味着提高生产率：转向新市场或改善质量可能同样重要。

**第 4 段**

Two other explanations are more speculative. First, some of the business restructuring of recent years may have been ineptly done. Second, even if it was well done, it may have spread much less widely than people suppose.

**直译**　另外两种解释更具推测性。第一，近些年一些企业重组可能实施得很不高明。第二，即使实施得很好，其推广范围也可能比人们设想的小得多。

**第 5 段**

Leonard Schlesinger, a Harvard academic and former chief executive of Au Bon Pain, a rapidly growing chain of bakery cafes, says that much “re-engineering” has been crude. In many cases, he believes, the loss of revenue has been greater than the reductions in cost. His colleague, Michael Beer, says that far too many companies have applied re-engineering in a mechanistic fashion, chopping out costs without giving sufficient thought to long-term profitability. BBDO’s Al Rosenshine is blunter. He dismisses a lot of the work of re-engineering consultants as mere rubbish – “the worst sort of ambulance chasing.”

**直译**　哈佛大学学者伦纳德·施莱辛格曾担任快速扩张的面包咖啡连锁店Au Bon Pain 的首席执行官，他说，许多“流程再造” 做得很粗糙。他认为，在许多情况下，收入的损失大于成本的削减。他的同事迈克尔·比尔说，太多公司机械地运用流程再造，只顾砍掉成本，却没有充分考虑长期盈利能力。BBDO 公司的阿尔·罗森沙因说得更直截了当。他把流程再造顾问所做的大量工作斥为纯粹的垃圾——“最恶劣的追逐事故牟利行为”。篇章简析主旨：企业复苏的宣传与生产率数据之间存在落差，裁员降本并不保证长期盈利。结构：提出疑问— 用统计限定乐观判断— 解释脱节— 引述专家批评。首段whether 从句作establish 的宾语；末段without giving ... 指没有充分考虑长期后果。ambulance chasing 在此比喻利用企业困境招揽业务牟利。

## Text 3

**第 1 段**

Science has long had an uneasy relationship with other aspects of culture. Think of Galileo’s 17th-century trial for his rebelling belief before the Catholic Church or poet William Blake’s harsh remarks against the mechanistic worldview of Isaac Newton. The schism between science and the humanities has, if anything, deepened in this century.

**直译**　长期以来，科学与文化的其他方面之间一直存在紧张关系。想想17 世纪伽利略因其叛逆的信念在天主教会面前受审，或诗人威廉·布莱克对艾萨克·牛顿的机械论世界观所作的尖锐批评吧。如果说有什么变化，那么科学与人文学科之间的裂痕在本世纪反而加深了。

**第 2 段**

Until recently, the scientific community was so powerful that it could afford to ignore its critics – but no longer. As funding for science has declined, scientists have attacked “anti-science” in several books, notably Higher Superstition, by Paul R. Gross, a biologist at the University of Virginia, and Norman Levitt, a mathematician at Rutgers University; and The Demon-Haunted World, by Carl Sagan of Cornell University.

**直译**　直到不久前，科学界还十分强大，强大到可以不理会批评者—— 但现在已不再如此。随着科学经费减少，科学家在几本书中攻击了“反科学”，其中著名的有弗吉尼亚大学生物学家保罗·R. 格罗斯和罗格斯大学数学家诺曼·莱维特合著的《高级迷信》，以及康奈尔大学卡尔·萨根所著的《魔鬼出没的世界》。

**第 3 段**

Defenders of science have also voiced their concerns at meetings such as “The Flight from Science and Reason,” held in New York City in 1995, and “Science in the Age of (Mis) information,” which assembled last June near Buffalo.

**直译**　科学的捍卫者也在一些会议上表达了担忧，例如1995 年在纽约市举行的“逃离科学与理性”，以及去年6 月在布法罗附近举行的“（错误）信息时代的科学”。

**第 4 段**

Anti-science clearly means different things to different people. Gross and Levitt find fault primarily with sociologists, philosophers and other academics who have questioned science’s objectivity. Sagan is more concerned with those who believe in ghosts, creationism and other phenomena that contradict the scientific worldview.

**直译**　“反科学” 对不同的人来说，显然有不同的含义。格罗斯与莱维特主要批评那些质疑科学客观性的社会学家、哲学家和其他学者。萨根则更关注那些相信鬼魂、神创论以及其他与科学世界观相矛盾的现象的人。

**第 5 段**

A survey of news stories in 1996 reveals that the anti-science tag has been attached to many other groups as well, from authorities who advocated the elimination of the last remaining stocks of smallpox virus to Republicans who advocated decreased funding for basic research.

**直译**　对1996 年新闻报道的一项调查表明，“反科学” 的标签也被贴到了许多其他群体身上，从主张消灭最后剩余的天花病毒库存的权威人士，到主张削减基础研究经费的共和党人，都在其列。

**第 6 段**

Few would dispute that the term applies to the Unabomber, whose manifesto, published in 1995, scorns science and longs for return to a pre-technological utopia. But surely that does not mean environmentalists concerned about uncontrolled industrial growth are anti-science, as an essay in US News & World Report last May seemed to suggest.

**直译**　很少有人会否认，这个词适用于“大学航空炸弹客”：他在1995 年发表的宣言鄙视科学，渴望回到技术出现以前的乌托邦。但这当然不意味着，关注工业无节制增长的环保人士就是反科学的，尽管去年5 月《美国新闻与世界报道》的一篇文章似乎作出了这样的暗示。

**第 7 段**

The environmentalists, inevitably, respond to such critics. The true enemies of science, argues Paul Ehrlich of Stanford University, a pioneer of environmental studies, are those who question the evidence supporting global warming, the depletion of the ozone layer and other consequences of industrial growth.

**直译**　环保人士不可避免地会回应这类批评者。环境研究先驱、斯坦福大学的保罗·埃利希认为，科学的真正敌人，是那些质疑支持全球变暖、臭氧层损耗以及工业增长的其他后果之证据的人。

**第 8 段**

Indeed, some observers fear that the anti-science epithet is in danger of becoming meaningless. “The term ‘anti-science’ can lump together too many, quite different things,” notes Harvard University philosopher Gerald Holton in his 1993 work Science and Anti-Science. “They have in common only one thing that they tend to annoy or threaten those who regard themselves as more enlightened.”

**直译**　事实上，一些观察者担心，“反科学” 这一称号有变得毫无意义的危险。哈佛大学哲学家杰拉尔德·霍尔顿在1993 年的著作《科学与反科学》中指出：“‘反科学’ 这个词可能把太多相当不同的事物混在一起。它们只有一个共同点：往往会惹恼或威胁那些自认为更加开明的人。”篇章简析主旨：反科学标签指向过于宽泛，容易掩盖不同争议的性质。结构：回顾科学与人文的冲突— 列举标签的多种用法— 展示环保争议双方— 指出概念失效的风险。第6 段Few would dispute that ... 表示几乎无人否认；最后一段only one thing 后的that 从句解释共同点。

## Text 4

**第 1 段**

Emerging from the 1980 census is the picture of a nation developing more and more regional competition, as population growth in the Northeast and Midwest reaches a near standstill.

**直译**　1980 年人口普查呈现出这样一幅国家图景：随着东北部和中西部的人口增长几乎停滞，各地区之间的竞争正日益加剧。

**第 2 段**

This development – and its strong implications for US politics and economy in years ahead – has enthroned the South as America’s most densely populated region for the first time in the history of the nation’s head counting.

**直译**　这种发展变化，以及它对美国今后多年政治和经济的重大影响，使南部在人口普查史上首次登上美国人口最密集地区的位置。

**第 3 段**

Altogether, the US population rose in the 1970s by 23.2 million people – numerically the third-largest growth ever recorded in a single decade. Even so, that gain adds up to only 11.4 percent, lowest in American annual records except for the Depression years.

**直译**　总的说来，美国人口在20 世纪70 年代增加了2320 万；按绝对人数计算，这是有记录以来第三大十年增量。即使如此，这一增幅也只有11.4%，是美国历年记录中除大萧条时期以外的最低水平。

**第 4 段**

Americans have been migrating south and west in larger numbers since World War II, and the pattern still prevails.

**直译**　第二次世界大战以来，越来越多的美国人向南部和西部迁移，而这一模式仍占主导。

**第 5 段**

Three sun-belt states – Florida, Texas and California – together had nearly 10 million more people in 1980 than a decade earlier. Among large cities, San Diego moved from 14th to 8th and San Antonio from 15th to 10th – with Cleveland and Washington. D. C., dropping out of the top 10.

**直译**　1980 年，“阳光地带” 的佛罗里达、得克萨斯和加利福尼亚三州人口合计比十年前多了近1000万。在大城市中，圣迭戈从第14 位升至第8 位，圣安东尼奥从第15 位升至第10 位，而克利夫兰和华盛顿特区跌出了前十名。

**第 6 段**

Not all that shift can be attributed to the movement out of the snow belt, census officials say. Nonstop waves of immigrants played a role, too – and so did bigger crops of babies as yesterday’s “baby boom” generation reached its child bearing years.

**直译**　人口普查官员说，这种变化并不全能归因于人口迁出“冰雪地带”。接连不断的移民浪潮也起了作用；同样，随着昔日“婴儿潮” 一代进入生育年龄，更多婴儿的出生也起了作用。

**直译**　此外，人口统计学家发现，持续向南部和西部迁移的趋势，还伴随着一种相关但较新的现象：美国人似乎越来越不仅寻找工作机会更多的地方，也寻找人口更少的地方。以下是一些例子：

**第 8 段**

•Regionally, the Rocky Mountain states reported the most rapid growth rate – 37.1 percent since 1970 in a vast area with only 5 percent of the US population.

**直译**　从地区来看，落基山各州报告了最快的增长速度：自1970 年以来增长37.1%；这片广阔地区的人口只占美国人口的5%。

**第 9 段**

•Among states, Nevada and Arizona grew fastest of all: 63.5 and 53.1 percent respectively. Except for Florida and Texas, the top 10 in rate of growth is composed of Western states with 7.5 million people – about 9 per square mile.

**直译**　从州来看，内华达州和亚利桑那州的增长速度最快，分别为63.5% 和53.1%。增长率居前十的州，除佛罗里达和得克萨斯外，都是西部各州；这些西部州合计有750 万人口，每平方英里约9 人。

**第 10 段**

The flight from overcrowdedness affects the migration from snow belt to more bearable climates. Nowhere do 1980 census statistics dramatize more the American search for spacious living than in the Far West. There, California added 3.7 million to its population in the 1970s, more than any other state.

**直译**　对过度拥挤的逃离，影响着从冰雪地带向气候更宜人地区的人口迁移。1980 年人口普查数据在任何地方都没有像在美国远西部那样，鲜明地显示美国人对宽敞生活空间的追求。在那里，加利福尼亚州在20 世纪70 年代增加了370 万人口，比其他任何州都多。

**第 11 段**

In that decade, however, large numbers also migrated from California, mostly to other parts of the West. Often they chose – and still are choosing – somewhat colder climates such as Oregon, Idaho and Alaska in order to escape smog, crime and other plagues of urbanization in the Golden State.

**直译**　然而，在那十年中，也有大量人口迁出加利福尼亚州，大多迁往西部其他地区。为了逃离“金州” 城市化带来的烟雾、犯罪及其他祸患，他们往往选择—— 而且现在仍在选择—— 气候略冷的俄勒冈、爱达荷和阿拉斯加等地。

**第 12 段**

As a result, California’s growth rate dropped during the 1970s, to 18.5 percent – little more than two thirds the 1960s’ growth figure and considerably below that of other Western states.

**直译**　结果，加利福尼亚州在20 世纪70 年代的人口增长率降至18.5%，仅略高于60 年代增长率的三分之二，而且明显低于西部其他各州。篇章简析主旨：美国人口继续向南部、西部迁移，同时出现追求低密度居住环境的新动向。结构：人口普查总貌— 区域与城市数据— 迁移原因— 加州迁出案例。首句Emerging ... is ... 为倒装；倒数第3 段Nowhere ... more ... than ... 用否定词前置和比较级强调远西部最突出。文中人口数据均按所附历史文章保留。整理说明：依据所附年度试卷提取Text 1–Text 4；每段英文后接中文直译。已修复明显断词、粘连、错字和标点损坏。文中数据、事件及观点均置于原文章的历史语境中理解。篇章简析为本次整理所加。
