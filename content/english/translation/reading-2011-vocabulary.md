## Text 1

| orchestra | n. 管弦乐团 | the orchestra’s repertoire |

| substitute | n. 替代物 | no substitute for live performance |
| availability | n. 可获得性 | widespread availability |
| vibrant | adj. 有活力的 | more vibrant organization |
| repertoire | n. 全部演出曲目 | expanding the orchestra’s repertoire |
| compete | v. 竞争 | must compete not only with |
